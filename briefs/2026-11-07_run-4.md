# Princeton, Northwestern and Purdue

The link check at the end of the previous unit reported two URLs on these hosts
as HTTP 404 rather than as connection failures. A 404 means the connection
succeeded. One direct check per host returned HTTP 200 from all three, and ten
pages were then fetched and read with plain requests, no header spoofing and no
bypass of anything.

This closes BACKLOG E3 and Q002, which had been handed to the maintainer as work
only a person could do. **That hand-off was wrong**: rounds 1 and 2 observed a
transient failure of one fetching path and recorded it as a permanent property
of three universities' servers. The correction is written into the note, the
insights page and the schools page rather than quietly dropped.

## What is now on the record (research/2026-11-07_three-blocked-hosts-read_v1.md, V4)

- **Princeton**: a graded written paper is required of every applicant, with its
  subject, length, format and "originally written in English" rules, and the two
  routes for submitting it; the two-cycle test policy from the page itself (test
  optional for fall 2027 entry, required again from the 2027-28 cycle); two
  teacher letters from higher-level courses in different academic areas; School
  Report and a separate counselor letter; arts supplement only, submitted
  through the portal *after* the application (Nov 6 / Jan 8, after the Nov 1 /
  Jan 1 application dates); optional alumni interview with an opt-out in the
  Princeton questions, no on-campus interviews, and no recording by either side;
  the three-application limit; dated SCEA and RD deadlines.
- **Northwestern**: the Common App personal essay is **no longer required**; the
  2026-27 supplement prompts verbatim with their 300-word and 200-word limits;
  unsolicited supplements refused in writing, **websites included**; two letters
  plus an optional extra that should add new information; Glimpse (US high
  schools) and InitialView (international), Nov 6 for ED and Jan 16 for RD, with
  InitialView not rating its own interviews; Bienen, ISP, MMSS and Musical
  Theater extras, ISP being a separate application whose outcome does not affect
  the general decision; dated 2026-27 deadlines; about 50% of the class from ED.
- **Purdue**: Nov 1 Early Action is the deadline "that encompasses scholarship
  consideration"; the fall window is Aug 1 to Jan 15; the **CLT is accepted**
  alongside the SAT and ACT with no preference and a five-year validity; the
  factor list in full, which **contains no letters of recommendation**; four
  years of maths and three of lab science for every major; admission is to a
  specific major.

Three claims that earlier leads attributed to these schools are **not on their
pages** and remain unasserted: Princeton's "not looking for perfect" sentence,
Northwestern's "tally of touchpoints" demonstrated-interest wording, and a
Purdue letters policy.

## Then Williams and Michigan too

With the three TLS hosts open, the other two blocked schools were re-tested the
same way. `curl` still returns 403 for both, but **the browser opened both
without any challenge**, so both were read:

- **Williams**: ED Nov 15 with the Agreement Form and a Dec 14 decision; RD Jan
  4, decision April 1, reply May 1; three platforms with no preference, which
  finally confirms **Apply Coalition with Scoir** from Williams's own page (it
  had appeared only on Scoir's list); three recommendations; $65 fee; "truly
  test optional" with superscoring across the legacy and enhanced ACT; and four
  optional channels — an academic writing sample that may be ungraded or
  handwritten but "**Please do not submit lab reports**", an arts portfolio
  through SlideRoom reviewed by faculty, **scientific research abstracts** with
  an optional VIVA recording, and InitialView although Williams holds no
  interviews. Two admission officers read every application together.
- **Michigan**: **it has added an Early Decision plan** — Nov 1 is now the
  deadline for both ED and EA, with ED aid Nov 15, notifications by Dec 24, a
  Jan 6 deposit, and an Agreement that the applicant, a parent or guardian and
  the counselor must all sign together; RD Feb 1; EA notifications by Jan 29,
  RD by April 2; EA/RD aid Mar 1; deposit May 1. A $75 fee; School Report; **one
  teacher evaluation, which may come from a core academic teacher "or other
  non-relative"**; "Do not submit additional documents unless specifically
  requested." The 2026-27 essay limits, a named gap since the first run, are now
  sourced: Common App 250–650 words, Challenges and Circumstances 250, U-M
  question 1 100–300, question 2 100–550. The page also says the admissions
  process is being modified.

So **five of five** hosts this repo had recorded as unreadable were read in one
morning: three because a transient failure cleared, two in a browser while a
plain request still gets 403. The honest generalisation is that "refuses
automated access" is a claim about a client and a minute, and this repo had been
writing it as a property of universities.
