# Run 3 — the pages that were not actually unreadable

This run started as a watchdog check on the 04:41 scheduled run. That run had
finished cleanly at 05:10 (PR #13 and #14 merged, lock removed, brief written),

## What changed

**E5 — both dead aid pages recovered.** Harvey Mudd publishes an itemised
2026-27 cost of attendance at `/admission/afford/cost-of-attendance/`; the
earlier run's URL was a guess and the guess was wrong. On campus the total is
$104,512 (transportation "varies by distance" and health insurance are both
outside that total). Harvey Mudd's aid cell is therefore no longer computed
against an incomplete denominator: $32,564 ÷ $104,512 = 31.2%, where the site
previously showed 33.5% and flagged the denominator.

Amherst publishes a 2026-27 student expense budget at `/tuition`: tuition
$75,330, housing $10,980, meals $9,340, fees $710, personal expenses $1,800,
travel varying by home location, health insurance $3,627 waivable and excluded
from the budget used for aid. On the same basis as the other rows, $98,160.
Amherst is also the only school read so far with **no books line at all**:
required course materials are inside the comprehensive fee.

**Amherst was misdiagnosed, and that is now corrected across the site.** It was
recorded as refusing automated access behind a "Human Verification" page. Every
page of `amherst.edu` opens in a browser. The earlier HTTP 405 came from guessed
URLs and automated requests. Recovered in one pass: ED 2026-11-09, RD
2027-01-05, reply by 2027-05-01; the $75 fee and Quick Pass waiver; SAT/ACT
optional with official scores required only of admitted students who enrol;
arts supplements reviewed by faculty; **an "Optional Research Questions" section
in Amherst's own writing supplement for research "undertaken independently of
the high school curriculum"**; at least two admission deans on every file; no
admission interviews, with alumni interviewing ended "over a dozen years ago"
though alumni may still write supporting letters; and need-blind for
international applicants in Amherst's own words, which had rested on a search
extract and carried a † on the schools page.

**E4 — Stanford's FAQ was retired, not gated.** `/faqs/` returns a 39-byte empty
document and no FAQ link appears in the site navigation; the answers were moved
into accordions on each Apply page. Read there, the claim O53 is confirmed in
substance and **corrected in mechanism**: Stanford adds no supplementary
materials to a file and offers no upload, and new information goes through the
Update Application Form in the Stanford portal, not the Common App Additional
Information field as the earlier search extract implied. The same answer says
links carry "no guarantee that readers will review" them, and states a grace
period after the deadline for teachers and counselors.

**E4 — UT Austin's answers were read from the DOM.** Letters optional, at most
two, not from a relative, with letters from outside the high school encouraged,
and one of the five things UT asks them to cover is "Your preparation for your
selected first-choice major, both in school and out". No admission interviews,
no demonstrated interest, optional resume with no preferred format or length,
no documents by email. The waitlist form takes "any academic updates in 200
words or less"; other materials "will not be accepted or reviewed" (O99
confirmed verbatim).

**New insights section** `#outside-record-places`: the three schools' boundaries
on work done outside school, side by side, plus the 404-as-instrument finding.

## Non-obvious finding worth keeping

A 404 in this repo's link log means "re-locate", not "gone". Johns Hopkins
(yesterday), Harvey Mudd and Amherst each moved a URL and kept the page; opening
the 404 and following the navigation it offers found all three in one or two
clicks. A link checker cannot tell that case from a page that no longer exists,
so `logs/links*.json` should be worked through by hand, not trusted as a list of
losses. Of the six pages round 2 called "refused automated access", four were
not refusing anything.
