**PR [#17](https://github.com/Timmy97-TW/application-atlas/pull/17) is open,
MERGEABLE, CLEAN, and not merged.** Everything runs 5 and 6 produced sits on
`run/2026-11-07-integrate`, not on the live site. The live site serves the
**05:50** state: the deploy log's newest successful run is the merge of PR #16.

`gh pr merge 17` was refused three times — at 07:06, at 08:06 and again at
08:51 — by the permission layer the scheduled runs execute under, which declines
a merge without review. Rule 10 and the 2026-11-07 decision do allow a scheduled
run to merge its own PR, and runs 1-4 did exactly that for PRs #1-#16, so what
changed is what a run may do, not repo policy. Nothing was routed around it:
pushing to `main` is forbidden by the same rule, and working around a refused
permission is not a fix.

**What a person needs to do: merge PR #17.** One click, or `gh pr merge 17
--merge`. Pages then deploys and all of the following goes live together. Runs 6
and 7 deliberately committed to that same branch instead of opening more PRs, so
one merge ships everything.
