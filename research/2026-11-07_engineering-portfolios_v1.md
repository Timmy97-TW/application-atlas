# Engineering portfolios, research abstracts and project links (cycle 2026–27)

摘要（zh）：本筆記查核一位工程類申請者能否、以及透過哪個管道，把 maker／工程作品集、研究摘要或專案連結（GitHub、個人網站）送進申請檔案。MIT 是唯一一所在官方頁面上把 Maker Portfolio 規格寫到「影片總長 2 分鐘、最多 25 個附件、程式專案必須附 codebase 與可運作的 demo」這種細節的學校，並由 MIT 教職員與校友組成的評審委員會審閱（SlideRoom，每份 10 美元）。Caltech 透過自家的 Beaver Breakroom 接受作品集與研究論文，且明列接受連結／URL。Olin 允許在 Common App 的 Additional Information 放超連結，但明說「不保證」會被審閱。Harvey Mudd 只要研究摘要、不要全文。Stanford、Carnegie Mellon（非藝術學院）、Georgia Tech、UIUC、UC Berkeley 則明文不收作品集或額外材料；Stanford 更寫明讀者「不保證」會點開申請中的連結。Michigan 的官方網域對自動擷取回傳 HTTP 403，相關欄位留空。本次執行的網路搜尋配額在中途用盡，部分欄位因此只能寫 `not stated (checked 2026-11-07, URL)`。

Summary (en): This note checks whether, and through which channel, an engineering applicant can put a maker or engineering portfolio, a research abstract, or a project link (GitHub, personal site) into the application file. MIT is the only school among the twelve whose official page specifies a maker portfolio down to a two-minute total video cap, 25 attachments, and a rule that code projects must include the codebase and a working demo; a board of MIT faculty and alumni reviews it in SlideRoom ($10 per portfolio). Caltech accepts portfolios and research papers through its own Beaver Breakroom portal and lists "Links and URLs" as an accepted format. Olin allows a hyperlink in the Common App Additional Information section but says it cannot promise review. Harvey Mudd wants an abstract, not a full paper. Stanford, Carnegie Mellon (outside its fine-arts schools), Georgia Tech, UIUC and UC Berkeley state that they do not accept portfolios or unsolicited materials; Stanford adds that there is no guarantee readers will review links. Michigan's official domains returned HTTP 403 to automated retrieval, so Michigan cells are empty. The run's web-search budget ran out partway; affected cells say `not stated (checked 2026-11-07, URL)`.

### 1.2 What MIT admissions staff have written about strong maker portfolios

All three staff sources below are archived or dated. They show what MIT said at the time.

- [事實] A 2013 FAQ by an MIT admissions staff blogger (updated October 2018, archived) asks applicants to treat the portfolio as an "elevator pitch" about the project, and says Advisory Board reviewers "have a limited amount of time to review the materials." [S5]
- [事實] The same 2013 FAQ answers "How do I send in my source code? How do I send you my website?" by telling applicants to summarize rather than send code. [S5]
- [事實] The current page now requires the codebase plus a demo for code projects. [S1]
- [推論] MIT's guidance on code has changed since 2013, so advice copied from older posts or forums may be out of date. [S1][S5]
- [事實] In 2021 an admissions officer who oversees the creative portfolios and their reviewers wrote that "most students are best served by completing only the standard MIT application". The same post asks applicants to "please focus on quality over quantity." [S6]
- [事實] That 2021 post says a robotics-team member is not expected to submit a maker portfolio. [S6]
- [事實] The 2015 Faculty Newsletter article says an outstanding portfolio "can provide a compelling reason to admit an otherwise qualified candidate". It also says more than 2,000 students submitted portfolios in the first two years. [S7]
- [事實] The 2015 article reports that 7.4% of male applicants and 2.8% of female applicants submitted a Maker Portfolio. Among applicants interested in engineering, the figures were 8.5% (812 of 9,531) and 3.8% (130 of 3,414). [S7]
- [事實] The 2016 follow-up reports the gender gap narrowed by 0.6 percentage points and "the needle hasn't moved as much as we would like." [S8]
- [事實] An MIT student blogger (not staff) wrote in 2019: "I did not submit any kind of portfolio, but I got in." [S9]
- Gap: no current (2025–26 or 2026–27) post by MIT admissions staff on how faculty evaluate maker portfolios was located. Search budget ran out before mitadmissions.org/blogs could be swept. Status: not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S4, S6).

### 1.4 Where a link can go in the MIT application

- [事實] The activities section is MIT's own form: "Please use our form, not a resume." It has space for four activities. [S3]
- [事實] There is "one final, open-ended, additional-information text box." No length is given. [S3]
- [事實] The essays and activities page does not mention URLs or links (checked 2026-11-07, S3).
- [推論] The only documented place where MIT expects a repository is the Maker Portfolio. A code project must include its codebase, ideally version-controlled, and a reachable demo. [S1]
- [推論] A GitHub link inside SlideRoom is consistent with that rule, but MIT does not name GitHub. [S1]

## 4. Non-obvious findings

Each finding is given in zh and en, with its tag and sources.

1. 【zh】MIT 2013 年的官方 FAQ 曾叫申請者不要寄原始碼；2026–27 的官方頁面反過來要求程式專案「必須」附 codebase 與可運作的 demo。舊文章的建議已過期。
   【en】MIT's 2013 FAQ discouraged sending source code. The current page requires the codebase and a working demo for every code project, so older advice is out of date.
   [事實] S5, S1. [推論] for "out of date".
2. 【zh】MIT Maker Portfolio 的影片上限是「整份作品集合計」2 分鐘，不是每個專案 2 分鐘；有活動零件的專案必須附影片；官方明說影片集錦和露臉「沒有加分」。
   【en】MIT's 2-minute video cap is for the whole portfolio, not per project. Video is mandatory for moving parts, and MIT says there is "no bonus" for a reel or for appearing on camera.
   [事實] S1.
3. 【zh】MIT 的問卷不只是說明，它被用來把作品集分派給最合適的評審；評審被假設只具備該類別的一般專業。
   【en】The MIT questionnaire routes the portfolio to a reviewer, and MIT says to explain anything not obvious to someone with only general expertise in the category.
   [事實] S1.
4. 【zh】MIT 只做「完成部分」的審閱：進行中的作品可以交，但只有已完成的部分會被看；缺乏製作過程證據的專案無法審閱。
   【en】MIT reviews only completed work. Projects without build-process evidence cannot be reviewed.
   [事實] S1.
5. 【zh】MIT 的活動表只有四格，且明說用表格「不要用 resume」。
   【en】MIT's activities form has four slots and says to use the form, not a résumé.
   [事實] S3.
6. 【zh】2015 年 MIT 以招生辦公室口吻在教職員通訊中寫明：每份 Maker Portfolio 至少由一位 Engineering Advisory Board 成員審閱，評語「加入申請者檔案」；提交「既非必要也非充分」。
   【en】In 2015, writing in the Admissions Office's voice, MIT said each maker portfolio is reviewed at least once by an Engineering Advisory Board member and the evaluation is added to the folder. It also said submitting is "neither necessary nor sufficient."
   [事實] S7 (2015 statement). [推論] that it still applies.
7. 【zh】MIT 2015 年資料：男性申請者 7.4% 交 Maker Portfolio，女性 2.8%；2016 年追蹤顯示差距只縮小 0.6 個百分點。
   【en】MIT reported in 2015 that 7.4% of male and 2.8% of female applicants submitted a maker portfolio. The gap had narrowed by only 0.6 points by 2016.
   [事實] S7, S8.
8. 【zh】Caltech 的作品集截止日比申請截止日晚：REA 11/1 申請、11/6 前補交材料；RD 1/4 申請、1/10 前補交。上傳管道 Beaver Breakroom 在送出申請後才會寄連結。
   【en】Caltech gives extra days for supplemental materials (Nov 6 after a Nov 1 REA application; Jan 10 after Jan 4 RD). The Beaver Breakroom link arrives only after the application is submitted.
   [事實] S13, S10.
9. 【zh】Caltech 對「研究」的定義很窄（大學或研究機構實驗室、資深研究者指導、有論文成果）；課堂專題和實習應放進 Portfolio，而 Portfolio 明列接受連結／URL。
   【en】Caltech's "research" category is narrow: a lab at a university or research organization, a senior researcher, and a paper. Class projects and internships go in the Portfolio, which accepts links and URLs.
   [事實] S12, S11.
10. 【zh】Caltech 官方寫明教職員閱讀申請，且「每位錄取生都得到一位教授的正面評價」；但沒有寫教職員是否看作品集。
    【en】Caltech says faculty read applications and every admitted student gets a positive review from a professor. It does not say whether faculty see the portfolio.
    [事實] S15. [推論] for the second sentence.
11. 【zh】VIVA 是第三方（InitialView）的付費影片訪談工具。Caltech 對研究申請者「鼓勵」使用、但不提供費用減免；CMU 只開放給工程學院與電腦科學學院申請者。
    【en】VIVA is a paid third-party (InitialView) video tool. Caltech encourages it for research and offers no fee waiver of its own; CMU offers it only to Engineering and SCS applicants.
    [事實] S12, S23.
12. 【zh】CMU 明文不收 resume、作品集、網站；但它的 Common App 學校題目裡有一個放 Schoolhouse.world 作品集 URL 的欄位，是少數官方指定的連結欄位。
    【en】CMU refuses résumés, portfolios and websites, yet its Common App member questions include a URL field for a Schoolhouse.world portfolio, one of the few officially designated link fields found.
    [事實] S23.
13. 【zh】Stanford 不收任何補充材料，也明說讀者「不保證」會看申請裡的連結；若要交藝術作品集，Common App 必須提早送（REA 10/15、RD 12/5）。工程作品不在藝術作品集範圍內。
    【en】Stanford accepts no supplementary material and says there is "no guarantee" readers will review links. Arts-portfolio applicants must file the Common App early (Oct 15 REA, Dec 5 RD), and engineering work is not among the four arts areas.
    [事實] S21, S22, S20.
14. 【zh】Harvey Mudd 要研究「摘要」而非全文；並提醒更新表單裡的附件常常「無法開啟或閱讀」，只要文字摘要即可。
    【en】Harvey Mudd wants a research abstract, not the paper. It warns that attachments in its update form often cannot be opened by counselors, and a short written summary is enough.
    [事實] S18.
15. 【zh】Olin 允許在 Common App Additional Information 放作品集超連結，但寫明「無法保證」所有選交材料都會被審閱；Olin 的錄取還包含必須出席的 Candidates' Weekend（含設計挑戰與個人面談）。
    【en】Olin allows a portfolio hyperlink in Additional Information but cannot promise review. Olin also requires attendance at a Candidates' Weekend with a design challenge and an individual interview.
    [事實] S16, S17.
16. 【zh】UIUC 會在審閱前把未被要求的材料（包括推薦信）從檔案中移除；送出後活動與作文欄位不能修改。
    【en】UIUC removes unsolicited materials, including recommendation letters, before review. Activities and writing answers cannot be changed after submission.
    [事實] S35.
17. 【zh】UC Berkeley（含 EECS）不收作品集，寄到辦公室的東西「不會被審閱」；官方建議把延伸的課外活動寫進 PIQ 區的 Additional Comments 欄。
    【en】Berkeley (EECS included) accepts no portfolios and says anything sent will not be reviewed. Its own tips say to use the Personal Insight Additional Comments box to expand on extracurriculars.
    [事實] S38, S39.
18. 【zh】Georgia Tech 2026–27 取消學校補充作文，不收另外的 resume，推薦信最多三封且只限輔導老師與教師。
    【en】Georgia Tech dropped its supplemental essay for 2026–27, does not accept separate résumés, and caps optional recommendations at three, from a counselor and teachers only.
    [事實] S27, S24, S25.
19. 【zh】Cornell 其他學院的作品集／設計補充截止日等於申請截止日（ED 11/1），比其他材料（11/13）早；Duffield Engineering 本身沒有作品集，只有六題補充作文。
    【en】At Cornell, portfolios and design supplements for colleges that require them are due with the application (Nov 1 ED), earlier than other materials (Nov 13). Duffield Engineering has no portfolio, only six supplemental essays.
    [事實] S32, S30.

## Gaps and reasons (for the run brief)

- Michigan: every page on admissions.umich.edu and engin.umich.edu returned HTTP 403 or a bot challenge. Cells are empty. [事實] S44
- Purdue: only the deadline page could be read. Purdue's criteria and guide pages are rendered by JavaScript; the URLs tried returned 404 or redirected to the home page. Portfolio and link policy: not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S34).
- Common App and Coalition field limits (Additional Information length, activity character counts, URL handling) were not confirmed from an official Common App or Scoir page this run. The Common App help center is JavaScript-rendered, help.scoir.com returned 403, and the 2026-27 Requirements Grid PDF text could not be extracted without a PDF tool.
- Current MIT staff blog posts on maker-portfolio evaluation (2024–26) and Georgia Tech blog post bodies were not swept, because the shared web-search budget ran out mid-run.
- MIT Maker questionnaire and Research Supplement word limits exist only inside SlideRoom, which was not opened.
