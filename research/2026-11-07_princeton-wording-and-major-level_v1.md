# 1. Princeton's sentence exists, and it is a blog post `[事實]`

Run 5 recorded that a sentence beginning roughly "We are not looking for
perfect" was asserted nowhere on the four Princeton pages it had read, and left
it as a gap. It exists. `V6-P1`, a post titled "Authentically You: Application
Essays and AI", dated **1 September 2026** and bylined **"By Dean Karen
Richardson '93"** — the Dean of Admission, per the author note on the same page:

> "Note: do not do that! We are not looking for perfect. We're looking for
> authentic."

and, in its closing paragraph:

> "Remember that we are not looking for perfection but rather for you to be
> perfectly YOU."

Also on the same post, on what Princeton says it is looking for:

> "As we build a campus community, we are looking for students who will add to
> the academic and the social fabric of campus and who will contribute in the
> classroom, the common room, on the soccer pitch, the stage … you get the
> picture."

Under rule 1 this is **official**: an admissions office in its own voice,
including its blogs, and at the most senior level in it. Both quotes were read
from the live page directly before being written here, not taken on trust.

## Two corrections this forces

- **The sentence is on no standing policy page.** A search of every URL in
  `admission.princeton.edu/sitemap.xml` — 1,248 of them — found the phrase only
  in this post. So the citation must say "Princeton admission blog, Dean of
  Admission, 1 September 2026", not "Princeton's what-we-look-for page". A blog
  post is official and it is also dated and personal; a reader should be able to
  see that this is the dean writing in September 2026, not a published standard.
- **`admission.princeton.edu/how-apply/what-we-look-for` does not exist.** It
  returns HTTP 404, confirmed directly, and no `/about-us` path is in the
  sitemap either. QUESTIONS.md Q002 asked Timmy to open that URL. The real
  equivalent page is `V6-P2`, `/apply/before-you-apply/helpful-tips`, which
  returns 200 and opens: "We look for students with intellectual curiosity, who
  have pursued and achieved academic excellence. We also look for students with
  strong personal and extracurricular accomplishments." It also says "Above all,
  please write in a style that reflects your own voice."

# 2. Michigan: you do not apply to the university `[事實]`

`V6-M1`, verbatim: "At Michigan, you don't apply to the university – you apply
to one of our 15 undergraduate schools and colleges". Ten units take first-year
applications, and **Engineering is one of them**, so engineering is a
direct-admit college at the point of application. "The majority of Michigan
first-years are admitted to our liberal arts college, LSA."

- **No major-level admission.** `V6-M1`: "It's OK if you don't yet know what your
  major will be," and "Many students do not indicate their major until the end of
  their sophomore year". A biology applicant applies to LSA — `V6-M7` tells
  applicants to "apply specifically to the College of Literature, Science, and
  the Arts" — and biology is one of its 85+ majors.
- **No engineering-specific essay, but a college-specific one for everybody.**
  `V6-M2` requires of all applicants: "Describe the unique qualities that attract
  you to the specific undergraduate college or school … How would that curriculum
  support your interests?", 100–550 words. Engineering is **absent** from
  `V6-M3`'s list of units with additional application requirements (Ross,
  Taubman for Architecture, Stamps, SMTD, the Education programmes, ROTC,
  home-schooled and online students). The one engineering exception is in
  `V6-M5`: the Integrated Business and Engineering dual degree "must also
  complete the Ross Admissions Portfolio".
- **Engineering reviews to an engineering standard.** `V6-M5`: criteria "are
  based on the strength of your academic preparation to pursue an engineering
  degree", with "special emphasis on precalculus, chemistry and physics", and
  "No special class rank, grade point average, test score, or other
  qualification by itself will assure admission."
- **Changing in later is possible and is not a formality.** `V6-M6`: "A
  Cross-Campus applicant who completes the required courses and has a GPA of 2.5
  or above is not guaranteed admission into the College of Engineering. Incoming
  students must remain in their home unit for a year before applying to transfer
  into the College of Engineering." Prerequisites are named (Calculus 115/116,
  Physics 140/141, chemistry, computing, first-year writing) and "We require a
  grade of C or better in all prerequisite courses", under a 55-credit ceiling.

This matters for sequencing: at Michigan the engineering decision is made at
application time, and the fallback route costs a year and is not guaranteed.

# 3. Williams: no major at application, and no Williams engineering degree `[事實]`

- **A major is declared later.** `V6-W2`: "Students are required to declare a
  major spring of sophomore year." Williams awards one degree: "Williams College
  offers a course of study leading to the Bachelor of Arts degree."
- **The application names no major or school.** `V6-W5`'s checklist is
  exhaustive on its face — application system, transcript and mid-year report,
  counselor and two teacher recommendations, $65 or a waiver, and the ED
  Agreement for ED applicants — and contains no major, school, division or
  programme field. Recorded as an **absence on the pages read**, `[推論]` for the
  general claim, not as an affirmative statement by Williams that applicants do
  not apply to a major. The one place the catalog ties a major to entry is
  transfers: "Those who enroll as first-semester juniors will select a major upon
  matriculation."
- **Engineering: not a flat no.** The catalog's majors list contains no
  engineering major, but `V6-W3` describes a "Combined Program in Liberal Arts
  and Engineering": a 3-2 programme with Columbia's engineering school and a
  2-1-1-1 with Dartmouth's Thayer School, each five years, after which "a student
  receives a Bachelor of Arts degree from Williams as well as either a Bachelor
  of Science (Columbia) or Bachelor of Engineering (Dartmouth)". The precise
  formulation: **Williams confers no engineering degree of its own; the
  engineering degree is conferred by Columbia or Dartmouth.** Whether either
  programme can be elected at the point of application is **not stated**.
- Biology is an ordinary Williams major under Division III, declared on the
  college-wide timetable. No biology-specific application step is stated.

# Still not stated, with where it was looked for

- Whether Michigan's Common App requires an **intended major** as distinct from a
  school or college (`V6-M1` to `V6-M5` silent).
- LSA's own rule and deadline for declaring a major, and any biology-specific
  requirement: several LSA paths that would hold it return LSA's own "Page not
  found", so only the central statement on `V6-M1` is available.
- The content of the admissions changes Michigan announces on `V6-M3` ("We are
  modifying our admissions process and requirements"). The page it points to was
  not locatable; `/apply/application-changes` returns 404. **Michigan's rows
  should be treated as liable to move**, and the site already says so.
- Whether the Williams dual-degree engineering programmes are indicated at
  application.

## 中文摘要

- **Princeton 那句話找到了，而且它是一篇部落格文章。** V6-P1，標題「Authentically You: Application Essays and AI」，日期 **2026-09-01**，署名 **招生院長 Dean Karen Richardson '93**：「Note: do not do that! We are not looking for perfect. We're looking for authentic.」結尾還有一句「Remember that we are not looking for perfection but rather for you to be perfectly YOU.」依規則 1，招生辦自己的部落格算 official，這還是其中層級最高的人寫的。兩句都在寫上站之前直接對線上頁面核對過。
- 這逼出兩個更正：(1) **這句話不在任何常設政策頁上**——把 `sitemap.xml` 裡 1,248 個網址全查過，只有這一篇有。所以引用必須寫成「Princeton 招生部落格，招生院長，2026-09-01」，不能寫成「Princeton 的 what-we-look-for 頁」；讀者要能看出這是院長在 2026 年 9 月寫的話，不是公布的標準。(2) **`/how-apply/what-we-look-for` 這個網址不存在**，直接查是 HTTP 404，sitemap 裡也沒有 `/about-us`。QUESTIONS.md 的 Q002 當初請 Timmy 去開的就是這個網址。真正對應的頁是 V6-P2 `/apply/before-you-apply/helpful-tips`（200），開頭是「We look for students with intellectual curiosity, who have pursued and achieved academic excellence…」，還有「Above all, please write in a style that reflects your own voice.」
- **Michigan：你不是申請這所大學。** V6-M1 原文「At Michigan, you don't apply to the university – you apply to one of our 15 undergraduate schools and colleges」。十個單位收新生申請，**工程學院是其中之一**，所以工程是在申請時就直接錄取的學院。生物走 LSA（V6-M7 叫申請者「apply specifically to the College of Literature, Science, and the Arts」），而且**沒有科系層級錄取**：「Many students do not indicate their major until the end of their sophomore year」。
- 工程沒有專屬文書，但**所有人**都要寫一篇學院專屬文書（V6-M2，100–550 words）；V6-M3 列出有額外要求的學院清單裡**沒有工程**，唯一例外是 V6-M5 的 Integrated Business and Engineering 要另交 Ross Admissions Portfolio。工程自己的審查標準是「based on the strength of your academic preparation to pursue an engineering degree」，特別看 precalculus、化學、物理。
- **之後再轉進工程要付代價**：V6-M6 寫明 GPA 2.5 以上「is not guaranteed admission」，而且「must remain in their home unit for a year before applying to transfer」，先修課要 C 以上，還有 55 學分上限。對規劃的意義很直接：Michigan 的工程決定在申請當下就做完了，補救路線要花一年而且不保證。
- **Williams：申請時不選主修，而且 Williams 自己不發工程學位。** 主修在**大二春季**才申報（V6-W2），學校只發 B.A.。申請清單（V6-W5）從頭到尾沒有主修、學院或學程欄位——這記成「讀到的頁面上沒有」，一般性結論標 `[推論]`，不當成 Williams 明文宣告。工程不是單純的「沒有」：V6-W3 有「Combined Program in Liberal Arts and Engineering」，與 Columbia 的 3-2 和 Dartmouth Thayer 的 2-1-1-1，五年拿 Williams 的 B.A. 加 Columbia 的 B.S. 或 Dartmouth 的 B.E.。精確說法是：**Williams 不發自己的工程學位，工程學位由 Columbia 或 Dartmouth 授予**；能不能在申請時就選這兩個學程，未載明。
- 仍未載明：Michigan 的 Common App 是否要填「intended major」（有別於學院）；LSA 自己的主修申報規則與期限（好幾個會寫這件事的 LSA 網址回它自己的「Page not found」）；Michigan 在 V6-M3 公告的招生流程變更內容（它指向的頁找不到，`/apply/application-changes` 是 404，所以 **Michigan 的各列都該視為可能會動**，站上已經這樣寫）；Williams 雙學位工程學程能否在申請時指定。
- 抓取註記：`admissions.umich.edu`、`engin.umich.edu`、`lsa.umich.edu`、`williams.edu`、`catalog.williams.edu` 對腳本請求回 **403**，瀏覽器正常打開，上面每一句 Michigan 與 Williams 的引文都來自瀏覽器。`admission.princeton.edu` 對其中一條抓取路徑回「certificate rejected」，但一般請求讀得到——這是本repo第三次記到同一種「client 的問題被當成網站的問題」。沒有繞過任何保護。
