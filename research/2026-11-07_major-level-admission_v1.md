# Major-level admission — engineering (incl. EE/ECE/CS) and biology/biomedical, 30 schools

Question for each school: is the student admitted to engineering or biology when the application is decided, or later? Is the major capped? Is a second-choice major read? Can a student move into engineering or CS after entry, and on what published terms? Is CS separate from engineering? Which high-school courses are stated? Does the school publish different admit rates by college or major?

Method and limits (read first):

- [事實] Every source below was read on 2026-11-07. All are the institution's own pages (class `official`). No secondary source is used for any fact. Search-result summaries were used only to find official URLs; each URL was then opened and read, except where a row says "not read".
- [事實] Michigan pages (engin.umich.edu, cse.engin.umich.edu, lsa.umich.edu, advising.engin.umich.edu) returned HTTP 403 on 2026-11-07 to every method tried in this run. Michigan facts here come only from Michigan Engineering pages read earlier the same day by the A5 roster run (roster part 1, S84, S86, S87). Michigan internal-transfer terms are **not verified**.
- [事實] Williams (admission.williams.edu) returned HTTP 403. Purdue's catalog is script-rendered; it was read in the built-in browser pane. The Georgia Tech CS change-of-major timeline is published as an image and was not read.
- [推論] "Not stated" means "not stated on the pages listed in the cell". It does not mean no policy exists.
- [推論] "Capped" in this note means the school's own page says entry depends on space, capacity, or a limited number of seats. A competitive review alone is not called a cap.
- [事實] No school in this set publishes first-year admit rates by college or major on the pages read, with one exception noted for internal moves (UC San Diego's continuing-student selection data, M70). UIUC states that "Admit rates vary depending on your college or program of study" without giving numbers (M45).

## A. Schools that admit to the institution, with no major chosen at entry

These schools say so on their own pages. Engineering and biology are declared after enrolment.

### 1. MIT
- **Entry.** [事實] "When you're admitted to MIT as a first-year student, you're admitted to the entire Institute. We don't ask you to decide on a major until you've had a chance to explore"; most students declare in spring of the first year, some at the end of sophomore year [M01].
- **Cap / internal transfer / second choice.** Not stated (checked 2026-11-07, M01). [推論] With no major at entry, there is no internal-transfer step into engineering.
- **CS.** [事實] MIT's engineering admissions page says ~70% of undergraduates declare an engineering major [M01]. Whether CS sits inside engineering is not stated on M01.
- **Courses.** [事實] No required science courses; calculus foundations and physics/chemistry/biology recommended (roster S5) [M02].

### 2. Caltech
- **Entry.** [事實] "students don't declare a major until the end of their first year"; applicants name one or two areas of interest in the supplement [M03].
- **Cap / transfer / second choice.** Not stated (checked 2026-11-07, M03).
- **Courses.** [事實] Required: 4 years math including 1 year calculus, 1 year physics, 1 year chemistry; biology recommended, not required [M04].

### 3. Stanford
- **Entry.** [事實] "you apply to the university as a whole, not to a particular major, department or school"; prospective majors are not binding [M05].
- **Cap / transfer.** Not stated (checked 2026-11-07, M05).
- **Courses.** [事實] Recommendations only; no engineering-specific coursework listed [M06].

### 4. Harvard
- **Entry.** [事實] SEAS: "All prospective undergraduate students, including those intending to study engineering and applied sciences, must apply directly to Harvard College. Students do not need to declare their intended concentration until their sophomore year." [M07] [事實] Supplement: "we do not admit applicants to particular programs" [M08].
- **Cap / transfer.** Not stated (checked 2026-11-07, M07, M08).
- **Courses.** Not stated (checked 2026-11-07, M07).

### 5. Yale
- **Entry.** [事實] Applicants indicate up to three academic areas "as of this moment" [M09]. No admission by major is described [M09].
- **Courses.** [事實] Yale "does not prescribe a set of secondary school courses that are required for admission" [M10].

### 6. Brown
- **Entry.** [事實] Applicants list top concentration choices; "There are no quotas among concentrations and we do not admit students by academic interest." [M18]
- **Courses.** [事實] Sc.B. candidates: at least one recommendation should come from a math or science teacher (roster S49) [M19]. Course prerequisites not stated.

### 7. Harvey Mudd
- **Entry.** [事實] Every student takes a common technical Core; the biology, engineering and CS majors build on it [M81]. [推論] Admission is to the college; no admission by major is described on M80 or M81.
- **Courses.** [事實] "Requirements (for enrollment)": one year each of high-school calculus, chemistry and physics; if not completed, HMC "may offer you conditional admission" requiring a one-semester college course with B or better [M80].

### 8. Olin
- **Entry.** [事實] Olin offers three ABET-accredited degrees (ECE, ME, Engineering) and a Bioengineering concentration (E:Bio); every student takes courses in software, electronics and mechanical systems [M79b]. [推論] Admission is to the college; no admission by major is described.
- **Courses.** [事實] A calculus and physics foundation "is therefore a requirement for admission" (roster part 2, S7) [M79].

### 9. Swarthmore
- **Entry.** [事實] Swarthmore has an Engineering Department within a liberal-arts college [M82]. Admission by major: not stated (checked 2026-11-07, M82; roster part 2 S49–S50).

### 10. Amherst
- **Entry.** [事實] The first-year application page describes ED/RD and required items; no major selection is described [M84]. [事實] The academic departments page read on 2026-11-07 lists no engineering department [M85]. [推論] An engineering applicant has no engineering major to enter here; biology is declared after enrolment. Not confirmed by a statement on the page.

### 11. Williams
- **Not read.** admission.williams.edu returned HTTP 403 on 2026-11-07 [M83]. All cells: not checked.

## B. Schools that admit to a school or college at application

### 12. Princeton
- **Entry.** [事實] The application asks A.B. or B.S.E. (roster S32). [事實] "Once enrolled, students may pursue either the Bachelor of Arts (A.B.) or the Bachelor of Science in Engineering (B.S.E.)"; computer science offers both A.B. and B.S.E. [M11].
- **Internal move.** [事實] "All degree candidacy changes — whether into or out of the B.S.E. program — are initiated by the Undergraduate Affairs Office" of SEAS; an A.B. admit who wants B.S.E. contacts the office; degree changes for new students are not processed between July 15 and the first day of fall classes [M12]. [事實] A.B. students keeping engineering open should take Physics 103-104 or 105-106 and Math 103 or higher in the first year, and COS 126 for CS [M12]. No GPA threshold or cap is stated [M12].
- **Courses.** [事實] Not requirements: 4 years math "including calculus for students interested in engineering"; 2+ years lab science "including physics and chemistry for students interested in engineering" [M11].

### 13. Columbia
- **Entry.** [事實] "Applicants to Columbia must choose either Columbia College or Columbia Engineering." [M14] Biology sits in Columbia College [推論, from the two-school structure on M13].
- **Internal transfer.** Not stated on the pages read (checked 2026-11-07, M13, M14, M14b). [事實] The CC–SEAS advising FAQ states Engineering students declare a major in the first semester of sophomore year and College students in the second semester [M14b]. [推論] A third-party mirror describing a March 1 internal-transfer application was found by search and is **not used**; the official page was not located.
- **Courses.** [事實] Engineering "strongly advises" 4 years math through calculus and 4 years lab science including a year each of chemistry and physics [M13][M14]. One teacher letter must come from math or science for Engineering applicants [M13].

### 14. University of Pennsylvania
- **Entry.** [事實] "you don't technically apply to Penn, you apply to one of our four undergraduate schools (or one of our coordinated dual-degree programs)" [M15]. Penn Engineering grants the BSE and BAS [M15].
- **Internal transfer into Penn Engineering.** [事實] Must complete at least 8 Penn course units; "The minimum gpa for consideration is 3.0"; should have completed MATH 1400 and 1410 and typically PHYS 0150/0151; must apply "prior to the end of the sophomore year"; must select a major and submit a four-year plan; external transfers are ineligible [M16]. Acceptance numbers: not stated [M16].
- **Courses.** [事實] Engineering looks for "exposure to calculus and physics" [M15]; "Strong preparation in physics and mathematics, particularly calculus" (roster S44) [M17].

### 15. Cornell
- **Entry.** [事實] "you'll apply directly to one of our colleges or schools" [M20].
  - Engineering: [事實] first-years start in a common curriculum and apply "for affiliation with your major in your second year" [M21].
  - Biology: [事實] CALS applicants "apply directly to one of our 20+ majors" [M22]. Arts & Sciences applicants select up to two areas of interest and declare by the end of sophomore year [M23]. [推論] Biological Sciences is reachable through more than one college; the application decides which college, and in CALS the major too.
- **Internal transfer into Duffield Engineering.** [事實] All 14 majors open, including Computer Science and ECE; no internal transfer into undeclared status; must spend at least three semesters affiliated; first-years may first apply in the second semester ("Applications submitted after just one semester will be denied"); not open to second-semester juniors or seniors; decisions are made by each department; "conditional transfer" possible [M24][M25]. A numeric GPA threshold is not stated; the page refers to each major's published affiliation criteria [M24]. Acceptance numbers: not stated.
- **CS.** [事實] Computer Science is listed among Duffield Engineering majors open to internal transfer [M24]. [推論] CS is offered through Engineering and Arts & Sciences; only the Engineering route is documented here.
- **Courses.** [事實] Duffield requires 4 units math including calculus, 1 physics, 1 chemistry; recommends 1 CS and 1 biology [M21]. CALS: biology and chemistry required; calculus required for Biological Sciences and Biological Engineering, physics for Biological Engineering [M22].

### 16. Georgetown
- **Entry.** [事實] "Applicants are required to apply directly to one of Georgetown's six undergraduate schools." [M26] Engineering: no engineering school is named in the roster record of the six schools (roster S62). Not confirmed on M26 itself.
- **Courses.** [事實] Science/math majors: 4 years math and at least 3 years science (roster S64) [M27].

### 17. Carnegie Mellon
- **Entry.** [事實] "Students applying to the College of Engineering apply to and are admitted directly to a department/major unless choosing undecided." Only students admitted as undecided may choose among Civil, Chemical, ECE, Environmental, Materials Science and Mechanical in spring of year one [M28].
- **Internal move.** [事實] "Requests to change majors will be considered after the first year, but are not guaranteed to be approved and will be evaluated on a case by case basis." [M28] [事實] Biomedical Engineering is an additional major declared in spring of the first year, alongside a primary major [M28].
- **Second choice.** [事實] ED applicants "can apply to only one college within the university" (roster S69) [M30].
- **CS.** [事實] The School of Computer Science is a separate college with its own course requirements and required SAT/ACT [M29][M28b].
- **Courses.** [事實] Engineering and Mellon College of Science: 4 math (through pre-calculus, "preferably calculus"), 1 chemistry, 1 physics, 1 biology; SCS: 4 math, 1 physics, 2 years of chemistry, biology or CS [M29].

### 18. Johns Hopkins
- **Entry.** [推論] Admission is to the university with an intended major; BME is the exception.
- **BME capped.** [事實] "Students are admitted to BME based on evaluation of credentials and space available. It is possible to be admitted to the university without acceptance to the BME major." BME must be the first-choice major; no separate application [M51][M52]. [事實] An admit not placed in BME "may select any other intended major in the Whiting School of Engineering or Krieger School of Arts & Sciences" [M51].
- **Internal transfer into BME.** Not read: engineering.jhu.edu returned HTTP 403. [推論] A search summary reported that WSE advising states students cannot change into BME. This is **not verified** and is not used as fact.
- **Courses.** Not stated on M51 (checked 2026-11-07).

### 19. Duke
- **Entry.** [事實] Applicants "Choose One": Trinity College of Arts & Sciences or Pratt School of Engineering [M54].
- **Internal transfer.** [事實] "After completing one semester in the college or school in which you matriculated, Trinity College and Pratt School of Engineering students may apply to transfer between the two schools." [M55] [事實] Deadlines: Spring transfer December 15, Fall transfer May 15; must be in good academic standing; transfers into Pratt enter as undeclared [M56]. GPA threshold or cap: not stated [M55][M56].
- **Courses.** [事實] Pratt: "we require coursework in calculus and strongly recommend physics"; one letter should come from a math or science teacher [M54].

### 20. Northwestern
- **Entry.** [事實] "Northwestern requires all applicants to apply to one of the six undergraduate schools. There is no strategic advantage to applying to any specific school, as we use the same admission review criteria for all"; applicants may apply "Undecided" to Weinberg, McCormick or SESP [M57].
- **Internal transfer.** [事實] Moving between schools is an Interschool Transfer (IST); "Each undergraduate school maintains its own admission standards; IST requests are not always approved by the proposed new school." [M59] McCormick-specific GPA or course thresholds: not stated (checked 2026-11-07, M59).
- **Courses.** [事實] McCormick recommends 3+ years lab science ("chemistry and physics preferred") and 3.5–4 years math ("calculus preferred, if available") (roster part 2 S21) [M58].

### 21. Rice
- **Entry.** [事實] Architecture and Shepherd School of Music have separate materials; music admits "must pursue the music program for at least one year before changing schools" [M60]. Whether engineering or natural-sciences applicants are admitted to a school: not stated (checked 2026-11-07, M60).
- **Courses.** [事實] "the natural science and engineering divisions require trigonometry or precalculus, and both chemistry and physics"; a second year of chemistry or biology may replace physics [M60].

## C. Public universities and institutes with capacity rules

### 22. Georgia Tech
- **Entry.** [事實] First-year applicants "do not apply to a specific major or college"; they select an intended (primary) major and may add a secondary one [M31].
- **Change before enrolment.** [事實] Deposited students confirm their major by May 15 and may confirm a major other than the one on the application; only Music Technology is restricted there [M32].
- **Change after enrolment.** [事實] Institute rule: one unrestricted change of major before 60 credit hours; after that, at the receiving school's discretion; "The Institute reserves the right to limit access to programs due to capacity" [M33].
- **CS.** [事實] The College of Computing is "now prepared to begin accepting Change of Major applications again on a limited basis"; since April 15, 2025 the process differs for students who matriculated before and after summer 2024 [M34]. The post-summer-2024 rules are in an image on M35 and were **not read**.
- **Courses.** [事實] University System of Georgia minimums only; engineering-specific prerequisites not stated (roster S77) [M31b].

### 23. Purdue (West Lafayette)
- **Entry.** [事實] Applicants pick a primary location and major and may pick an alternate; "Changing majors or locations is never guaranteed." [M36] [事實] Review asks whether the applicant is competitive "for the program based on available space" [M36]. [事實] Engineering, CS and other programs have a November 1 priority deadline after which applications are accepted "only if space allows" (roster S80) [M38].
- **Engineering capped after entry.** [事實] 2026-27 catalog, First-Year Engineering CODO: minimum 1 semester, 12 credit hours, cumulative GPA 2.7, a 2.5 "CODO GPA" across three course categories (math; science/CS; an engineering or communication course); "Students are admitted on a SPACE AVAILABLE BASIS ONLY after holistic review. Space is extremely limited."; effective terms Spring and Summer only [M40].
- **CS (College of Science, separate from Engineering).** [事實] 2026-27 CS CODO: cumulative GPA 2.75; A- or better in CS 18000; B or better in calculus; "SPACE AVAILABLE BASIS ONLY. Space is extremely limited." [M41]
- **Courses.** [事實] "Engineering applicants: Chemistry experience is expected" (roster S83) [M39].

### 24. University of Michigan
- **Entry.** [事實] Read by the roster run on 2026-11-07: Michigan Engineering first-years enter undeclared within the College of Engineering (roster S87) [M43]. [推論] Admission to the College of Engineering is decided at application; the engineering major is chosen later.
- **Internal transfer (cross-campus).** **Not verified** (pages returned 403 on 2026-11-07). A search summary of an official Michigan Engineering URL reported GPA figures; they are not used [M44b].
- **Courses.** [事實] Review places "special emphasis on precalculus, chemistry and physics" (roster S84) [M42]; Engineering advises at least one course in chemistry, calculus and physics and says it will not penalize applicants whose school lacks them (roster S86) [M44].

### 25. University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign
- **Entry.** [事實] Applicants are reviewed for a first-choice major; "Admit rates vary depending on your college or program of study." [M45]
- **Second choice.** [事實] "we strongly recommend selecting a second choice major"; admission to a second choice means being "wait listed for your first choice"; "Illinois residents will be given preference for admission into second-choice majors"; Computer Science (including all CS blended majors), Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering and Mechanical Engineering are not available as second choices [M45].
- **CS closed after entry.** [事實] B.S. Computer Science, CS + Physics and CS + Bioengineering in Grainger "are direct admit only and closed to on-campus transfer (ICT/IDT)" for students entering fall 2022 and later [M47][M48].
- **Engineering after entry.** [事實] Engineering Undeclared "accepts a limited number of ICT students each semester, so the application process is competitive"; eligible only in the second and/or third semester; minimum cumulative GPA 3.30 and Grainger technical GPA 3.0 with B- or better in technical courses; Calculus 1 required [M49]. [事實] Students who entered as off-campus transfers into other colleges are not eligible for later transfer into Grainger [M50][M48].
- **Courses.** [事實] Every Grainger major requires 3.5 years math "including trigonometry or higher"; 2 years lab science required [M46].

### 26. UC Berkeley
- **Entry.** [事實] Berkeley "only guarantees the review of the primary major and will not review students for an alternate major" (roster S25) [M61].
- **Engineering capped.** [事實] College of Engineering: "Will I be admitted? The answer depends upon the quality of the applicant pool and the number of spaces available in the major." [M63] [事實] Change of major after admission is "highly unlikely for impacted majors, such as Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences"; students who want a different major "are encouraged to accept their offer of admission to another institution where they are guaranteed to pursue their major of choice" [M63].
- **Change of college into Engineering.** [事實] "very competitive and highly unlikely"; not accepted for EECS or EECS joint majors; Environmental Engineering closed for 2026-27; only first-year admits may apply, in the fall or spring of the second year; at least 2 semesters of letter grades; decisions final [M64].
- **L&S high-demand majors.** [事實] Listing a high-demand L&S major as primary major guarantees it if admitted to L&S, subject to conditions; switching later is by review, with "one opportunity", and "extremely limited" [M62]. Which majors are high-demand is not listed on M62.
- **Courses.** [事實] Engineering strongly recommends physics beyond the two-year lab-science minimum [M63].

### 27. UCLA
- **Entry.** [事實] "UCLA reviews first-year applicants for one major only"; the UC alternate major is for other campuses [M65]. [事實] Changes into or within Engineering allowed until January 1 [M65].
- **Change of major into Samueli.** [事實] "no application is guaranteed for approval"; earliest after two quarters (longer for CS, CSE, CE); latest Fall of the third year; "Only ONE change into an engineering major will be approved"; application windows each quarter in 2026-27 [M66]. Numeric GPA thresholds are in tables on M66 that were not extracted in this run.
- **Transfers.** [事實] Junior transfers cannot change into CS, CSE, Aerospace or Mechanical Engineering because they are "severely impacted majors" [M67].

### 28. UC San Diego
- **Entry / capped.** [事實] "A selective major is one with limited enrollment, due to capacity"; Engineering is selective; applicants should list a non-selective alternate major, admitted "if space is available" [M68]. [事實] CS, ECE, EE, Bioengineering and other engineering majors are marked selective on the majors page; Biology majors are not marked [M69].
- **Switching in later.** [事實] Continuing students apply once a year (first eligible the summer after year one); minimum 2.0 UC GPA plus screening courses; ranked by a point system (one point each for 3.0+ screening GPA, California residency, Pell eligibility, first-generation status); ties broken at random; no appeal [M70][M71].
- **Published selection numbers.** [事實] M70 publishes continuing-student cycles: CSE 76 applied / 25 selected (33%) in Summer 2025 and 55 / 25 (45%) in Summer 2026; Bioengineering 42 / 26 (62%) and 46 / 36 (78%); ECE 33 / 22 (67%) and 50 / 42 (84%); Mechanical 126 / 35 (28%) and 73 / 40 (55%); Aerospace 34 / 25 (74%) and 37 / 25 (68%). Some cycles carry the note "All applicants who met the eligibility criteria were selected." [M70]

### 29. UT Austin
- **Entry.** [事實] Applicants choose first- and second-choice majors; "Competitiveness of the major you applied to" is a review factor; admits wanting a different major "should join the waitlist" [M72][M73b].
- **Engineering.** [事實] Cockrell offers freshman admission for summer or fall only; Cockrell application deadline December 1 [M73][M75].
- **Internal transfer into Cockrell.** [事實] Minimum cumulative and technical GPA 3.0; in-residence calculus and technical courses; 500-word essay; offered "on a space-available basis"; "The Cockrell School does not have a standard GPA requirement for internal transfer admission. The cumulative and required technical GPAs of admitted students vary by semester and requested major, as does the number of seats offered." [M76]
- **CS (Natural Sciences, separate from Cockrell).** [事實] CNS internal transfer is required for CNS students applying to Computer Science; minimum GPA 3.0, 24 in-residence hours; "very competitive" [M77].
- **Biology.** [事實] Natural Sciences students "are admitted with entry-level status in their chosen major" [M73].
- **Courses.** [事實] Calculus readiness required for all Cockrell majors and for Computer Science (AP Calculus 3+, or B or better in a Calculus I course, among other markers) [M74].

### 30. University of Washington (Seattle)
- **Entry / capped.** [事實] Three kinds of major: open, minimum requirements, capacity-constrained ("selective"; meeting minimums "does not guarantee entry") [M78]. [事實] Direct-to-major list includes Computer science, Biochemistry and Neuroscience; engineering applicants are considered for "Direct to College admission to the College of Engineering" [M78].
- **CS.** [事實] Not admitted as a first-year: "it's unlikely that you'll be admitted to computer science or computer engineering as a UW student" [M78].
- **Second choice.** [事實] "the second-choice major is not used for placement into direct-to-major or direct-to-college/school options" [M78].
- **Engineering later.** [事實] "there are opportunities to apply at a later date" [M78]. Thresholds not stated on M78.

## Non-obvious findings

1. [事實] UIUC does not allow CS, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering or Mechanical Engineering as a second-choice major, and Grainger CS has been closed to on-campus transfer since fall 2022 [M45][M47]. [推論] For these majors at UIUC, the first-choice line on the application is the only route in.
2. [事實] Berkeley Engineering tells applicants who want a different major to accept "another institution where they are guaranteed to pursue their major of choice" [M63], and does not accept change-of-college applications for EECS at all [M64].
3. [事實] UC San Diego publishes how many continuing students applied and were selected into each selective major; CSE selected 25 in both Summer 2025 and Summer 2026 while applicants fell from 76 to 55 [M70]. [推論] This is the only school in the set that publishes internal-entry numbers on the pages read.
4. [事實] UC San Diego's internal selection awards points for California residency and Pell eligibility, alongside a 3.0 screening-course GPA [M70]. [推論] An international student starts at least one point behind a California resident with the same grades.

[事實] Georgia Tech admits to no college, lets deposited students confirm a different major by May 15 (Music Technology excepted) [M32], and allows one unrestricted change before 60 credit hours, but reserves the right to limit programs by capacity, and CS change of major is open only "on a limited basis" [M33][M34].

[事實] At Penn, students who entered from another university are ineligible for internal transfer into Penn Engineering [M16]; at UIUC, students who entered other colleges as off-campus transfers cannot move into Grainger [M50]; at UCLA, junior transfers cannot change into CS, CSE, Aerospace or Mechanical [M67].

11. [事實] UW says the second-choice major is not used for direct-to-major or direct-to-college placement [M78]. [推論] At UW the second choice does not work as a fallback into engineering or CS.

## 中文摘要

這份筆記回答一個問題：申請工程（含 EE/ECE/CS）或生物／生醫的學生，到底是在申請時就決定進哪個學院或主修，還是入學後才決定？之後能不能轉進去？

- [事實] 約三分之一的學校不按主修錄取：MIT、Caltech、Stanford、Harvard、Yale、Brown、Harvey Mudd、Olin 都在官方頁面寫明錄取的是整個學校，主修入學後再宣告。Brown 更明說各 concentration「沒有名額」[M01][M03][M05][M07][M18]。
- [事實] 私校多半要求申請時選學院：Penn 四個學院擇一、Columbia 在 CC 與 Engineering 之間只能選一、Duke 在 Trinity 與 Pratt 之間擇一、Northwestern 六個學院擇一（並表示選哪個學院沒有策略上的優勢）、Cornell 直接申請學院（CALS 甚至直接申請到主修）、CMU 工學院直接錄取到系 [M14][M15][M20][M28][M54][M57]。
- [事實] 公立大學有明文的容量限制：UC San Diego 的工程是 selective major（「因容量而限制人數」），Berkeley 工學院錄取「取決於該主修的名額」，UW 有 capacity-constrained majors，Purdue 轉入 First-Year Engineering 與 CS 都寫「SPACE AVAILABLE BASIS ONLY」[M40][M41][M63][M68][M78]。
- [事實] 入學後轉 CS 最難：UIUC Grainger 的 CS 自 2022 年秋起只收直接錄取、不開放校內轉入；UW 表示沒在申請時錄取 CS，之後「不太可能」進得去；Berkeley 工學院不受理轉入 EECS 的 change of college [M47][M64][M78]。
- [事實] UIUC 不允許 CS、CE、EE、ME 當第二志願；UW 的第二志願不用於直接錄取主修的安置 [M45][M78]。
- [事實] 唯一公布校內轉主修數字的是 UC San Diego：CSE 在 2025 夏季 76 人申請、25 人入選（33%），2026 夏季 55 人申請、25 人入選（45%）。其積分制有一分給加州居民、一分給 Pell 資格 [M70]。[推論] 國際學生在這套積分制裡起點就少至少一分。
- [事實] 校內轉入有公開門檻的例子：Penn 工學院 GPA 3.0、修滿 8 個 course units；UT Austin Cockrell GPA 3.0 但「沒有標準 GPA」，席次每學期不同；UIUC Engineering Undeclared GPA 3.30、只限第二或第三學期；Purdue FYE CODO GPA 2.7 [M16][M40][M49][M76]。
- [事實] 除了 UIUC 一句「錄取率依學院或主修而不同」之外，30 所學校的官方頁面都沒有公布按學院或主修區分的新生錄取率 [M45]。
- 未能核實：Michigan 全部頁面（403）、Williams（403）、JHU BME 入學後能否轉入、Columbia 校內轉學院的官方頁面、Rice 是否按學院錄取、Georgia Tech 2024 夏季後入學者轉 CS 的細則（以圖片公布）。這些欄位留白，不填推測。
