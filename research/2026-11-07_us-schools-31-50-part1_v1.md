# US schools 31–50, part 1: roster (G2) and supplementary-materials matrix (G3)

Schools in this note: University of Chicago, Dartmouth College, Vanderbilt University,
Washington University in St. Louis (WashU), University of Notre Dame, Emory University,
University of Southern California (USC), University of Virginia (UVA), New York University (NYU),
Tufts University. Part 2 (the other 10 of the 20) is a separate note.

## How to read this note / 閱讀說明

- Every sourced statement carries a tag and a source id `[Sxx]`; the id resolves in `## Sources`
  to URL, access date (all 2026-11-07) and class. [事實] = stated on that page. [推論] = our
  inference from stated facts. [假設] = assumption. Nothing here describes how readers behave
  unless an admissions office said it on its own page.
- "Cycle 2026–27" = applying in autumn 2026 for entry in fall 2027 (Class of 2031).
- `not stated (checked 2026-11-07, URL)` means we read that page and it does not answer the
  question. It does not mean "no".
- Method: pages were fetched directly from each school's admissions domain (plain HTTP, or the
  page's own data file for script-rendered sites, or a browser pane for NYU). Web search was
  not available in this run. Quotes are ≤15 words.
- 本筆記以英文為主要記錄語言，每校附繁體中文摘要；所有數字、日期以英文段落與來源為準。

## 1. University of Chicago

**Roster**

- Application systems: Coalition (powered by Scoir) or Common Application; QuestBridge route
  also described [S01][S03]. [事實]
- Plans and deadlines 2026–27: Early Action (non-binding) and Early Decision I, both
  **Nov 2, 2026**; Early Decision II and Regular Decision, both **Jan 4, 2027**; 11:59 p.m.
  applicant's local time [S03]. EA→ED I switch deadline Dec 1; deferred EA / RD→ED II switch
  deadline Jan 15 [S03]. [事實]
- Test policy 2026–27: SAT/ACT optional, with a "No Harm" policy: a submitted score is used
  only if it helps; applies to international applicants too [S01]. [事實]
- English proficiency: no fixed exam requirement. Applicants "are invited" to submit any
  English proficiency exam; if English mastery is shown elsewhere in the application, formal
  scores are not required; self-reported scores accepted [S06]. [事實]
- Need-blind/need-aware (internationals): not stated (checked 2026-11-07, [S06][S07]). The
  pages say UChicago meets 100% of international students' demonstrated need, and that
  internationals must apply for aid at the time of application to be eligible later [S02][S07].
  [事實] Whether ability to pay is considered for internationals is not stated; we do not infer it.
- Supplement: one extended essay chosen from a list of prompts plus one "Why UChicago" essay
  [S01]. No strict word limits; the FAQ gives 500–700 words (extended) and 300–600 words (Why
  UChicago) as "rough guidelines" [S05]. [事實] 2026–27 prompts (paraphrased) [S04]: how
  thoughts eat; use an artistic practice's principles to solve a problem (origami/JWST
  framing); invent and defend a mixed metaphor; design an Olympic event for an everyday task;
  a case of mistaken acronym identity; or choose a past prompt / write your own. [事實]
- Teacher letters: two, from teachers of academic subjects [S01]. [事實]
- Other Recommender: yes, one optional supplemental letter (another teacher, employer, role
  model, youth leader, or friend); "not an expectation" [S01]. [事實]
- Interview: no on-campus or alumni interviews; an optional two-minute **video profile** is
  offered "in lieu of" an interview; Glimpse or InitialView accepted in place of the video
  profile [S02][S05]. [事實]

## 2. Dartmouth College

**Roster**

- Application system: Common App only for first-year applicants ("must use the Common App");
  QuestBridge finalists may use the QuestBridge application [S08]. [事實]
- Plans and deadlines: Early Decision (binding) **Nov 1**; Regular Decision **Jan 1** per the
  RD timeline and the portfolio page [S08][S16]. [事實] The QuestBridge non-finalist block on
  the same page states the RD deadline as **January 2** [S08]. [事實] The page therefore gives
  two RD dates; we record both and do not choose. [事實]
- Test policy: SAT or ACT **required** for students at US high schools; students outside the
  US may instead use three AP exams, IB predicted/final, A-Level predicted/final, or final
  national exams [S09]. The requirement was reactivated "beginning with applicants to the
  Class of 2029" [S10]. [事實]
- English proficiency: required if first language is not English and the curriculum has not
  been in English for at least two years; TOEFL, IELTS, Duolingo, or Cambridge English; no
  minimums, but typical successful scores published (IELTS >7, DET >135, Cambridge >185,
  TOEFL ≥5 on the post-Jan-21-2026 scale) [S11]. [事實]
- Need-blind for internationals: **yes**, "universal need-blind" for all international
  citizens, effective from the Class of 2026 [S12]. [事實]
- Supplement (Class of 2031) [S13]: (1) Why Dartmouth, ≤100 words; (2) one of two prompts on
  background/self-introduction, ≤250 words; (3) one of six prompts (A–F, e.g. what excites
  you, impact, reading, a difficult conversation, "celebrate your nerdy side", difference),
  ≤250 words. [事實]
- Teacher letters: two teacher evaluations required [S08][S14]. [事實]
- Other Recommender: yes. A **peer recommendation** is "strongly encouraged" (listed as
  "Recommended Application Component"); an additional recommendation may be sent via Common
  App "Other Recommender" or the portal; arts applicants are asked to have an arts instructor
  submit via "Other Recommender" [S08][S14][S15]. [事實]
- Interview: optional alumni interviews offered after application, not to all applicants;
  "informative and evaluative"; an interview report is added to the file [S19]. [事實]

## Non-obvious findings / 不明顯的發現

1. **Only two of nine verified schools name a way to send research outside the arts, and they
   point in opposite directions on length.** UChicago invites "an abstract of original research"
   via its account [S05]; Dartmouth accepts single-page abstracts and says "Please do not
   submit full research papers" [S18]. Emory accepts any PDF without naming research [S40].
   [事實] → For a biology applicant at these schools, a one-page abstract is the format both
   explicit statements converge on. [推論]
   研究成果：芝加哥大學與達特茅斯都只要「摘要」，達特茅斯明說不收全文。[事實][S05][S18]
2. **USC Viterbi refuses unrequested material outright**: "we do not accept anything that we do
   not ask for in the application", including resume, CV, portfolio, and extra letters [S48].
   [事實] → An engineering applicant's project record can enter USC only through the activities
   list and written answers. [推論]
   USC Viterbi 明確拒收未要求的履歷、作品集與額外推薦信。[事實][S48]
3. **Tufts puts the maker portfolio inside a required essay.** Engineering applicants must
   describe a project they helped "build, design, create, or iterate" and their role, in
   100–200 words [S56], while no separate maker-portfolio upload is listed [S55]. [事實]
   Tufts 工程申請者以必答短文（100–200 字）描述專案，未見另設作品上傳。[事實][S55][S56]
4. **Three schools ask for only one teacher letter** (WashU, Notre Dame, Tufts) and two ask for
   one letter that need not be from a teacher (USC: counselor or teacher; NYU: anyone "in a
   position of authority", up to three) [S24][S33][S55][S44][S51]. Notre Dame and USC Viterbi
   discourage more letters [S35][S48]. [事實] → An outside mentor can be a full recommender at
   NYU and an optional extra at UChicago and Dartmouth, but has no stated place at Notre Dame,
   USC Viterbi, Vanderbilt, Emory, WashU or Tufts. [推論]
5. **Dartmouth is the only school here that asks for a peer letter**, and it is listed as
   "Recommended" rather than optional; it may arrive after the deadline, ideally within ten
   days [S08][S15]. [事實]
6. **Testing direction differs by school and year.** Required now: Dartmouth (from Class of
   2029) [S10]. Optional now, required next: Notre Dame (from the 2027–28 cycle) [S31] and
   Vanderbilt (from fall 2029 entry) [S22]. Optional through 2027–28: NYU [S52]. [事實]
   A G10 student in 2026–27 applying to Notre Dame in autumn 2027 would face a requirement.
   [推論]
7. **Emory's two pages disagree on the entry year** of its test-optional statement: fall 2027
   on the exams page [S39], fall 2028 in the FAQ [S40]. [事實] Re-check before publishing a cell.
8. **Emory tells international aid applicants to apply Regular Decision** [S43], so ED is not
   the route for an international student who needs aid. [事實][推論 for the consequence]
9. **Need policy for internationals is explicit at only four schools**: need-blind at
   Dartmouth [S12] and Notre Dame [S34]; need-aware at Vanderbilt [S21]; no need-based aid at
   USC [S45]. WashU excludes internationals from its need-blind program [S27]. Five others do not
   say. [事實]
10. **Two schools set video as the interview substitute**: UChicago (two-minute profile, "highly
    recommended") [S05] and WashU (90 s, "we mean optional") [S28]. [事實] USC and Tufts accept
    third-party interviews only as English evidence, not as evaluation [S45][S58]. [事實]
11. **Dartmouth's studio-art rules exclude links**: "Do not submit the link or HTML code" for
    website work; make a video instead [S17]. Emory, by contrast, accepts "link to a YouTube
    page" [S40]. [事實]
12. **Tufts BFA portfolios do not use SlideRoom**; they upload to the Applicant Status Page
    [S60], while WashU and Notre Dame use SlideRoom [S29][S33]. [事實]
