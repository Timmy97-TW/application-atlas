# Application systems other than Common App (cycle 2026–27, entry fall 2027)

摘要（繁中）：本筆記整理 Common App 以外的五個申請系統（UC Application、Coalition on Scoir、MIT 自有申請、Georgetown Application、ApplyTexas），並逐校查核 30 所學校 2026–27 第一年申請接受哪些系統。重點更正兩個前提：Universal College Application 並未關閉（2026/2027 版仍上線，但目錄只列 1 所學校）；Coalition 的獨立平台轉到 Scoir 的時間是 2022 年 8 月 1 日起可申請，而非 2023 年。所有標記依 CLAUDE.md：[事實] 有來源、[推論] 由事實推得、[假設] 未經驗證。

Summary (EN): Five non-Common-App systems are documented with their fields and limits, followed by a 30-school matrix built from each school's own apply page where the page could be read. Two premises in the brief did not hold up: the Universal College Application is still operating for 2026/2027 (with one listed member), and the Coalition's move to Scoir dates from 2022, not 2023.

Method note: the WebSearch budget for this run ran out partway through; remaining checks used direct fetches of known official URLs. Pages that returned 403/404 are recorded as such, not filled in.

## 1. University of California (UC) Application

### 1.1 What it covers
- [事實] One application, campuses chosen inside it in the "Campuses & majors" section; "All UC campuses are now open for the fall 2027 application." [S9]
- [事實] The nine undergraduate campuses linked from the UC application site are Berkeley, Davis, Irvine, UCLA, Merced, Riverside, San Diego, Santa Barbara, Santa Cruz. [S59]
- [事實] Sections: About you; Campuses & majors; Academic history; Test scores; Activities & awards; Scholarships & support programs; Personal insight questions; Review & submit. [S3]
- [事實] Fee: $80 per campus; $95 for international and non-immigrant applicants. Fee waivers cover up to four campuses. [S9] [S8]
- [事實] New for this cycle: a checkbox to declare non-UC fee waivers (NACAC, College Board) inside the application; waivers still cannot be combined beyond four campuses. [S4]

### 1.2 Filing window and dates (fall 2027 entry)
- [事實] Application opens 1 August; filing period 1 October – 30 November. [S2]
- [事實] The UCOP September 2026 deck gives the filing deadline as "Monday, November 30, 2026". [S4]
- [事實] Decisions 1–31 March (some campuses earlier); SIR by 1 May; final transcripts by 1 July; AP/IB official results by 15 July. [S2]
- [事實] No extension is stated on the official dates page as of 2026-11-07. [S2]
- [事實][secondary] A consultant blog states that UC extended the deadline to 1 December in the fall 2026 cycle. [S58] — not verified on an official page; treat as unconfirmed. [推論] A one-time extension in a prior cycle does not imply one this cycle; plan to 30 November.

### 1.3 Personal insight questions (PIQ)
- [事實] Answer 4 of 8 questions; up to 350 words each; "All questions are equal." [S1]
- [事實] The eight prompts cover: leadership; creativity; greatest talent/skill; educational opportunity or barrier; significant challenge; inspiring academic subject; making school/community better; what makes you a strong candidate beyond the rest of the application. [S1]
- [事實] The page does not mention a 2026 change to the prompts. [S1]
- [事實] UC's 2026 counselor deck includes a "Statement of application integrity" slide framing AI as "a tool not a replacement" and the PIQs as "an interview, not an essay." [S4]

### 1.4 Activities & awards
- [事實] Up to 20 entries. [S7] (UCSB, official campus page)
- [事實] Six categories: Award or honor; Educational preparation programs; Extracurricular activity; Other coursework; Volunteering/Community service; Work experience. [S3] [S7]
- [事實] Description field: 350 characters (UCSB worksheet, file created 2026-06-11). [S6]
- [事實] Systemwide page S3 states no maximum count and no character limits on its own text; it says "We're looking for quality, not quantity." [S3] — the 20/350 figures come from a campus (UCSB) source, not the systemwide page.
- [事實] Worksheet fields: category, activity/award name, description, grades involved (9–12), hours/week, weeks/year. [S6]

### 1.5 Additional comments
- [事實] Systemwide page S3 states no character limit; the field "shouldn't be an essay." [S3]
- [事實] UC's 2026 myths deck: the additional comments box "should NOT be a 5th PIQ. We will not consider that." [S5]
- Limit: not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S3, S5). [事實] Secondary sources quote 550 characters (academic history) and 550 words (post-PIQ) attributed to a 2023 UC deck whose URL now returns 404; not used here.

### 1.6 Letters of recommendation
- [事實] International applicants page: do not submit LoRs with the UC application; "some campuses may request them later during supplemental review." [S8]
- [事實] Berkeley's student guide (undated; describes a policy "as of fall 2015") says some first-year applicants are invited by email to submit up to two optional letters, one from an academic teacher; letters go to Berkeley directly, not to the UC Application Center; deadline 15 January; uninvited letters are not added. [S11]
- [事實] Berkeley's current FAQ and first-year policy pages do not mention letters of recommendation. [S10] [S12]
- [推論] S11 lists a November 1–30 filing window, which predates the current 1 October – 30 November window, so its dates should not be used for 2026–27. Current-cycle Berkeley LoR dates: not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S10, S12).

### 1.7 Testing
- [事實] "UC will not consider SAT or ACT test scores when making admissions decisions or awarding scholarships." "SAT and ACT scores may only be reported in your application post-submission." [S3]
- [事實] Berkeley: "test-free"; scores used only for placement/credit after admission. [S10] [S12]
- [事實] BOARS is reviewing the use of standardized testing; report due February 2027; Regents to receive recommendations by June 2027. "No change to admissions requirements for students applying this November for admission to fall 2027." Fall 2028+ is TBD. [S4]

### 1.8 Academic record: self-report, not a separate product
- [事實] Applicants enter schools, courses, and grades in the application from their transcripts; "don't enter information from your memory." [S3]
- [事實] Records are not submitted with the application; official records are required only if admitted (grade 9 onward). [S8]
- [事實] California public high school students have CaliforniaColleges.edu (CCGI) accounts, and UC lists "CCGI integration" among 2026 updates. [S4] Detail of what the integration transfers: not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S4).
- "UC Academic Record", "UARS", "SIRS": not stated as UC products on any UC page read (checked 2026-11-07, S3, S8). [推論] UC's self-report sits inside the application itself, unlike UW's STARS or UIUC's post-submission link (see §7).
- [事實] Students with college/dual-enrollment courses must report them; "failure to do so may lead to application cancellation." [S4]

### 1.9 International applicants
- [事實] Report each course and grade exactly as the school records it; external exams (IGCSE, GCSE, GCE, SPM, etc.) go on the International Exams page under Test Scores; report every sitting. [S8]
- [事實] Requirements: 15 year-long academic courses, 3.4 GPA, plus country-specific rules and English proficiency. [S8]
- [事實] TOEFL iBT moved to a 1–6 scale in January 2026; UC's minimum is 4.5; campuses may set higher. [S4]
- [事實] Fee $95 per campus. [S9]

## 2. Coalition Application on Scoir

- [事實] The Coalition describes itself as "more than 150" member colleges. [S14]
- [事實] Scoir's list says 116 colleges "accept applications through Scoir" as of 20 October 2026. [S16]
- [推論] Coalition membership and accepting the Coalition application are different things (see §7 and findings).
- [事實] Stages of Part 1 (core): Personal; Family; Education (high school, coursework, tests, English tests, honors and distinctions, activities); Essay & More (Coalition essay "if required by the college", additional information, counselor); Review & Submit. Part 1 cannot be edited after submission. [S18]
- [事實] Part 2 (supplemental questions) is completed on each college's own portal via SSO after Part 1. [S18]
- [事實] Essay: "minimum of 250 words but 500-650 is typically a good target." [S18] Six prompts, the sixth topic of choice; "aim for your essay to be between 500-650 words." [S21] (S21 labels the prompts 2024–25 & 2025–26; a 2026–27 label is not stated, checked 2026-11-07.)
- Activities count, honors count, character limits: not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S18). [事實] Activities entered on the Scoir profile "don't auto-populate yet" into the application. [S19]
- [事實] Scoir is free for students. [S14]
- [事實] Recommendations: Scoir high schools send documents after Part 1 submission; non-Scoir schools follow a separate guide; required number not stated. [S18]

### 2.1 History: MyCoalition, the Locker, the move to Scoir
- [事實] Scoir: "Coalition for College discontinued its standalone application portal"; students could begin applying through Scoir on 1 August 2022. [S19]
- [事實] The Coalition: "We've discontinued our use of our previous technology platform." [S15]
- [事實] Search-index text of the Coalition FAQ said the MyCoalition services were rebranded "StandOut Admissions Network" by their owner; the live FAQ fetched on 2026-11-07 no longer shows this text. [S14]
- Locker: not stated on any current Coalition or Scoir page read (checked 2026-11-07, S14, S15, S19). [假設] The Locker was part of the MyCoalition platform and ended with it; not verified from an official source in this run.

### 2.2 Scoir + Common App integration (2025–26 onward)
- [事實] "Scoir + Common App are integrated for the 2025-26 academic year!" [S16]
- [事實] Students still build and submit in Common App with separate logins; the integration syncs college lists and lets counselors send forms from Scoir. [S20]
- [推論] A Scoir account does not mean an application was submitted through the Coalition route.

## 3. MIT's own application

- [事實] Platform: "The MIT application portal is where you'll manage all required components." No Common App. [S23]
- [事實] Fee $75 or waiver. [S23]
- [事實] Components: first-year form (personal info, essays, activities, academics, recommenders, test scores); Secondary School Report with transcript; two teacher letters; SAT or ACT; English proficiency exam if applicable; interview if available; optional creative portfolio; February Updates & Notes (FUN) form. [S23]
- [事實] Deadlines: EA 1 November / RA 4 January for form, fee, letters, SSR, portfolio; tests by the November (EA) or December (RA) date; aid 30 November / 15 February; decisions mid-December / mid-March. [S23] The page does not name the entry year. [S23]

### 3.1 Essays (2026–27, as labelled by MIT)
- [事實] Four essays, about 100–200 words each: (1) field of study from a drop-down and what led to it; (2) doing something different from what was expected in one's educational journey; (3) how experiences shape the problems one wants to tackle and the hoped-for impact on community; (4) managing an unexpected situation or challenge. [S22]
- [事實] Four short responses, 40–50 words each: what you do for fun; someone you admire; a topic you could talk about for hours; "generalist" or "specialist". [S22]
- [事實] Additional information: one open box; limit not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S22).

### 3.2 Activities and academics
- [事實] "There is only enough space to list four things"; use MIT's form, not a resume. Character limits: not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S22).
- [事實] Self-reported coursework form is for US school systems only; it does not replace the official transcript. [S22]

### 3.3 Recommendations
- [事實] "We require letters of recommendation from two teachers," ideally one math/science and one humanities/social science/language; not a hard rule. [S24]
- [事實] SSR required; counselor letter only if available, without disadvantage; one optional supplemental recommender, "neither expected nor required." [S24]

### 3.4 Portfolios (all via SlideRoom, same deadline as the application round)
- [事實] Maker Portfolio: questionnaire plus up to 25 media items; code projects need a codebase and working demo; no more than 2 minutes of video total; reviewed in SlideRoom by MIT faculty and alumni; MIT is "more interested in your build process than your end results." [S25]
- [事實] Research Supplement: questionnaire, abstract or poster PDF (citation if published), and a letter from the research mentor/PI nominated in SlideRoom; one per applicant. [S25]
- [事實] Music & Theater: teacher letter and performing-arts résumé; musicians two contrasting unedited recordings, 10 minutes combined; other limits by discipline. [S25]
- [事實] Visual Art & Architecture: up to 10 images with title, medium, date, description. [S25]
- [事實] No portfolio for creative writing, essays, or poetry. [S25]

### 3.5 Interview
- [事實] Educational Counselor interview, in person when possible, virtual by agreement; most EA interviews in November, most RA in January; "If we are unable to offer you an interview, it will be waived, and your application will not be adversely affected." [S26]

## 4. Georgetown

- [事實] "Students can apply to Georgetown using either the Georgetown Application or the Common Application." [S27]
- [事實] SAT or ACT required; AP scores highly recommended. [S27] [S28]
- [事實] Deadlines: EA 1 November (decision by 15 December); RD 1 January (decision by 1 April); financial aid 1 February. [S27] Year not stated on the page.
- [事實] Writing supplement: two short essays (250 words each: most-involved activity; interaction with differing viewpoints), Essay 1 (650 words, personal or creative; the Common App personal statement may be used if it fits), Essay 2 (500 words, school-specific: College of Arts & Sciences, McCourt, Earth Commons, Nursing, School of Health, Walsh SFS, McDonough). [S28]
- [事實] Recommendations: counselor and one teacher required; optional second teacher; subject constraints by school (e.g., McDonough: math or social studies teacher). [S28]
- [事實] Midyear report due 10 February. [S28]
- [事實] Alumni interviews are assigned as applications arrive and "students must complete them when assigned"; some international applicants may not be offered one, without negative effect. [S28]
- Application fee: not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S27, S28). The year Georgetown began accepting Common App: not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S27).
- Georgetown Application Part 1 / Part 2 structure: [事實] S27 refers to "Part 1 of the Georgetown Application" as the trigger for interview assignment; the parts are not described further on S28 (checked 2026-11-07).

## 5. ApplyTexas and UT Austin

- [事實] UT Austin freshman page: "Apply using the Common App for summer/fall admission." ApplyTexas appears only as a link label on that page. [S29]
- [事實] UT Austin essays page: freshman short answers go with "either the Common App or ApplyTexas application." [S30]
- [推論] The two UT pages are inconsistent on whether ApplyTexas is still a route for fall 2027 first-year entry. ApplyTexas itself (applytexas.org) returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches on 2026-11-07, so its term list could not be checked.
- [事實] Fee $75; at least one official SAT or ACT (CLT also accepted); up to two optional letters of recommendation; optional resume ("Details about what each activity involved rather than a general description"). [S29]
- [事實] The page uses "resume", not "expanded resume". [S29]
- [事實] Essay: topic of choice, 500–650 words; the Common App personal essay satisfies it. Short answers: two required (first-choice major; the activity you are most proud of), one optional (circumstances affecting academic performance), about 250–300 words / 40 lines each. [S30]
- [事實] Deadlines: Early Action apply by 15 October, materials by 22 October, decisions by 15 January 2027; Regular apply by 1 December, materials by 10 December, decisions by 15 February 2027. [S29]

## Non-obvious findings

1. [事實] The Universal College Application is still open for 2026/2027 but lists a single school (University of Charleston, WV); it was not closed. [S31] [S32]
2. [事實] The Coalition's standalone portal was replaced by Scoir with applications from 1 August 2022, a year earlier than the "2023" often repeated. [S19]
3. [事實] Coalition membership does not equal accepting the Coalition application: Harvard, Olin, and Rice are Coalition members [S17] but their first-year pages name only Common App (and QuestBridge). [S35] [S47] [S52]
4. [事實] Duke appears on Scoir's list of colleges accepting applications through Scoir [S16], yet Duke's own page limits Coalition with Scoir to transfer applicants; first-year uses Common App or QuestBridge. [S50]
5. [事實] The Coalition counts "more than 150" members [S14] while Scoir lists 116 colleges accepting applications as of 20 October 2026. [S16] [推論] Roughly a quarter of members do not take the Coalition application.
6. [事實] Scoir's Common App integration (2025–26 onward) syncs lists and counselor forms; students still submit inside Common App. [S20]
7. [事實] UC accepts SAT/ACT scores only after submission and does not use them; a systemwide testing review reports in February 2027, so fall 2028 requirements are not yet set. [S3] [S4]
8. [事實] UC told counselors in September 2026 that the additional comments box "should NOT be a 5th PIQ. We will not consider that." [S5]
9. [事實] Three schools in the 30 collect the academic record as structured self-report rather than a transcript at application: UC (inside the application) [S3], UW (STARS, after submission, same email as Common App) [S53], UIUC (link emailed after Common App submission) [S46].
10. [事實] UC's 20-entry and 350-character activity limits appear on UCSB pages and worksheets, not on the systemwide "filling out the application" page, which states neither. [S3] [S6] [S7]
11. [事實] MIT's activity form holds four entries, and MIT has no portfolio for creative writing; Maker Portfolio video is capped at 2 minutes total across the portfolio. [S22] [S25]
12. [事實] TOEFL iBT's January 2026 rescaling to 1–6 sets UC's minimum at 4.5, replacing the old 80/120. [S4]
13. [事實] UT Austin's freshman page tells summer/fall applicants to use Common App, while its essays page still says short answers go with "either the Common App or ApplyTexas." [S29] [S30] [推論] One page is likely out of date; confirm with UT before relying on ApplyTexas.
14. [事實] Berkeley's only official description found of invited letters of recommendation is an undated older guide (policy "as of fall 2015", filing window Nov 1–30); current pages are silent. [S11] [S10] [S12]
15. [事實] Georgetown requires SAT or ACT and asks applicants to complete alumni interviews when assigned, while MIT waives the interview without penalty when none is available. [S27] [S28] [S26]
