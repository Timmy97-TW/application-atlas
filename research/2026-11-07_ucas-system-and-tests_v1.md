# F1, F2 — UCAS Undergraduate and UK admissions tests, 2027 entry / UCAS 申請系統與英國入學考試（2027 年入學）

**中文摘要** [事實] 2027 年入學屬 UCAS 2026–27 cycle：2026-05-12 開放填寫，2026-09-01 起可送出，Oxford／Cambridge 與多數 medicine、dentistry、veterinary 課程的 equal-consideration 截止為 2026-10-15 18:00（UK time），其餘多數課程為 **2027-01-13** 18:00（不是 14 日），2027-06-30 18:00 後的申請直接進 Clearing（S1）。報名費 £34.50、最多 5 個 choices、medicine 等最多 4 個（S2）。Personal statement 自 2026 年入學起改為三題、合計 4,000 characters、每題至少 350 characters（S3, S4）。Reference 分三段、合計 4,000 characters（S8）。入學考試方面，ESAT 與 TMUA 由 UAT-UK 經 Pearson 考場辦理，2026-10-12 至 16 日考，費用依考場地點：UK／愛爾蘭 £78、其他地區 £133，成績 2026-11-16 才公布，晚於 10-15 的 UCAS 截止（S27）。Oxford 已停辦 MAT，改用 TMUA（S34），Engineering 改用 ESAT（S35）；UAT-UK 2027 名單把 Oxford Biomedical Sciences 列為須考 ESAT（S32）。Oxford 官網 ox.ac.uk 與 UCAT 官網本次皆拒絕自動讀取（403），相關欄位留空。

**English summary** [事實] 2027 entry is the UCAS 2026–27 cycle. Applications open 12 May 2026, can be sent from 1 September 2026, and the equal-consideration date for Oxford, Cambridge and most medicine, dentistry and veterinary courses is 15 October 2026 at 18:00 UK time. For most other courses it is **13 January 2027** at 18:00, not the 14th. Applications after 30 June 2027 at 18:00 go straight into Clearing (S1). The fee is £34.50 for up to five choices, with at most four in medicine, dentistry and veterinary subjects combined (S2). Since 2026 entry the personal statement is three questions with a combined 4,000 characters and at least 350 characters per answer (S3, S4). The school reference has three sections sharing 4,000 characters (S8). ESAT and TMUA are run by UAT-UK at Pearson test centres between 12 and 16 October 2026. The fee depends on where the test centre is: £78 in the UK and Ireland, £133 elsewhere. Results come out on 16 November 2026, after the 15 October UCAS deadline (S27). Oxford has dropped the MAT for the TMUA (S34) and uses the ESAT for Engineering (S35). UAT-UK's 2027 list makes the ESAT compulsory for Oxford Biomedical Sciences (S32). ox.ac.uk and ucat.ac.uk returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches on 2026-11-07, so cells that depend on them are empty.

### F1.2 Application sections

- [事實] UCAS lists these sections in order: personal details; nationality details; where you live (addresses for the previous three years, used for fee status); contact details; supporting information (EU/EEA/Swiss residence or work); finance and funding; diversity and inclusion (optional for submission); more about you (UK addresses only); education; employment; extra activities; personal statement; references (S2).
- [事實] Choices: up to five, which can be the same or different courses and providers (S2).
- [事實] Medicine, dentistry, veterinary medicine and veterinary science: at most four of the five choices in these subjects combined (S2).
- [事實] Oxford and Cambridge: usually one course at one of the two. The exception is Cambridge graduate medicine (A101), which can be combined with Cambridge medicine (A100) and Oxford graduate medicine (A101) (S2).
- [事實] Application fee for 2027 entry: £34.50 for up to five choices. It is paid once every section is complete and a reference has been added (S2).
- [事實] Employment covers paid work only. Voluntary work does not go here. It asks for company name, address and dates (S2).
- [事實] "Extra activities" covers summer schools, taster courses and similar (S2). [推論] For an applicant with a long record built outside school, this field and personal-statement Question 3 are the two UCAS places where that record can appear. UCAS states no character limit for extra activities (checked 2026-11-07, S2).

### F1.3 Education section for an international (Taiwan) applicant

- [事實] UCAS says to search for the qualification first and, if it is not listed, to type "other" and enter the details by hand (S2).
- [事實] UCAS says: "Always use the actual qualification name and grade on your certificate" (S2). Applicants should not convert grades to a UK equivalent.
- [事實] UCAS can send only some results to providers (it names the IB). Other proof of results may have to go to universities directly, and policies differ by university (S2).
- [事實] English-language test numbers go in the international area of the Education section. For 2027 entry, certificates can be uploaded as supporting documents (S2).
- [事實] Whether UCAS's qualification list includes the Taiwan senior high school diploma, the GSAT (學測) or AP: not stated on the page read (checked 2026-11-07, S2). The list itself sits inside the logged-in Hub, which was not accessed.
- [推論] A Taiwan applicant will probably enter the senior high diploma and the GSAT under "other" with their certificate names, and AP as its own qualification if listed. This is unverified until someone checks the Hub list.
- [事實] Cambridge says that for Taiwan, "the Senior High School Leaving Certificate alone will not be competitive". It suggests A Levels, the IB, or five or more AP tests at score 5 alongside the certificate (S25).
- [事實] Cambridge "will not download or review any additional documents uploaded to UCAS". Transcripts go through My Cambridge Application or the College (S23).

### F1.4 Personal statement (2026 entry onward, so it applies to 2027 entry)

- [事實] UCAS uses three questions from 2026 entry applications onward (S4). The wording is:
  1. "Why do you want to study this course or subject?" (S3, S4)
  2. "How have your qualifications and studies helped you to prepare for this course or subject?" (S3, S4)
  3. "What else have you done to prepare outside of education, and why are these experiences useful?" (S3, S4)
- [事實] The overall limit is 4,000 characters including spaces, shared across the three answers (S3). The adviser toolkit says the questions themselves do not count toward the limit (S4).
- [事實] Each answer must be at least 350 characters (S3). The 350 figure is confirmed.
- [事實] Line limit: not stated on the pages read (checked 2026-11-07, S3, S4, S5).
- [事實] UCAS publishes guidance on using AI tools in the personal statement, linked from its hub (S5). That guide was not read this run. Similarity-detection text was not found on S5 (checked 2026-11-07).
- [事實] UCAS's international tips call Question 3 a good place to explain why you want to study in the UK. They say Question 2 should not repeat grades shown elsewhere and may include a short online university course (S6).
- [事實] Cambridge's My Cambridge Application has its own optional Cambridge-specific statement of 1,200 characters (S23).

### F1.5 Reference

- [事實] One reference is required. The referee writes it online in the reference section. It cannot come from family, friends or a partner (S7).
- [事實] A centre-linked reference has three sections, headed for universities as "Establishment details", "Extenuating circumstances" and "Other supportive information" (S8). The structure is confirmed.
- [事實] The prompts are: a general statement about the school or centre; extenuating circumstances, if any, that affected the applicant's education; and other supportive information specific to the applicant and the courses (S8).
- [事實] The total is 4,000 characters including spaces, headings and line breaks. UCAS advises staying under 3,800 and sets no per-section limit (S8).
- [事實] Sections 2 and 3 can be marked "no information to enter". No bold, italics, underline or bullets are supported (S8).
- [事實] Predicted grades for pending qualifications are entered by the referee (S7).
- [事實] The year this three-section format started: not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S8). The page refers to a "reference template 2024" and a review in the 2025 cycle.
- [事實] Independent applicants can ask a former school to link the application with its buzzword, or ask an employer for an academic-style reference (S7).

### F1.6 Decisions, replies, Extra, Clearing, Adjustment

- [事實] Offers are conditional (depending on results or other requirements) or unconditional (S10).
- [事實] An applicant accepts one firm offer and, if the firm is conditional, can hold one insurance. All other offers must be declined. The applicant cannot swap firm and insurance at results time (S10).
- [事實] Reply deadlines depend on when the last decision arrives: 5 May, 2 June or 21 July 2027 (S10).
- [事實] Extra: open to applicants who used all five choices and hold no offers. It runs from 25 February to 1 July 2027, one course at a time. If no decision comes within 21 days, the applicant may add another choice (S11).
- [事實] Clearing for 2027 entry runs from 2 July to 18 October 2027 (S1). Applicants can release themselves into Clearing with the "decline my place" button (S12).
- [事實] Adjustment: no mention on any UCAS page read (checked 2026-11-07, S1, S9, S12, S13). [推論] Its absence from the 2027 adviser timeline (S9) is consistent with Adjustment no longer being offered. This note does not confirm when or whether UCAS withdrew it, so the cell stays "not stated".

### F1.7 Cambridge-specific steps (2027 entry)

- [事實] Cambridge's steps are: register for any required admissions assessment, submit UCAS, then complete My Cambridge Application (S15).
- [事實] My Cambridge Application is emailed within 48 hours of UCAS submission. Its deadline is 22 October 2026 at 18:00 UK time, and the transcript is due the same day. Mature January applicants have until 20 January 2027 (S16, S23).
- [事實] The form asks for a transcript, school subjects, English test scores, SAT or AP results where required, a photo, funding details for international students, and an optional 1,200-character statement. Most applicants from outside the UK, Ireland, the Channel Islands and the Isle of Man also pay a £60 application fee, with waivers for some countries (S23, S24).
- [事實] COPA (Cambridge Online Preliminary Application): not mentioned on any Cambridge page read (checked 2026-11-07, S15, S22, S23, S24, S25). [推論] My Cambridge Application appears to be the single additional form for international applicants. Whether it formally replaced the SAQ and COPA is not stated on the pages read.
- [事實] Interviews: invitations go out mostly in November. The main interview period is 7–18 December 2026, the winter pool meets in mid to late January 2027, and Cambridge cannot reschedule except in exceptional circumstances (S22).
- [事實] Interview format depends on the College. Many Colleges interview applicants based outside the UK online, and some, such as Pembroke and Trinity, let overseas applicants choose (S22).
- [事實] Applicants interviewed in December hear the outcome on 27 January 2027. Final confirmation comes in August 2027 (S16).

### F2.2 ESAT detail

- [事實] Each ESAT module lasts 40 minutes, is multiple choice and is taken back to back. Mathematics 1 is compulsory. There is no pass mark and no penalty for wrong answers (S18).
- [事實] Module rules come from the course. Cambridge Engineering, Oxford Engineering and Physics, and Imperial Engineering and Physics require Mathematics 1, Mathematics 2 and Physics. Imperial's biology courses require Mathematics 1, Chemistry and Biology. Imperial Design Engineering takes Mathematics 1 and Mathematics 2 only. Cambridge Natural Sciences and Oxford Biomedical Sciences allow Mathematics 1 plus any two others (S32).
- [事實] UAT-UK says that when a candidate applies to several ESAT courses, the courses with compulsory modules decide the selection. Modules "cannot be changed or added after booking", so a change means cancelling and rebooking (S32).
- [事實] Registration works like this: create a UAT-UK account with a name that matches your ID and the same email as your UCAS account, apply for any bursary or access arrangements, then book and pay in the Pearson system (S30).
- [事實] The fee depends on the test centre's location, not on nationality or address (S30).
- [事實] Candidates can sit only once per admissions cycle (S27).
- [事實] There are fixed days for China, Hong Kong and Macau: ESAT on 12–13 October and TMUA on 15–16 October 2026 (S27). Taiwan is not named in that rule (S27). Whether a Pearson centre in Taiwan offers ESAT or TMUA is not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S28, S30). Centres appear only during booking.
- [事實] The bursary is for UK candidates in financial need (S18).

## Non-obvious findings

1. [事實] The January equal-consideration date for 2027 entry is **13 January 2027**, not the 14th (S1, S9).
2. [事實] ESAT and TMUA results come out on 16 November 2026, a month after the 15 October UCAS deadline (S27). [推論] Oxford, Cambridge and Imperial-ESAT applicants choose their five UCAS choices without knowing their score.
3. [事實] The UAT-UK 2027 list sets out Oxford engineering as Engineering Science (H100) plus separately coded Biomedical (H811), Chemical (H800), Civil (H200), Electrical (H620), Information (H630) and Mechanical (H300) Engineering, each with a Foundation Year variant (S32). Oxford Engineering's own FAQ still describes a single degree (S37). [推論] Oxford may be listing new UCAS codes for 2027 entry. This needs checking on ox.ac.uk, which was unreadable this run.
4. [事實] A biology-leaning applicant to Oxford Biomedical Sciences needs the ESAT, though the course is not engineering or physical science (S32). Imperial requires the ESAT (Maths 1, Chemistry, Biology) for Biological Sciences, Biochemistry, Biotechnology and Microbiology, while Imperial Medical Biosciences needs no test (S32, S38).
5. [事實] UCL Mechanical Engineering uses the TARA, not the ESAT, while UCL Electronic & Electrical Engineering uses the ESAT (S32). [推論] An engineering applicant applying to both UCL and Imperial or Oxford may need two different UAT-UK tests.
6. [事實] Cambridge's and UAT-UK's ESAT dates disagree. Cambridge says registration opens 20 July 2026, the access-arrangement deadline is 14 September and the bursary deadline is 21 September (S16, S18). UAT-UK says booking opened 1 June 2026, access arrangements were due 20 July and bursaries 14 September (S27). [推論] Applicants should follow the test owner (UAT-UK). The difference is logged for the maintainer.
7. [事實] For 2027 entry, Cambridge uses only the UCAT cognitive subtest score and sets no minimum (S21).
8. [事實] For 2027 entry, UCAS lets applicants upload English test certificates (S2), but Cambridge will not review any additional documents uploaded to UCAS (S23). [推論] An applicant to Cambridge has to send the same document through two channels.
9. [事實] Fees depend on where the test centre is: ESAT and TMUA cost £78 or £133 (S30), and LNAT £75 or £120 (S40). [推論] An applicant sitting in Taiwan pays the higher fee.
10. [事實] Cambridge says a Taiwan senior high certificate alone will not be competitive (S25). [推論] The qualification route (A Level, IB or AP) has to be planned before G11 ends, which ties into F3.
11. [事實] The reference has its own 4,000-character limit in three fixed sections, and section 3 is the only one about the individual applicant (S8). [推論] Material from a long record outside school can reach the reader through section 3 of the reference as well as Question 3 of the personal statement.
12. [事實] Adjustment does not appear in the 2027 adviser timeline (S9) or on any Clearing page read (S12, S13).
