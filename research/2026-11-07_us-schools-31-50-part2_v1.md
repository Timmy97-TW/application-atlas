# US schools 31–50, part 2 (G2 roster, G3 matrix)

How to read this note:
- Every non-trivial statement carries a tag. `[事實]` means a sourced fact, `[推論]` means an inference drawn from sourced facts, and `[假設]` means an assumption.
- Citations such as `[S1]` point to the Sources table at the end. Every source was accessed on 2026-11-07. Every source in this note is class `official` (the school's or the UC/UNC System's own pages).
- "not stated (checked 2026-11-07, Sx)" means the school's own page was read and does not say. It does not mean "no".
- Quotes are kept at 15 words or fewer. Longer prompts are paraphrased.
- "Fall 2027 entry" = the 2026–27 application cycle.

**Method notes and limits of this run.**
- The web-search budget for this session was exhausted before the run began. Every page here was reached by fetching a school's own admissions URL and following its own navigation links. Some pages a search would have surfaced (for example a school's FAQ on supplement prompts) were therefore not found; those cells say "not stated" with the pages that *were* checked.
- UNC Chapel Hill's admissions site returned HTTP 403 (a "Checking connection" interstitial) to the automated fetcher. Its pages were read instead in an ordinary browser session, with no CAPTCHA involved. URLs are given as they resolved.
- Many deadline pages print dates without a year. Where a page says "fall 2027" or "2026-2027" this note says so; otherwise the date is reported as printed, and assigning it to the 2026–27 cycle is `[推論]` from the access date.
- Supplement prompts that live only inside the Common App (not on the school's own site) were not read in this run. Those cells are marked as gaps.

## 1. UNC Chapel Hill

- **Application system.** [事實] "Apply online through the Common Application." No other system is listed [S1][S2].
- **Plans and deadlines (2026–27).**
  - [事實] Early Action (non-binding): application October 15; supporting materials and test scores November 7; decision by December 20 for NC residents, by February 15 for all others [S1].
  - [事實] Regular Decision: application January 15; supporting materials February 21; decision March 31 [S1].
  - [事實] No ED plan is listed [S1].
  - [事實] International first-year applicants use the same EA/RD dates [S2].
- **Test policy.** [事實] Under the UNC System rule, "for students applying for the 2026-2027 academic year and beyond," applicants with a weighted GPA of 2.8 or above (4.0 scale) are not required to submit a score; below 2.8, an ACT 17+ or SAT 930+ is required [S1][S12]. [事實] Self-reported scores are used; multiple sittings are superscored [S1]. [事實] Applicants without a 4.0-scale weighted GPA but "above a B+ average" are not required to submit [S1].
- **English proficiency (internationals).** [事實] Required for non-citizens/non-permanent residents whose first language is not English. Accepted: TOEFL (code C182), IELTS, Duolingo, Cambridge C1 Advanced, Cambridge C2 Proficiency [S3][S4]. [事實] "Strong applicants typically score" TOEFL 5+ (tests on or after 2026-01-21) or 100+ (earlier), IELTS 7.5+, Duolingo 130+, C1 Advanced 185+; the page says these "are not minimums" [S4]. [事實] Waived automatically for four years living in an English-speaking country or four years at an English-speaking school [S3]. [事實] SAT/ACT do not satisfy the requirement [S4].
- **Need-blind / need-aware (internationals).** [事實] Non-citizens/non-permanent residents "are not eligible to receive need-based financial aid" [S3]. Whether admission is need-blind for them: not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S3, S2).
- **Supplement prompts.** [事實] The "Application Prompts for 2026-2027" page lists only the Common App personal essay, 250–650 words [S10]. UNC-specific short-answer prompts: not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S10, S1, S2). [推論] Either UNC has no school-specific short answers for 2026–27 or they appear only inside the Common App; this needs a check inside the Common App.
- **Teacher letters.** [事實] One required, from a teacher in a core academic area (English, math, science, social science, world language) [S1][S2].
- **Other Recommender.** [事實] Yes: "We're happy to receive an additional letter"; may be emailed if not sent via Common App [S7].
- **Interview.** [事實] Not used: "we don't use interviews as a part of our admissions review" [S8].
- **Out-of-state limit.** [事實] Carolina "reserves more places in our entering classes for students from North Carolina" [S11]. A numeric cap: not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S11, S12, S1).
- **Engineering admission.** [事實] All students begin in the College of Arts and Sciences and apply to Biomedical Engineering or Computer Science "by the end of your second year"; intended-major selections "won't affect your admission in any way" [S5]. → **Later declaration / internal application, not direct admit.**

## 2. University of Wisconsin–Madison

- **Application system.** [事實] Common App or the Universities of Wisconsin Application; use only one [S13].
- **Plans and deadlines.** [事實] Fall Early Action (non-binding): application November 1, materials November 9, decision on or before January 31. Fall Regular Decision: application January 15, materials January 22, decision on or before March 31. All at 11:59 p.m. Pacific [S13][S14]. No ED plan listed [S14]. Years are not printed on the deadlines page [S14].
- **Test policy.** [事實] ACT/SAT optional "through the spring 2028 term"; only the top composite is considered, no superscoring [S13][S16].
- **English proficiency.** [事實] Required for first-year applicants educated in non-English-speaking countries unless English was the language of instruction for all four secondary years. Minimums: DET 115, IELTS 6.5, TOEFL iBT 80 (tests on or before 2026-01-20) or 4.5 (2026-01-21 or later). Scores ≤ 2 years old; not superscored. IB HL English, AP English, ACT, SAT, Cambridge and PTE cannot waive it [S13][S17]. [推論] The "4.5" figure matches the new 1–6 TOEFL band scale used by UC for the same date [S45]; it is not a typo.
- **Need-blind / need-aware.** Not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S17). [事實] F-1/J-1 students submit proof of one year of funding after admission, not with the application [S17].
- **Supplement prompts.** [事實] Two essays: (1) Common App personal essay or, on the UW application, a prompt about experiences/talents/interests brought to campus; (2) for everyone, why UW–Madison and why the chosen major(s). Maximum 650 words each; the page suggests 300–500 [S15].
- **Teacher letters.** [事實] One required, from someone who can speak to academic ability (teacher, counselor, or faculty member); engineering applicants are encouraged to use a math or science teacher [S13][S16].
- **Other Recommender.** [事實] Yes: an optional second letter, e.g. employer, coach, "research mentor," community leader [S13].
- **Interview.** Not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S13, S16).
- **Engineering admission.** [事實] Engineering is a direct-entry program; applicants list it as first-choice major and "will be required to list a major outside of the College of Engineering as their second choice" [S18]. → **Direct admit.**

## Non-obvious findings

1. [事實] UNC's EA deadline is October 15, with supporting materials due November 7 — two weeks before the November 1 used by the other nine schools here [S1]. [推論] An applicant planning UNC EA alongside November 1 deadlines needs the teacher letter requested earlier than for the rest.
2. [事實] UNC's 2026–27 prompts page lists only the Common App essay [S10]. [推論] If UNC has no school-specific short answers this cycle, the activities list and one teacher letter carry most of the non-grade evidence; confirm inside the Common App before relying on this.
3. [事實] UNC says intended-major choices "won't affect your admission in any way"; BME and CS are applied to later [S5]. [事實] UW–Madison, UCSB and (via LEP) Maryland admit to engineering at entry [S18][S52][S40]. [事實] CWRU admits "to the university, not a single area of study" [S30].
4. [事實] UW–Madison requires engineering applicants to list a non-engineering second-choice major [S18], while UCSB "will not consider alternate Engineering major choices" [S52]. The two direct-admit public schools handle the fallback in opposite ways.
5. [事實] Case Western is the only school in this batch whose own page names research abstracts, web links and videos as accepted additional materials, through a portal form [S29].
6. [事實] RPI, an engineering-focused school, says its process "does not require or recommend" a portfolio and accepts portfolios only from Architecture applicants [S56][S58]. [推論] A maker portfolio for RPI has no stated channel other than a résumé or an email to the updates address.
7. [事實] Both UC campuses refuse unsolicited letters and work samples [S48][S53]. [事實] UCSB's College of Creative Studies is the exception: its separate portal takes letters (required for Biology, Chemistry, CS, Physics) and optional supplemental materials, with a January 5, 2027 priority deadline — after the UC filing window closes [S55]. [事實] The same page shows a Physics priority date of "January 21, 2026" next to the fall 2027 deadline [S55]; [推論] this looks stale and should be re-checked.
8. [事實] The new 1–6 TOEFL scale (for tests from January 21, 2026) now appears in published thresholds: UC system and UW–Madison 4.5, CWRU 4.5, Maryland 5, Rochester (recommended) 5, UNC ("strong applicants typically") 5, Northeastern middle-50% 5.0–5.5 [S45][S13][S28][S42][S22][S4][S34].
9. [事實] UNC treats English-test scores as evaluative, "one factor among many," with typical scores that "are not minimums" [S4]; Northeastern publishes ranges and states it has no minimum [S34]; RPI, Maryland, Pomona and CWRU publish minimums [S57][S42][S62][S28].
10. [事實] Rochester states its interview "will be considered equally in our application review process" [S23]; UNC and RPI state they do not interview [S8][S56]; Northeastern accepts only third-party unscripted video interviews [S33].
11. [事實] Pomona requires the ACT Science section from ACT submitters [S60] and is need-aware for internationals [S62]; Rochester states need is "a contributing factor" for internationals [S22].
12. [事實] UW–Madison's optional second letter may come from a "research mentor" [S13]. [推論] For UW–Madison, an outside research record has a stated channel only through that letter, not through an upload.
