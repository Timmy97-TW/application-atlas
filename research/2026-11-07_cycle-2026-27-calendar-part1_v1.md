# Cycle 2026–27 calendar, part 1

摘要（zh）：本筆記整理 15 所美國大學 2026–27 申請季（2027 年秋季入學）的早申/常規截止日、放榜窗口、國際生財務補助截止與 need-blind / need-aware 政策、作品集截止、期中成績單、回覆日、標準化考試政策與面試政策。所有日期均取自各校官方頁面（存取日 2026-11-07）。Michigan 的官方網域（admissions.umich.edu、finaid.umich.edu、smtd.umich.edu、stamps.umich.edu）對自動擷取回傳 HTTP 403，因此 Michigan 全部欄位留空並註明原因，未以推測填補。

Summary (en): This note records, for 15 US universities and Fall 2027 first-year entry (the 2026–27 cycle), the early plan and deadline, regular deadline, decision windows, international financial-aid deadlines and need-blind/need-aware status, portfolio deadlines that differ from the application deadline, mid-year report timing, reply date, 2026–27 testing policy, and interview policy. Every date comes from the school's own pages, accessed 2026-11-07. Michigan's official domains returned HTTP 403 to automated retrieval, so every Michigan cell is left empty with the reason stated.
