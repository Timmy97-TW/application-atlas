## 中文摘要

- 補充材料在實務上走五種管道：SlideRoom（第三方作品集平台，各校自設）、各校自己的 applicant portal（多半在送出申請後才開通，例如 Yale Status Portal、Caltech、Brown、Harvard、JHU、Swarthmore、Rice、Cornell）、Common App 內的欄位（Additional Information、各校 Questions 裡的 URL 欄位）、email（少數學校接受，多數明文拒絕）、以及「不收」（UC Berkeley、UW、UIUC、CMU 非藝術申請者、Duke 非藝術材料）。[事實，見下方各節來源]
- SlideRoom 的檔案上限由平台預設：圖片 <5 MB、影片 <250 MB、音訊 <30 MB、PDF <10 MB；可用 Link to Media 嵌入 YouTube / Vimeo / SoundCloud / Sketchfab 的單一公開項目。數量上限由學校設定，平台說明頁未給上限。[事實 P2]
- 費用不是 SlideRoom 統一收的：是否收費、收多少由學校設定（MIT 每份 $10、Harvard「small fee」）；Swarthmore 自家 portal 免費。[事實 P3, P7, P8, P23]
- 「Additional Information」在 Common App 是唯一可放任意說明與連結的通用欄位，但學校之間對連結態度相反：Olin 明說可放網站連結；Stanford Art Practice 說連結不審；UW 說不要送網站連結。[事實 P18, P14, P25]

## 1. SlideRoom

### 1.1 What it is and where the link appears
- [事實] SlideRoom's applicant help center is operated under the Liaison
  domain; support.slideroom.com returns a 301 redirect to
  help.liaisonedu.com/SlideRoom_Applicant_Help_Center. (P1, checked 2026-11-07)
- [事實] Each school runs its own SlideRoom site under a school subdomain
  (examples seen: mitadmissions.slideroom.com, harvard.slideroom.com,
  brown.slideroom.com, duke.slideroom.com, cornellaap.slideroom.com,
  cornellche.slideroom.com, artsandhumanities-ucsd.slideroom.com). (P7, P8,
  P12, P17, school pages in B2 note; P30)
- [事實] Brown says its SlideRoom is linked from the Common App. (P12)
- [事實] UC San Diego requires the UC application to be completed first, to
  obtain the ID number needed in SlideRoom. (P30)
- [事實] SlideRoom's getting-started page does not say how applicants reach
  SlideRoom from Common App. Not stated (checked 2026-11-07, P4).
- [事實] SlideRoom's submit article mentions Common App only for a transfer
  mismatch case: if Common App is submitted but the SlideRoom submission
  fails, check whether the SlideRoom application is titled "Transfer". (P3)
- [推論] Applicants therefore should expect the SlideRoom link on each
  school's own admissions page or inside that school's Common App section,
  not as a Common App-wide feature; this is consistent with every school
  page above naming its own SlideRoom URL. Only Brown states the Common App
  link explicitly.

### 1.2 Fees
- [事實] "If an application fee applies, the Payment Information page appears
  after you click Submit"; amounts are not listed by SlideRoom. (P3)
- [事實] MIT charges $10 per portfolio, with fee-waiver requests at least
  3 days before the deadline. (P7)
- [事實] Harvard describes a small, waivable fee for its SlideRoom. (P8)
- [事實] Swarthmore says arts applicants can share work samples "free of
  charge" in its own portal (not SlideRoom). (P23)
- [推論] The SlideRoom fee is a school setting, separate from the Common App
  application fee; whether a given school charges is a per-school cell in B2.

### 1.3 File types and size limits (platform defaults)
From P2 ("Working with Media and Your Portfolio"), checked 2026-11-07:

| media | accepted extensions | size limit |
|---|---|---|
| image | .jpg .jpeg .png .gif .tif .tiff .bmp .tga | < 5 MB; images over 1280×1280 at 72 ppi are resized |
| video | .m4v .mov .mp4 .wmv .flv .asf .mpeg .mpg .mkv | < 250 MB |
| audio | .mp3 .wma .ogg .flac | < 30 MB |
| document | .pdf | < 10 MB unless the school's Portfolio step says otherwise |
| 3D model | not stated on P2 (Sketchfab links are accepted, below) | not stated |

- [事實] The help article says "the institution may specify otherwise" about
  formats, and the options in the Add Media window depend on what the
  organization has enabled. (P2)
- [事實] Item count: not stated by SlideRoom (checked 2026-11-07, P2). Counts
  are set per school (MIT Maker: up to 25 media attachments, 2 min video
  total; MIT Visual Art: up to 10 images; CMU Art: 10–20 works; CMU Design:
  10–19 slides). (P7, P29)

### 1.4 URL and video embeds
- [事實] "Link to Media" accepts a URL to a single public item from YouTube,
  Vimeo, SoundCloud, or Sketchfab; not playlists, sets, or collections;
  YouTube videos must allow embedding. (P2)
- [事實] This is the only URL option documented on P2. A general website
  link is not a SlideRoom media type in that article. (P2)

### 1.5 After submission
- [事實] After submitting, the applicant "can no longer edit this
  application"; other changes require the organization to unsubmit. (P3)

### 1.6 Who reviews
- [事實] SlideRoom does not say who reviews; it says the organization "has
  immediate access to your application" once submitted. (P3)
- [事實] Reviewers are named by schools, e.g. MIT Maker Portfolio: a board of
  MIT faculty and alumni with maker experience; MIT Visual Art: professional
  faculty and staff; Duke: a faculty member in the program; CMU CFA: faculty
  in the program; UC San Diego: department faculty who share evaluations with
  admissions. (P7, P17, P29, P30)

## 2. Other third-party portfolio platforms
- [事實] CMU School of Drama and School of Music use Acceptd, with a separate
  application in addition to Common App, due December 1. (P29)
- [事實] Duke, Rice, and Swarthmore name InitialView products (Glimpse video,
  VIVA) as optional video channels; CMU names VIVA for engineering and
  computer science applicants to present a research or special project. (P17,
  P20, P23, P28)
- [推論] A "portal" for a portfolio is therefore not one system: SlideRoom,
  Acceptd, InitialView, and school-built portals coexist, sometimes at one
  school (CMU uses SlideRoom and Acceptd).

## 5. URL fields inside applications
- [事實] Harvard's supplement asks, for honors listed, to "provide a link to
  the honor/award if available." (P9)
- [事實] Brown: a YouTube or online-presence link goes in the Brown University
  Questions section of the Common App. (P12)
- [事實] CMU: a Schoolhouse.world portfolio URL goes in the CMU supplemental
  questions; otherwise CMU does not accept websites. (P28)
- [事實] Penn lists "Upload Link or URL" as a portfolio format but will not
  process links to Google Docs, Box folders, or file-storage systems. (P15,
  P16)
- [事實] Caltech's portfolio accepts "Links and URLs" as a format. (P11)
- [事實] Rice Art: YouTube/Vimeo URLs inside the portfolio are accessible;
  links to websites of multiple works "will not be reviewed." (P20)
- [事實] Stanford Art Practice: "Links and URLs will not be reviewed." (P14)
- [事實] UW: asks applicants not to send supplemental materials including
  "links to websites". (P25)

## 6. Additional Information as a container
- [事實] Common App's first-year overview page does not describe the
  Additional Information section; not stated (checked 2026-11-07, P5).
  Field limits belong to unit A1 and are not asserted here.
- [事實] Schools that name it as the place for extra content: Duke (extra
  information goes in Additional Information) (P17); Olin (host on a
  website/blog and link it there; keep it to one to two pages) (P18); Penn
  ("the activities section or additional information sections") (P16);
  Harvey Mudd (Common App upload option, abstract not full paper) (P19).
- [事實] UC application equivalent, "Additional comments": "This shouldn't be
  an essay, but rather a place to note unusual circumstances." (P27)
- [推論] Because Additional Information is the only field every Common App
  school receives, it is the one channel that reaches schools with no
  supplementary-material channel; whether a link placed there is opened is
  stated by only a few schools (Olin yes; Stanford arts, UW no).

## 中文摘要（本筆記重點）

- [事實] 每所學校都有自己的 SlideRoom 子網域（例如 mitadmissions.slideroom.com、brown.slideroom.com），SlideRoom 說明中心現在掛在 Liaison 網域下。(P1, P7, P8, P12, P17, P30)
- [事實] 明說 SlideRoom 連結就在 Common App 裡的只有 Brown (P12)；SlideRoom 自己的入門頁沒有說明怎麼從 Common App 進入 (P4)。[推論] 申請者應該到各校招生頁或該校的 Common App 區塊找連結，不要期待 Common App 有統一入口。
- [事實] UC San Diego 要先完成 UC 申請、拿到 ID 號碼，才能在 SlideRoom 上填寫 (P30)。
- [事實] SlideRoom 的平台預設上限：圖片 <5 MB、影片 <250 MB、音訊 <30 MB、PDF <10 MB；件數上限由學校自訂，例如 MIT Maker 最多 25 個媒體、影片合計 2 分鐘，CMU Art 10–20 件。(P2, P7, P29)
- [事實] SlideRoom 的 Link to Media 只收 YouTube、Vimeo、SoundCloud、Sketchfab 上的「單一公開項目」，不收播放清單；一般網站連結不是 SlideRoom 的媒體類型。(P2)
- [事實] SlideRoom 送出後就不能再修改，要改得請學校先退回 (P3)；審閱者由各校自己指定，例如 MIT Maker Portfolio 由有 maker 經驗的教職員與校友組成的小組審閱。(P3, P7)
- [事實] 作品集平台不只一個：CMU 戲劇與音樂學院用 Acceptd，12 月 1 日截止 (P29)；Duke、Rice、Swarthmore 提到 InitialView 的 Glimpse 或 VIVA 影片，CMU 則讓工程與資工申請者用 VIVA 介紹研究或專題。(P17, P20, P23, P28)
- [事實] 很多學校在送出申請後才開通自己的 applicant portal：Yale Status Portal 收 STEM Research Supplement (P10)，Caltech portal 媒體上限 5 GB、文件 75 頁且不限件數 (P11)，Brown portal 有「Academic Paper/Research」類別與 90 秒內的影片自介 (P12)。
- [事實] Harvey Mudd 的 Applicant Hub 寫明「不要附加檔案」(P19)；UIUC 的 myIllini 送出後活動、honors 與寫作答案都會鎖住 (P31)。
- [事實] 接受申請者 email 補充資料的只有少數：Rice (P21)、Olin（不收影片與音樂作品集）(P18)、JHU 只限被 defer 的 ED 申請者 (P22)。Stanford 藝術、Duke、UT Austin、Penn 都明說不收或不審申請者的 email。(P13, P17, P24, P15) [推論] email 是例外，預設應走學校指定的 portal。
- [事實] Common App 裡的網址欄位各校規定不同：Harvard 請申請者為 honors 附上連結 (P9)；Brown 的 YouTube 連結放在 Brown University Questions (P12)；CMU 只收 Schoolhouse.world 的作品網址 (P28)；Penn 不處理 Google Docs、Box 等雲端資料夾連結。(P15, P16)
- [事實] 同樣是連結，學校態度相反：Caltech 作品集接受「Links and URLs」(P11)；Rice 藝術作品集接受 YouTube／Vimeo，但多件作品的網站「不會審閱」(P20)；Stanford Art Practice 說連結不審 (P14)；UW 請申請者不要送網站連結 (P25)。
- [事實] 指定 Additional Information 作為補充內容去處的學校：Duke (P17)、Olin（放網站或部落格連結，控制在一到兩頁）(P18)、Penn (P16)、Harvey Mudd（只收摘要、不收全文）(P19)。UC 的對應欄位 Additional comments「不該寫成一篇文章」。(P27)
- [推論] Additional Information 是所有 Common App 學校都會收到的唯一欄位，因此是唯一能送到「沒有補充材料管道」學校的路；但放在那裡的連結會不會被點開，只有少數學校表態。
- [事實] Common App 的第一年概覽頁沒有提到作品集、SlideRoom 或藝術補充 (P5)；收藝術作品集的學校全都走 SlideRoom、Acceptd 或自家 portal。(P7, P8, P10, P12, P13, P17, P20, P29, P30)
- [假設] Common App 已不再有自己的藝術補充表格；這與上述頁面一致，但沒有任何一頁明說。
- 缺口：Slate 的 Status Portal 廠商文件本次連不上 (404)；2026–27 Requirements Grid PDF 的欄位標題沒能讀出 (P6)；Common App 說明中心的 Additional Information、Honors 文章網址未找到。
