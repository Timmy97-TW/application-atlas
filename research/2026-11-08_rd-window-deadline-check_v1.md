# Regular-decision window check (2026-11-08)

## Method

- [事實] Input: the 79 dated events in `site/data/viz-deadlines.js` whose date
  (or, for a window, whose end) falls between 2026-12-08 and 2027-01-08. This
  includes December windows ("mid-December", "December") that the 2026-11-08
  near-term check did not cover because they end after 2026-12-08.
- [事實] Each item was checked against the official URL(s) already cited for it
  (48 distinct pages). Plain fetch first; pages that refused it were opened in
  one built-in browser tab (read only, no sign-in, nothing submitted), and pages
  the browser could not open were read with a plain command-line request.
- [事實] Verdicts: **confirmed**, **changed**, **not on page**, **blocked**.
- [事實] Unless noted, the pages give no time of day or time zone.

## Result

- [事實] 79 items: 78 confirmed, 1 changed, 0 blocked.
- [事實] Changed: Northwestern Bienen School of Music supplement, regular
  decision. The site had 2027-01-02 ("cycle year unconfirmed"). The Bienen page
  now carries a "Fall 2027 First-Year Regular Decision Timeline" with the
  supplement due **December 1** (R45). The same page's "Fall 2027 First-Year
  Early Decision Timeline" puts the ED supplement at **October 15**, not the
  November 1 the site had; this is outside the window but on the same page, so
  it was corrected too. Both are now confirmed for the 2026-27 cycle.
- [事實] MIT creative portfolios (RA, 2027-01-04): the portfolio page gives no
  date of its own; it says portfolios follow "the same deadline as your
  corresponding application cycle". [推論] The RA deadline (January 4, R32)
  therefore applies; kept, counted as confirmed.

## Details worth keeping

- [事實] Times stated: Brown RD "11:59 p.m. applicant's local time"; Harvard RD
  "11:59pm (your local time)"; Stanford RD "11:59 p.m. (in your local
  timezone)"; Georgia Tech "11:59 p.m. in your time zone"; Olin "11:59 PM in the
  applicant's local time zone"; UIUC "11:59 p.m. CT" (the only one in a fixed
  time zone).
- [事實] Years printed on the page: Olin December 15, 2026; JHU December 11,
  2026 / January 2, 2027; Caltech January 4, 2027 and December 31, 2026; Penn
  January 5, 2027; Cornell finaid January 2, 2027; Caltech aid December 15, 2026;
  Northwestern admissions January 4, 2027. All others print month and day only.
- [事實] Penn RD testing: latest SAT December 5, 2026; latest ACT December 12,
  2026 (R23). The site's December window stays; the label now gives both dates.
- [事實] Michigan lists "Early Action/Regular Decision suggested financial aid
  filing date" on December 15 (R20). Added to the site as a suggested date, not
  a deadline.
- [事實] Columbia's dates table carries the caveat that dates "are based on
  typical admissions cycles and specific dates may vary"; Columbia emails each
  applicant the actual dates.
- [事實] Caltech's deadlines page says regular-decision applicants have until
  January 10 for some materials, and in one place prints "May 1, 2026" where the
  rest of the page uses 2027 [推論: likely a typo]. Neither was changed on the
  site; the January 10 wording was summarised, not quoted, so it is a lead for
  the next check rather than a new row.
- [事實] Johns Hopkins and Carnegie Mellon frame their December / January 3 test
  dates as preferences ("we encourage", "preferred"), not hard deadlines; the
  site labels already say so.
