## Non-obvious findings

1. **Northwestern dropped the required personal essay for 2026–27.**
   - [事實] The required writing is one 300-word supplement. The Common App personal essay is optional but still reviewed if sent [S18][S19].
   - [推論] For an applicant with a strong personal essay already written for other schools, Northwestern is the one school in this batch where leaving it out is an explicit, official option.
2. **Four schools in this batch refuse recommendation letters outright, and one makes them optional.**
   - [事實] UIUC, UW, and UCLA say they do not accept letters [S3][S42][S34].
   - [事實] UT Austin makes letters optional, caps them at two, and encourages letters from outside the high school [S38].
   - [推論] For an applicant whose strongest evidence comes from an outside mentor, these four schools offer either no channel or one that competes with the teacher letters for two slots.
3. **Berkeley's letter-by-invitation policy cannot be found on its current site.**
   - [事實] The freshman requirements, checklist, FAQ, and counselor pages do not mention it, and the site's own search returns nothing for "recommendation" [S28][S29][S25][S30].
   - [事實] Berkeley states that it does not accept supplemental materials [S25][S52].
   - [推論] Either the policy has been withdrawn or it has moved off the admissions site. Re-verify before any site cell says "invited letters". Until then the cell stays "not stated".
4. **Harvey Mudd's teacher letters are not "math and science".**
   - [事實] One letter must be STEM and the other humanities, social sciences, or arts. Only students with STEM-only schedules can request a second STEM letter [S12].
   - [事實] The counselor letter is "when available" [S10][S12].
5. **Where research evidence is accepted differs sharply across this batch.**
   - [事實] Harvey Mudd: an abstract via Additional Information, no full papers [S12].
   - [事實] Johns Hopkins: abstracts via the portal after submission [S14].
   - [事實] Swarthmore: a paper, presentation, research project, or video of something built [S49].
   - [事實] Duke: "does not accept … research abstracts" [S16].
   - [事實] UIUC and Berkeley: no unsolicited materials [S3][S25].
6. **Choosing a major is an admission decision at the public universities.**
   - [事實] UCLA reviews one major only [S33]. Berkeley guarantees review of the primary major only [S25].
   - [事實] UCSD may admit a rejected selective-major applicant to the alternate major if space allows [S37].
   - [事實] UW Allen School applicants not admitted directly are "unlikely" to get in later [S43].
   - [事實] Berkeley L&S guarantees a high-demand major only if it was the primary major on the application [S27].
   - [推論] For engineering or CS applicants at these schools, the first-choice major field carries more weight in the decision than any essay.
7. **Olin's interview is a required, scheduled event, not a courtesy.**
   - [事實] Participation in Candidates' Weekend is required to stay in consideration. Applicants outside the contiguous US interview virtually [S8].
   - [推論] An applicant in Taiwan should plan for a late-February or early-March virtual interview in Taiwan time.
8. **Interviews are disappearing in this batch.**
   - [事實] Johns Hopkins offers none [S14]. Northwestern "no longer offers alumni interviews" [S18]. UCLA, Berkeley, and UW offer none [S34][S26][S42].
   - [事實] Several schools route internationals to third-party video instead: InitialView at Duke, Northwestern, Rice, Berkeley, and Swarthmore [S16][S18][S23][S26][S51].
   - [事實] Swarthmore makes one of three items mandatory for students at non-US schools [S49].
9. **Stated prerequisites vary: Harvey Mudd and Olin require them, Duke requires calculus, Rice requires chemistry and physics, and others only recommend.**
   - [事實] Harvey Mudd requires calculus, chemistry, and physics [S10]. Olin requires calculus and physics [S7]. Duke Pratt requires calculus [S16].
   - [事實] Rice natural sciences and engineering require chemistry and physics, but not calculus [S22].
   - [事實] Northwestern McCormick only *recommends* [S21]. UIUC Grainger sets a 3.5-year math floor [S4].
10. **The Rice Box is not evaluated.**
    - [事實] Rice says the image "is not used for evaluative purposes" [S23].
11. **UT Austin's international fee differs from its freshman page figure.**
    - [事實] UT lists $90 on the international page [S40] and $75 on the freshman page [S38].
    - [推論] The international figure is the one that applies to a non-US applicant. Confirm in the application itself.
