# January window check (2026-11-08)

## Method

- [事實] Input: the 40 dated events in `site/data/viz-deadlines.js` whose date
  (or window end) falls after 2027-01-08 and on or before 2027-02-08: late
  regular-decision materials, aid documents, early-round replies and decisions.
- [事實] Each was checked against the official URL(s) already cited for it.
  Plain fetch first; pages that refused it were read with a plain command-line
  request (Purdue, Princeton, Northwestern) or one built-in browser tab, read
  only (Columbia).
- [事實] Unless noted, the pages give no time of day or time zone.

## Result

- [事實] 40 items: 40 confirmed, 0 changed, 0 blocked. Every item was confirmed
  on at least one of its cited pages. Three secondary citations no longer carry
  the date (Harvard's first-year page for aid, J19; Georgia Tech design's
  portfolio guidance, J21) or refused reading (one Purdue page, J11); the
  primary page for each item still states it.
- [事實] Added: Caltech regular decision, "you will have until January 10 to
  submit all required and supplemental materials" (J29). The site had only the
  January 4 application date for Caltech RD.

## Details worth keeping

- [事實] Years printed: Olin January 15, 2027 (aid) and "Late January 2027"
  (candidate status); Johns Hopkins January 15, 2027 (ED II / RD aid and ED I
  reply); UT Austin January 15, 2027; Brown aid February 1, 2027; Northwestern
  RD aid February 1, 2027.
- [事實] Fixed time zones: UIUC "11:59 p.m. CT"; Georgia Tech "11:59 p.m. in
  your time zone".
- [事實] Caltech's deadlines page prints "until May 1, 2026" for regular-decision
  admits to reply, while its early-action section and FAQ say May 1, 2027.
  [推論] A stale year on the page; the site already uses May 1, 2027.
- [事實] Northwestern's ED enrolment deposit is due February 1 and Harvey Mudd
  gives February 6 as a document cutoff for RD; Johns Hopkins' ED II reply-by
  date is February 26, 2027. Noted for the next window check.
- [事實] UW's no-aid statement for international undergraduates is on its
  costs page, not its dates page; the site row cites both.
