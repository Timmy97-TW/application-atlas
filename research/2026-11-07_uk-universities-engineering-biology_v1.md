# UK universities: engineering and biology courses (2027 entry)

## Scope and method / 範圍與方法

**EN.** Ten universities: one engineering course and one biology course at each (St Andrews has no engineering degree). For each course the note records the UCAS code, the deadline, the admissions test, the interview, any extra form or written work, the typical A-level and IB offers, the English-language level, and what the university says about (a) US-style qualifications (AP, SAT, ACT) and (b) Taiwan qualifications (Senior High School Diploma; GSAT 學測; Advanced Subjects Test 分科測驗). Every value comes from an official university or UCAS page read on 2026-11-07, unless a cell says otherwise.

**Limits of this run.** [事實] www.ox.ac.uk and www.ucl.ac.uk returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool on 2026-11-07 (S11, S17), so the Oxford central course pages, Oxford's international-qualifications page, and all UCL pages could not be read. Oxford values below come from the Oxford department sites (S8–S10) only. [事實] The web-search budget for this run was used up early, so pages were reached by following links from official pages, not by search. Gaps are marked `not stated` or `not verified` with the URL. Nothing was filled in from memory.

**中文。** 十所大學，每校一個工程科系與一個生物科系（St Andrews 沒有工程學位）。每個科系記錄：UCAS code、截止日、入學考試、面試、額外表格或書面作品、A-level 與 IB 的 typical offer、英文門檻，以及學校對（a）美式成績（AP、SAT、ACT）與（b）台灣學歷（高中畢業證書、學測 GSAT、分科測驗 AST）的說法。除非儲存格另有註明，所有數值皆來自 2026-11-07 讀取的大學或 UCAS 官方頁面。

**本次限制。** [事實] 2026-11-07 抓取 www.ox.ac.uk 與 www.ucl.ac.uk 時皆回傳 HTTP 403（S11、S17），因此無法讀取 Oxford 中央科系頁、Oxford 國際學歷頁，以及所有 UCL 頁面。以下 Oxford 資料僅來自系所網站（S8–S10）。[事實] 本次執行的網路搜尋額度很早就用完，頁面都是從官方頁面的連結一路點進去取得。缺漏處標為 `not stated` 或 `not verified` 並附 URL，沒有任何一格憑記憶填寫。

## Shared facts / 共通事實

- [事實] UCAS 2027-entry dates: **15 October 2026, 18:00 UK time** is the equal-consideration date for Oxford and Cambridge (and most medicine, dentistry, veterinary courses); **13 January 2027, 18:00 UK time** is the equal-consideration date for most other undergraduate courses. (S50, official)
- [推論] The eight non-Oxbridge universities below fall under the 13 January 2027 date. None of their course pages read for this note gives a course-specific deadline (S12, S13, S18, S25, S31, S35, S39, S45); UCAS's "most undergraduate courses" wording is the basis.
- [事實] UCAS 2027 入學：**2026-10-15 18:00（英國時間）** 是 Oxford、Cambridge（及多數醫學、牙醫、獸醫）的 equal consideration date；**2027-01-13 18:00** 是其他多數大學部課程的 equal consideration date。（S50）
- [推論] 以下八所非牛劍學校適用 2027-01-13。依據是 UCAS「most undergraduate courses」的字面，以及本次讀到的科系頁都沒有寫出個別截止日。

## 1. University of Cambridge

### (a) Engineering — UCAS H100
- [事實] Test: ESAT, modules "Mathematics 1, Mathematics 2 and Physics"; registration deadline 28 Sep 2026; sitting 12–16 Oct 2026. (S1, S5)
- [事實] Minimum offer A*A*A; IB 41–42 with 776 at HL. Requires Maths and Physics; Further Maths to AS/A level if the school offers it. Peterhouse may ask for STEP II grade 2. (S1)
- [事實] Written work: "You won't usually be asked to submit examples of written work." (S1)
- [事實] Interview: "everyone who is made an offer will have been interviewed"; format (online / in person) set by College; 2027-entry main interview period 7–18 Dec 2026. (S4)
- [事實] Extra form: My Cambridge Application, deadline 22 Oct 2026 18:00; may ask for transcript, SAT scores, IELTS scores, photo, a £60 fee for some applicants outside the UK/Ireland, and an optional 1,200-character Cambridge-specific statement. (S7)

### (b) Natural Sciences (Biological) — UCAS BCF0
- [事實] Test: all NatSci applicants take ESAT. The course page does not list the modules (not stated on S2, checked 2026-11-07). (S2)
- [事實] Minimum offer A*A*A; IB 41–42, 776 HL. Biological route: "Mathematics is now a required subject" and "two sciences are now a requirement." (S2)
- [事實] Interview, My Cambridge Application, deadlines: same as (a). (S4, S5, S7)

### Cambridge: US and Taiwan routes / 美式與台灣路徑
- [事實] US: "a minimum of 5 Advanced Placement (AP) Scores at grade 5 in subjects related to the course"; High School Diploma alone not suitable; science courses SAT 1500 (Maths 750+) or ACT 33 (ACT Science 33+); tests within 2 years of matriculation. (S3)
- [事實] Taiwan is listed. Senior High School Leaving Certificate: "alone will not be competitive and are unlikely to be successful"; applicants should also have 5+ AP at 5, or consult shortlisted Colleges. GSAT/AST not mentioned (checked 2026-11-07, S3). (S3)
- [事實] English: IELTS normally 7.5 overall with 7.0 in each component. (S6)
- 中文：[事實] 劍橋國際學歷頁列有台灣，但只寫高中畢業證書「單獨不具競爭力」，需另有 5 科以上 AP 5 分，或洽詢學院；未提學測或分科測驗。美式路徑：5 科相關 AP 5 分；理科 SAT 1500（數學 750+）或 ACT 33。英文 IELTS 7.5，各項 7.0。工程與自然科學都要考 ESAT，且都會面試（拿到 offer 的人全部面試過）。My Cambridge Application 截止 2026-10-22。

## Non-obvious findings / 非顯而易見的發現

1. [事實] None of the eight Taiwan-specific pages read (Cambridge, Imperial, Edinburgh, Manchester, Bristol, Warwick, St Andrews; plus KCL's general page) mentions GSAT (學測) or the Advanced Subjects Test (分科測驗). Each assesses the diploma itself, and all treat it as insufficient for direct entry, or do not cover it. (S3, S15, S22, S27, S29, S33, S37, S46)
   [事實] 本次讀到的台灣相關頁面，沒有一頁提到學測或分科測驗；各校只看高中畢業證書，而且都認定它不足以直接入學，或根本沒有涵蓋。
   [推論] For a Taiwan-based applicant the working routes are AP (plus SAT/ACT where asked), IB, A-levels, or a foundation year. The national exam results do not open a direct route at any of these universities, based on what their pages state.
   [推論] 對台灣申請者而言，可行路徑是 AP（必要時加 SAT/ACT）、IB、A-level 或 foundation；依各校頁面所寫，學測與分科成績在這些學校都不構成直接入學路徑。
2. [事實] Imperial lists the Taiwan Senior High School Graduation Certificate under "Qualifications that are not accepted for entry" (S15). Edinburgh says its own International Foundation Programme "does not currently accept" the Taiwan diploma for direct entry (S22). Two different mechanisms close the same door.
   [事實] 帝國理工把台灣高中畢業證書列為「不接受入學」；愛丁堡則說連自家的 IFP 都「目前不接受」此學歷直接入讀。兩校用不同機制，結果都是此路不通。
3. [事實] Imperial Biological Sciences requires ESAT (Mathematics 1, Chemistry, Biology) for 2027 entry (S13), and Cambridge's Natural Sciences Biological route now requires A-level/HL Mathematics (S2). A biology applicant who dropped maths is excluded from both.
   [事實] 帝國理工生物科學 2027 入學要考 ESAT（數學 1、化學、生物）；劍橋自然科學（生物）現在要求數學。高中沒有修數學的生物申請者，兩校都進不去。
4. [事實] The number of APs at grade 5 asked for differs widely for comparable courses: Cambridge 5 at grade 5 (S3); Imperial 5,5,5,4,4 general, plus Calculus BC and Physics C at 5 for Mechanical (S12, S14); Warwick Engineering 3 APs plus diploma or 4–5 APs without it, with Calc BC 5 and AB not accepted (S38); St Andrews three at 4+ with tests optional (S47).
   [事實] 類似科系要求的 AP 數量與分數差異很大：劍橋 5 科 5 分；帝國理工一般 5,5,5,4,4，機械工程另需 Calculus BC 與 Physics C 5 分；華威工程有文憑 3 科、無文憑 4–5 科，且不收 Calculus AB；St Andrews 3 科 4 分以上，SAT/ACT 可不交。
5. [事實] English thresholds are not uniform inside one university: Imperial Mechanical uses the standard level (6.5/6.0) while Biological Sciences uses the higher level (7.0/6.5) (S12, S13, S16); Manchester EEE asks 7.0 in Writing, above its Biology course (S31, S32).
   [事實] 同一所大學內的英文門檻也不一致：帝國理工機械用 standard（6.5/6.0），生物用 higher（7.0/6.5）；曼徹斯特電機寫作要 7.0，高於生物。
6. [事實] Cambridge's My Cambridge Application (deadline 22 Oct 2026, a week after the UCAS deadline) is where SAT scores and transcripts go, and it carries a £60 fee for some applicants from outside the UK and Ireland (S5, S7). [推論] For a US-curriculum applicant this is a second deadline that sits outside UCAS.
   [事實] 劍橋的 My Cambridge Application 截止日是 2026-10-22（UCAS 截止後一週），SAT 成績與成績單在這裡提交，部分英國、愛爾蘭以外的申請者要付 £60。[推論] 對美式課程申請者來說，這是 UCAS 之外的第二個截止日。
7. [事實] St Andrews accepts the Common Application as well as UCAS; an overseas Common App or direct application counts as one of the five UK choices (S49). It is the only university in this set whose page states a non-UCAS route.
   [事實] St Andrews 除了 UCAS 也接受 Common App；海外申請者用 Common App 或直接申請，仍算五個英國志願之一。本組十校中只有它的頁面寫明非 UCAS 的申請管道。
8. [事實] Warwick's US page says its guidance "applies to 2027 entry only" (S38). [推論] AP/SAT combinations at Warwick should be re-checked each cycle.
   [事實] 華威美式頁註明只適用 2027 入學。[推論] 華威的 AP/SAT 組合每一輪都要重查。
