## 1. MIT

- **Application system.** [事實] MIT's own first-year application (biographical info, essays, activities, academics, recommenders, test scores) [S2]. [事實] The page does not name Common App; MIT is not on it in this source [S2].
- **Fee / waiver.** [事實] $75 or fee waiver [S2]. [事實] "Fee waivers are available for both domestic and international applicants", requested in the application's Fee Waiver Request section [S6]. [事實] MIT states it is need-blind, so a waiver request has no admissions effect [S6].
- **Supplements (2026–27, labelled).** [事實] The essays page is labelled for the 2026–2027 application [S1].
  - [事實] Four essays of about 100–200 words each: (1) field of study chosen from a drop-down list and what led to the interest; (2) "In what ways have you done something different than what was expected in your educational journey?"; (3) how personal and academic experiences shape the problems the applicant wants to tackle with an MIT education and the intended community impact; (4) "How did you manage a situation or challenge that you didn't expect? What did you learn from it?" [S1]
  - [事實] Four short responses of 40–50 words each: what you do for fun; someone you admire and why; a topic you could talk about for hours; "generalist" or "specialist" and why [S1].
  - [事實] An open "additional information" box; no word limit stated [S1].
  - [事實] Activities: four activities on MIT's own form, not a resume [S1]. [事實] Self-reported coursework form is for U.S. school applicants only; international school applicants submit a transcript instead [S1].
- **Recommenders.** [事實] Counselor: the Secondary School Report (SSR) and transcript are required; a counselor letter is optional ("may", "if available"), and an applicant must add a "School Counselor / Secondary School Report" recommender to submit [S3][S2]. [事實] Teachers: two. MIT recommends one math/science and one humanities/social science/language teacher, and states "this exact combination is not a hard requirement" [S3]. [事實] Optional: one supplemental recommender, "neither expected nor required", for "different or additional context"; the page does not name who (mentor, coach, employer) [S3]. [事實] Most applicants and admitted students do not submit one [S3].
- **Interview.** [事實] Offered "whenever possible" by Educational Council alumni volunteers (over 3,500), in person in the home area or virtual if the interviewer agrees; EA interviews typically November, RA January; usually about an hour [S4]. [事實] If none is available, "your application will not be adversely affected" [S4]. [事實] The page does not label it evaluative or informational, and does not address international availability specifically [S4].
- **School/major selection.** [事實] Applicants choose a field of interest in essay 1 from a drop-down; no admission by major is described on the pages read [S1]. [推論] MIT admits to the institute, not to a school.
- **Course prerequisites.** [事實] None required. MIT "do[es]n't have specific science course requirements"; it recommends math through "at least some exposure to the foundations of calculus" and introductory coursework in physics, chemistry and biology "if available" [S5]. [事實] International curriculum applicants are advised to take "the most challenging math and science courses available" [S5].

## 2. Caltech

- **Application system.** [事實] Common App or QuestBridge Application [S9]. [事實] Optional research and portfolio materials are uploaded through Caltech's own "Beaver Breakroom" portal after the application is submitted [S14].
- **Fee / waiver.** [事實] $85 or fee waiver [S9]. [事實] International page: fee waivers are offered "if you are applying for financial aid and the application fee presents a hardship" [S12]. [事實] Caltech is need-aware for international applicants [S12].
- **Supplements (2026–27, labelled "Fall 2027").** [事實] [S8]:
  - Required, STEM Academic Interest: pick one or two areas (no limit stated), then "Why did you choose your proposed area of interest?" — 150–200 words.
  - Required, Scholarly Character: choose 1 of 2 (Collaboration: a time STEM learning was shaped by another person or group; Process: a time your approach to a STEM problem mattered as much as the outcome) — max 200 words.
  - Required, Scientific Drive: choose 2 of 3 (Learning: a STEM "rabbit hole"; Pursuing: a STEM question or project that has held attention over time; Making: something created, tested, repaired, modeled, coded, built or redesigned) — max 200 words each.
  - Required, Fun Question: "What is something you would be excited to do, share, teach, make, start, or contribute as part of the Caltech community?" — 100–150 words.
  - Optional Academic Short Answer on academic preparation, school context or learning experiences — no limit stated.
  - [事實] Fall 2027 applicants must review Caltech's AI guidelines before submitting supplemental essays (linked from [S8]).
- **Recommenders.** [事實] Two teacher letters from the last two years of high school: one STEM ("Calculus (or beyond), Chemistry, Physics, or Biology") and one humanities/social science ("English, History, Government, or Economics"), the latter from a writing-heavy class [S9][S10]. [事實] Exception: applicants with no humanities/social science course in their last two years (e.g. A-level students) may substitute a second STEM letter [S10]. [事實] Counselor: the School Report is completed by the counselor, who "can submit a letter of recommendation in addition to the School Report" [S9]. [事實] Optional additional letters: accepted from "a coach, mentor, or employer" if it gives a significantly different evaluation, or from another teacher/professor in an extra STEM course or any non-required subject; no maximum stated [S10]. [事實] A research-paper submission requires a letter from a supervisor, mentor, adult collaborator or administrator; the applicant submits a form with the recommender's email and Caltech emails the upload link [S10][S9].
- **Interview.** [事實] Not mentioned on the requirements, letters or supplemental-materials pages [S9][S10][S14]. [事實] The international page recommends an optional InitialView conversation and writing sample, "not graded", recorded by the deadline [S12]. [推論] Caltech does not run its own admissions interview; a third-party recording is the only interview-like channel named.
- **School/major selection.** [事實] Applicants name one or two areas of interest in the supplement [S8]. [推論] No admission by option/major is described.
- **Course prerequisites.** [事實] Required: 4 years math including 1 year calculus; 1 year physics; 1 year chemistry; 4 years English; 2 years history/social science. Biology recommended, not required [S11]. [事實] If calculus, chemistry or physics was unavailable, the only accepted substitutes are AP score 5, IB HL/SL score 6–7, or a Schoolhouse.world certification, with the page stating "There will be no exceptions" [S11].
- [事實] Deadlines page (read 2026-11-07): REA application due November 1, 2026, materials November 6, testing by November 30; RD January 4, 2027, materials January 10 [S13].

## Non-obvious findings

1. [事實] Three schools in this set require only **one** teacher letter, not two: Penn [S42], Carnegie Mellon [S67], Georgetown [S63]; Michigan's international checklist also lists one teacher evaluation and makes the counselor letter optional [S85]. Georgia Tech requires none and labels all recommendations optional [S73][S79]. [推論] An applicant planning around "two teachers everywhere" over-prepares for these schools but must still meet the two-teacher rule at the other ten.
2. [事實] The common assumption that Cornell Engineering requires a math teacher **and** a science teacher is not what Cornell's page says: one of the two letters must be from a math, science or computer science teacher; the second may be any subject [S54].
3. [事實] Only Caltech, among the 15, names coaches, mentors and employers as acceptable extra recommenders on its own letters page, and it **requires** a research mentor/supervisor letter if a research paper is submitted [S10]. Penn names supervisor, mentor and coach for its single optional letter [S42]. Columbia welcomes a research mentor letter only alongside a research abstract [S39]. Georgia Tech explicitly refuses letters from anyone other than counselor and teachers [S73]. Carnegie Mellon accepts a third letter but its committees "consider only two" [S67].
4. [事實] Carnegie Mellon's own pages give three different Early Decision application deadlines on 2026-11-07: November 2 (deadlines page) [S69], November 3 (FAQ) [S68], and "the November 1 Early Decision application deadline" (FAQ, plan-switch answer; also November 1 for switching on the deadlines page) [S68][S69]. [推論] An applicant should treat November 1 as the safe date until CMU reconciles the pages.
5. [事實] Caltech's deadlines page (read 2026-11-07) describes "Fall 2026 entry" while giving November 1, 2026 and January 4, 2027 deadlines and a May 1, 2027 reply date; the essays page is labelled Fall 2027 [S13][S8]. [推論] The "Fall 2026" label is stale; the dates match the 2026–27 cycle.
6. [事實] Columbia's 2026–27 question page says "six short answer questions" at 150 words, but lists five 150-word questions plus a 100-word list [S37]. Brown's page says four essays are required for all, while its PLME and BRDD paragraphs refer to "the three essays required of all first-year applicants" [S47].
7. [事實] Princeton remains test-optional for fall 2026 and fall 2027 entry and announces required testing from the 2027–28 cycle [S31]; Columbia is test-optional for Fall 2027 entry and will require SAT/ACT from 2027–28 [S36]. [推論] Applicants in the 2027–28 cycle face a changed testing rule at both schools.
8. [事實] Princeton requires a graded written paper in English, social studies or history and explicitly prefers analytical writing "rather than science research" [S34]. [推論] A STEM-focused applicant needs a humanities paper on hand regardless of intended B.S.E. major.
9. [事實] Interview policies range across all three types in this set: Yale says all interviews are evaluative and invitation-only [S29]; Georgetown requires the interview once assigned and files an evaluative report [S63]; Columbia has no interviews [S36]; Georgia Tech and (by omission) Caltech and Carnegie Mellon run none of their own and point non-native English speakers to paid third-party recordings (InitialView, Vericant) [S74][S12][S70].
10. [事實] Penn [S46] and Carnegie Mellon [S65] both restrict supplementary materials: Penn allows portfolios only for VIC (and transfer Architecture); CMU "doesn't accept supplemental submissions" outside Fine Arts, but offers optional Schoolhouse.world links and, for Engineering/SCS, VIVA project-video assessments [S65]. By contrast Caltech [S14], Columbia [S39], Brown [S50] and Yale [S27] invite research submissions. [推論] Where a multi-year outside project can enter the file differs sharply among these 15 schools; this matters for unit B and the record page.
11. [事實] Georgia Tech charges internationals $85 vs $75 domestic [S72]; for international students, three schools in this set are need-aware or offer no need-based aid (Caltech need-aware [S12]; Cornell need-aware [S53]; Michigan: visa holders not eligible for need-based aid [S85]); Princeton states identical aid policy regardless of citizenship and need-blind admission [S35].
12. [事實] Course rules that are **required**, not recommended, in this set: Caltech (calculus, physics, chemistry, with only three named substitutes and "no exceptions") [S11]; Cornell Duffield Engineering (calculus, physics, chemistry) [S54]; Cornell CALS (biology and chemistry; calculus/physics by major) [S56]; Purdue Engineering (chemistry "expected") and Nursing (chemistry + biology) [S81]. MIT, Stanford, Yale, Princeton and Columbia state theirs as recommendations [S5][S20][S30][S33][S36].
13. [事實] Harvard's 2026–27 supplement states it "does not consider demonstrated interest" [S23]; Georgia Tech states the major listed and "evidence of your interest in this major" are review factors [S76]; Purdue lists "Time of year you apply" and "Space availability in the desired program" as review factors [S81].

## 中文摘要（本筆記重點）

- [事實] 本批 15 校所引頁面都在 2026-11-07 讀取，全部是學校自己的招生網站（official），沒有使用 secondary 來源。admissions.umich.edu 對所有讀取方式都回傳 403，Michigan 的資料只來自工學院自己的招生頁 [S88]。
- [事實] 申請系統：MIT 用自己的申請表，來源頁沒有提到 Common App [S2]；CMU 與 Georgia Tech 只收 Common App [S65a][S71]；Georgetown 可用自家 Georgetown App 或 Common App [S62]；Harvard 還接受郵寄紙本申請 [S21][S22]。
- [事實] 「每校都要兩封老師推薦信」並不成立：Penn [S42]、CMU [S67]、Georgetown [S63] 只要一封；Michigan 國際生 checklist 也只列一封老師評量 [S85]；Georgia Tech 一封都不強制，全部標為選填 [S73][S79]。
- [事實] Cornell 工程的規定是兩封中有一封來自數學、科學或 CS 老師即可，第二封不限科目，不是「數學、科學各一封」[S54]。
- [事實] 額外推薦人的範圍差很多：Caltech 明列教練、mentor、雇主都可以，交研究論文時則「必須」附研究指導者的信 [S10]；Penn 的一封選填信可找 supervisor、mentor、coach [S42]；Georgia Tech 明文拒收老師與 counselor 以外的人 [S73]；CMU 收第三封但委員會「只看兩封」[S67]。
- [事實] 面試三種型態都有：Yale 只限受邀且全部列入評估 [S29]；Georgetown 一旦分派就必須完成，並提交評估報告 [S63]；Columbia 沒有面試 [S36]；Georgia Tech、Caltech、CMU 不自辦面試，而是建議非英語母語者交 InitialView 或 Vericant 等付費第三方錄影 [S74][S12][S70]。
- [事實] Princeton 要求一份英文、社會或歷史課的批改過書面報告，並明說偏好分析性寫作「而非科學研究」[S34]。[推論] 即使申請 B.S.E.，理工取向的申請者手上也要準備一篇人文報告。
- [事實] Princeton 在 fall 2026 與 fall 2027 入學維持 test-optional，2027–28 申請季起恢復要求 [S31]；Columbia 也在 2027–28 起要求 SAT/ACT [S36]。
- [事實] 補充材料的開放程度落差很大：Penn 只在 VIC（與轉學 Architecture）收作品集 [S46]；CMU 除 Fine Arts 外不收補充材料，但提供 Schoolhouse.world 連結與 Engineering/SCS 的 VIVA 專案影片 [S65]；Caltech [S14]、Columbia [S39]、Brown [S50]、Yale [S27] 則主動接受研究類材料。
