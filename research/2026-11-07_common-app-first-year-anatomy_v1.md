## 0. Cycle facts

- [事實] The 2026–27 application launched 2026-08-01 (S4). Applicants may submit to no more than 20 colleges per cycle (S37, S35).
- [事實] A deadline is met by submitting by 11:59 pm on the deadline date in the applicant's own local time zone (S38).
- [事實] Required questions are marked with a red asterisk; others are optional (S28).
- [事實] New in 2026–27: email verification at account creation or email change; existing accounts are not re-verified (S1, S35).
- [事實] Account rollover keeps My Common Application answers (including the personal essay) into the new cycle (S3). The deck lists FERPA release authorization, recommender invitations, and college-specific question responses among items that do not roll over (S1, p.34). ## 4. Testing (student; self-report)

- [事實] Gate question (Required): "In addition to sending official score reports as required by colleges, do you wish to self-report scores or future test dates for… ACT, SAT, SAT Subject, AP, IB, Cambridge, TOEFL, PTE Academic, IELTS, and Duolingo English Test?" (S6).

- [事實] If official scores cannot be sent because of a disruption, Common App points students to self-reporting and recovery@commonapp.net (S43).

### 5a. Responsibilities and circumstances (student; same screen)

- [事實] Required checklist 1: responsibilities taking 4+ hours/week — assisting family with appointments/bank/visa tasks; farm or unpaid family-business work; interpreting/translating; managing household finances; providing transportation; caring for sick/disabled/elderly members; caring for younger members; caring for own child; paid job contributing to household income; Other (describe, 150 chars); None of these (S6, S12, S32).

## 6. Writing — Personal essay (student)

- [事實] Essay field: Long, minimum 250, maximum 650 (S6). The PDF wording: "write an essay of no more than 650 words… 650 words is your limit, not your goal" (S12).
- [事實] Colleges choose whether the essay is required; if not required, the student is given the option at submission to include it or not for that college (S6, S35).
- [事實] 2026–27 prompts are unchanged from 2025–26 (S1, S3). Prompts (S2):
  1. Some students have a background, identity, interest, or talent that is so meaningful they believe their application would be incomplete without it. If this sounds like you, then please share your story.
  2. The lessons we take from obstacles we encounter can be fundamental to later success. Recount a time when you faced a challenge, setback, or failure. How did it affect you, and what did you learn from the experience?
  3. Reflect on a time when you questioned or challenged a belief or idea. What prompted your thinking? What was the outcome?
  4. Reflect on something that someone has done for you that has made you happy or thankful in a surprising way. How has this gratitude affected or motivated you?
  5. Discuss an accomplishment, event, or realization that sparked a period of personal growth and a new understanding of yourself or others.
  6. Describe a topic, idea, or concept you find so engaging that it makes you lose all track of time. Why does it captivate you? What or who do you turn to when you want to learn more?
  7. Share an essay on any topic of your choice. It can be one you've already written, one that responds to a different prompt, or one of your own design.
- [事實] 2025–26 choice shares: topic of choice 28%, adversity 23%, growth 20%, background 18%, curiosity 5%, gratitude 3%, challenging an idea 3% (S3).
- [事實] After the first submission the personal essay can be edited without limit for later submissions; an already-submitted application cannot be changed (S40, S39).

- [推論] Guides that still quote "650 words" for Additional information describe the 2024–25 field, not the current one.

## 8. College-specific questions and writing supplements (student)

- [事實] Each college sets its own deadlines, fees, personal-essay requirement, Courses & Grades, test policy, portfolio, writing supplements, and recommendations (S35).
- [事實] Writing appears in three places: the personal essay, college-specific questions, and a separate writing supplement that some colleges use (sometimes triggered by other answers) (S35).
- [事實] Each college sets its own word counts; the system prompts if a response is outside the limit; some short answers have unlisted limits (S42).
- [事實] New 2026–27: colleges can show or hide their questions using answers from a subset of about 30 My Common Application questions (S1).
- [事實] The data catalog's system fields include a SlideRoom URL field and writing-supplement signature/submit dates (S6). [推論] Portfolio uploads run through a separate system that the application links to; details belong to unit notes on portfolios.

## 9. Recommender system

- [事實] Four recommender types: Counselor, Teacher, Other Recommender, Advisor; advisors track progress and see a PDF preview with permission but submit no forms (S21, S28, S36). Parents sign only the early decision agreement (S35, S20).
- [事實] One email can hold several roles; a student can invite the same person as, e.g., Teacher and Other Recommender (S28, S36).
- [事實] Students complete the FERPA release authorization, then invite recommenders in My Colleges → Recommenders and FERPA; Teachers and Other Recommenders get no invitation until assigned to at least one college, and must be assigned college by college (S35, S24).
- [事實] At schools using an integration partner, counselor and teacher invitations happen in that platform; Other Recommenders and Advisors are always invited inside Common App (S28, S35).
- [事實] "One-and-done": each recommender submits one general form per student, shared with every college the student assigns; the online form warns it "should not [be tailored] for any particular institution" and cannot be modified after submission (S20, S28, S6).
- [事實] Forms may be submitted after the student submits the application (S25).
- [事實] Counts: "Each college determines the number of required and allowed teacher evaluations" and of "other evaluations"; colleges also choose which Other Recommender types count (S20, S21, S22). Platform-wide maximum of teachers or Other Recommenders a student may invite: not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S20, S21, S35).

### 9a. Teacher

- [事實] Invite a teacher "who has taught you an academic subject (for example, English, computer science, math, science, social studies, or world language)" (S13). Subject areas: Computer Science, English, Math, Science, Social Studies, World Language, Other (S20, S6).
- [事實] The teacher profile asks "high school/secondary school teacher" or "college/university instructor" (S13, S6). A college professor invited as Teacher can complete the form and name their college (S23).

### 9b. Other Recommender

- [事實] Types: Arts Teacher, Clergy, Coach, College Access Counselor, Employer, Family Member, Peer, Other (S20, S21, S15, S6).
- [事實] Defined as "individuals you have worked with outside of school" who "provide context around your hobbies, passions, and other responsibilities" (S21); the form says other recommenders "help illustrate a student's interests and pursuits outside of school" (S15).
- [事實] An Other Recommender can be assigned only to colleges that accept at least one Other Recommender type, and only if the invited type is one the college accepts (S22). The type cannot be changed by re-inviting; a change goes through the solutions center (help-center search snippet, article "How do I change an other recommender type?", 2026-11-07; article page did not load).
- Whether an outside research mentor qualifies as an Other Recommender: not stated (checked 2026-11-07, S20, S21, S22, help-center search "research mentor"). [推論] No Common App page lists "research mentor" as a type; such a person would be invited under one of the eight types (most plausibly "Other" or "Employer"), and whether any given college accepts that type is the college's setting (S22). [推論] If the mentor is a college/university instructor who taught the student a course, the Teacher route with the "college/university instructor" flag exists (S23); whether a given college counts that as a teacher letter is not stated by Common App.

## 10. Recommender forms — fields and rating categories

### Teacher evaluation (teacher) — S13, S6

- Ratings, "Compared to other students in their class": Academic achievements; Intellectual promise; Quality of writing; Creative thought; Productive discussion; Faculty respect; Disciplined habits; Maturity; Motivation; Leadership; Integrity; Reaction to setbacks; Concern for others; Self confidence; Initiative; Overall — 16 rows. [事實]
- Scale (8 points): No basis; Below average; Average; Good (above average); Very good (well above average); Excellent (top 10%); Outstanding (top 5%); One of the top few encountered in my career. [事實] (S13, S6)
- Ratings are not required items, and the teacher profile asks "Do you complete applicants' academic ratings on the Common App teacher evaluation?" (Yes/No, required) (S6). [事實]
- Written: how long known and in what context (Long, max 1000); first words that come to mind (Long, max 1000); grade level(s) taught; courses taught incl. level (Long, max 1000); uploaded evaluation, PDF guidance "1000 words or less" (S6, S13). [事實] The college-instructor variant ("Academic evaluation") has the same 16 ratings, a department field (30 chars), and 150-char short answers (S6). [事實]

### School report (counselor) — S14, S6

- [事實] Student section includes the FERPA authorization (see §11).
- [事實] Counselor attaches the official transcript (with courses in progress and legend); a school profile is optional.
- [事實] School profile block: when mid-year grades are available; graduating class size; percentages (2-yr, 4-yr, ethnicity categories, first-generation, US/non-US citizens, free/reduced lunch, financial aid); setting (rural/suburban/urban); counts of AP/IB/Honors/Cambridge courses offered and any per-year cap; block schedule; graduation date; outside-US flag (opens international supplement); required volunteer service.
- [事實] Class rank (exact/decile/quintile/quartile/none, weighting, dates) and GPA (scale, weighting, passing mark, highest in class).
- [事實] Curriculum rigor: "In comparison with other college preparatory students at your school, the student's course selection is" Less than demanding / Average / Demanding / Very demanding / Most demanding / Prefer not to respond; IB, AICE, AP Capstone candidacy flags.
- [事實] Ratings (same 8-point scale): Academic achievements; Extracurricular accomplishments; Personal qualities and character; Overall. Counselor profile asks whether the counselor completes ratings (S6).
- [事實] Community-disruption box, max 500 words; most recent grades on the transcript; "I recommend this student": No basis / With reservation / Fairly strongly / Strongly / Enthusiastically.
- [事實] Homeschool supplement (supervisor invited as counselor; 1000-word fields on experience, grading, outside courses) and International supplement (lower/senior secondary exam results) exist (S20, S6).
- [事實] No question about school discipline or criminal history appears in the 2026–27 common questions or school report fields (text search of S6 for "disciplin"/"criminal" returns only the "Disciplined habits" rating; checked 2026-11-07).

### Counselor recommendation (counselor) — S16, S6

- [事實] How long known and context (100 chars online); first words (150 chars); evaluation by upload or text box (Long, max 1000), PDF guidance "1000 words or less", addressing academic/extracurricular/personal characteristics, context such as family situation or after-school obligations, and "observed problematic behaviors"; or a checkbox "I will not be sending an evaluation" plus up to 250 words. SR must be submitted before CR (S20).

### Mid-year, Optional, Final (counselor) — S17, S18, S19, S20, S28, S1

- [事實] Mid-year: send when first-semester/trimester grades are available; transcript upload; rank/GPA; course-change and evaluation-change yes/no; new 2026–27 text box "Describe the changes or updates" (max 250) in place of or in addition to an upload (S1, S6). No automatic reminders (S28).
- [事實] Optional report: update grades/transcripts, rank, GPA, course changes, evaluation, or corrections; not a substitute for mid-year/final; can be submitted only once per application year (S19, S20).
- [事實] Final report: only after the student has all decisions; only to the college where the student will enroll (or one where they will enroll if admitted from the waitlist); asks if the student graduated as expected; after FR, CR/MR/OR can no longer be submitted (S18, S20).
- [事實] Order: SR first, then MR/OR/FR (S28).

- [事實] Online: "What is your relationship with the applicant?" (20 chars, required); "How long have you known the applicant?" PDF guidance: "1000 words or less". No rating grid.

## 11. FERPA waiver

- [事實] Text on the school report: every school attended "may release all requested records and recommendations"; college staff "may confidentially contact my current and former schools"; options "I waive" / "I DO NOT waive my right to review all recommendations and supporting documents"; the selection "pertains to all colleges" and "cannot be changed after any recommendation or application submission" (S14).
- [事實] Common App: waiving tells colleges the student does not intend to read the letters; "Some recommenders may decline to write a letter for you if you do not waive your rights" (S26).

## 12. Fee waiver (incl. international)

- [事實] Eligibility (one or more): free/reduced lunch; SAT/ACT fee waiver; USDA income guidelines; public assistance; low-income programs (e.g. GEAR UP, TRIO); subsidized housing, foster home, or homeless; ward of the state or orphan; Pell eligibility; a supporting statement from a school official, college access counselor, financial aid officer, or community leader (S27).
- [事實] International applicants "may be eligible"; colleges may charge different fees, accept the waiver for U.S. students only, accept it for no one, or offer their own waiver (S27, S28). New in 2026–27: each fee-charging college shows whether it accepts the Common App waiver for all students, U.S. students only, or no students, with a new student affirmation (S1, S7).
- [事實] The counselor's Fee Waiver form verifies eligibility, but "the FW is not required to waive the fee. The student's indication that they qualify is the only technical requirement" (S20). [事實] A college that does not accept the waiver may ask the student to pay after submission (S28).

## 13. Fraud and AI

- [事實] Fraud includes "intentionally misrepresenting as one's own original work… the substantive content or output of an artificial intelligence platform, technology, or algorithm", plus fabricated recommendations, false transcripts/scores, misrepresented background, false identity or contact information, and circumventing official counselor channels (S33; last update August 2023).
- [事實] Any registered user, member college, or third party can report suspected fraud (S34). Process: written notice, investigation, appeal within 10 business days, review within 14 business days; outcomes may include suspension or termination of the account and disclosure to every college on the student's My Colleges list (S33, S34).
- [事實] The signature certifies that all information "is my own work, factually true, and honestly presented" (S12).
- [推論] Common App's text does not define "substantive"; school-level AI rules are covered in the separate AI-policy note.

## 16. Non-obvious findings

1. [事實] Additional information is 300 words, not 650. The 650-word field ended with 2024–25; 2025–26 and 2026–27 cap it at 300, alongside a separate 250-word Challenges and circumstances answer (S10, S9, S6, S12).
2. [事實] The COVID/community-disruption question was removed in 2025–26 and replaced by "Challenges and circumstances" (S10, S9). Counselors still have a 500-word community-disruption box (S14).
3. [事實] The teacher evaluation grid has 16 rating rows on an 8-point scale whose top value is "One of the top few encountered in my career" (S13). Ratings are optional; the recommender profile asks whether they complete ratings at all (S6).
4. [事實] The counselor rates only four items (academics, extracurricular, personal qualities, overall), plus course rigor on a 5-level scale and a 5-level "I recommend this student" (S14).
5. [事實] Every recommender letter is one document sent to all assigned colleges and cannot be tailored or edited after submission (S20, S6).
6. [事實] A college professor can fill out the Teacher Evaluation by marking "college/university instructor" (S23, S13).
7. [事實] An Other Recommender cannot even be assigned to a college that accepts none of the eight types or not the invited type (S22).
8. [事實] The position/leadership field in Activities is optional in 2026–27 and post-high-school experiences get real start/end dates; the 50/100/150 limits did not change (S1, S7, S6).
9. [事實] Courses & Grades is required by 76 listed entries (one of which is a scholarship foundation); 77 colleges used it in 2025–26 and 38 used the separate STARS system (S30, S29).
10. [事實] The counselor fee-waiver form is not technically needed to waive a fee; the student's own indication is the only technical requirement, though each college now states whether it accepts the waiver for all, U.S.-only, or no students (S20, S1).
11. [事實] Common App's fraud definition covers representing AI output as one's own, and any third party can file a fraud allegation; findings can be sent to every college on the student's list (S33, S34).
12. [事實] Deadlines are 11:59 pm in the applicant's local time zone, not the college's (S38).
13. [事實] The two Responsibilities and circumstances checklists are required for every applicant (S32, S6).
14. [事實] The 2026 application PDF still tells students with "more than 3 siblings" to use Additional information, while the live 2026–27 catalog allows 10 siblings (S12, S6).
15. [事實] The final report goes only to the one college the student will attend (or a waitlist college), and once sent, the counselor can no longer submit the counselor recommendation, mid-year, or optional reports (S20).
