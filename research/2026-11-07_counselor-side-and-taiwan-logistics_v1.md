### 中文摘要

- [事實] Common App 允許沒有「升學輔導老師（counselor）」的學校由校長或其他學校行政人員擔任 counselor 角色；國際學生應邀請「負責成績單的人」[C1][C4]。被邀請者可線上填寫，也可選擇離線（紙本）：由學生列印表格，學校逐校直接寄送 [C1][C3]。
- [事實] 2026–27 School Report 紙本範本要求：班排（Exact / Decile / Quintile / Quartile / None）、GPA 與量尺、及格分數、班上最高 GPA、課程難度五級評等、四項評比（至前 5%、前「few」）、本校 AP/IB/Honors 開課數等 [C6]。International Supplement 另問授課語言、是否以國家「畢業／升級考試」決定升級、學生是否已考，並要求把本校分數對應到美國 A–F [C7]。
- [事實] 30 校中沒有任何一所在本筆記查到的頁面上要求 WES/ECE 等學歷認證機構；Cornell 明說不需要 [U2]，UCSD 明說「不要自行換算，也不要請專業機構換算」[U4]。台灣學測（GSAT）在查過的頁面中未被提及（沿用 2026-11-07_international-applicant-specifics_v1.md 的結論）。
- [事實] SAT 在台灣有考場，台灣考場另收 $24 考場費，加上 $68 報名費與 $43 國際費 [L2]。AP 的「授權考場」清單沒有台灣 [L8]；學校不開 AP 的學生必須自己找願意收外校生的學校 AP coordinator，且要在 11 月中前 [L8][L9]。
- [事實] CSS Profile 第一所 $25、之後每所 $16 [L15]；fee waiver 只給「監護父母住在美國或美國領地」的家庭，海外美國公民也不行 [L16]。
- [推論] 對一位第一次用 Common App 的台灣公立高中輔導老師，最關鍵的三件事是：在八月 rollover 之後才被邀請、英文 school profile 與附原文的正式英譯成績單、以及在 Final Report 之前不要送 Final（Common App 規定須等學生收到所有結果）[C2][C11]。

## 1. Common App: the counselor / recommender side

### 1.1 Who can be "the counselor"

- [事實] "If there is no counselor at your school, the recommendations may be filled out by your principal or other school official." [C1]
- [事實] "Any school official, international or domestic, can be invited to fill out recommendations online by providing an e-mail address for that official". Once invited, the official is prompted for transcript information and "an international school forms supplement form". [C1]
- [事實] "International students should invite the individual responsible for their transcripts as their school counselor." That person is then asked additional questions within the standard School Report (the International Supplement). [C4][C2]
- [推論] At a Taiwan public senior high school this can be any staff member who can issue the official transcript and sign for the school. Common App does not require the title "counselor". Which office that is (guidance, academic affairs, the principal) is a school decision; this note does not assume one.

### 1.2 How the invitation happens, and when

- [事實] For teachers and Other Recommenders, assigning the recommender to at least one college in the Recommenders and FERPA section "also generates the invitation email". The step is repeated per college. [C10]
- [事實] Accounts roll over at the beginning of August. "any recommender invitations will not carry over in August. You should wait to complete those sections until after you rollover your account." [C11]
- [事實] The sample School Report has a FERPA waiver block stating the selection "cannot be changed after any recommendation or application submission". [C6]
- How the counselor (as opposed to teachers) is invited on the applicant side: the counselor-specific invitation steps were not found in the recommender help-center articles read (checked 2026-11-07, [C1][C2][C4][C10]). [假設] It follows the same Recommenders and FERPA flow; not verified.
- [推論] A counselor who received an invitation in grade 11 (before August) will receive a new one after rollover; any work done before August is not carried.

### 1.3 Online Recommender System vs offline (paper)

- [事實] A recommender can opt out of online submission in three ways: the "opt out" link in the invitation email; asking the applicant not to include an email address ("they'll be prompted to print you paper forms"); or contacting the Solutions Center. When a recommender opts out, "each student will be instructed to access hard copies of the recommendation forms from within the online application, and provide them to you for completion and mailing." [C3]
- [事實] If the official submits offline, "you will be required to print these forms and have your official send each set of forms directly to your schools of interest." [C1]
- [事實] The paper School Report says: "If applying via mail, please also provide your school official stamped envelopes addressed to each college", and ends "Please send this form directly to each college admission office. Do not send this form to Common App." [C6] The International Supplement and the Counselor Recommendation carry the same last line [C7][C8].
- [事實] Common App publishes accessible PDFs of the School Report, Homeschool Supplement, International Supplement, Counselor Recommendation, Optional Report, Mid-year Report, Final Report, Teacher Evaluation and Other Recommendation [C5]. The sample-forms article says applicants reach the official forms for offline recommenders "through their accounts in the Recommenders and FERPA section" [C12].
- [事實] Who may use paper: no eligibility restriction is stated in the articles read; any invited recommender can opt out [C3]. Colleges' own rules then apply to mailed or emailed documents:
  - Stanford "prefer[s] online submission through the Common Application (or your school's system partner) whenever possible" and accepts emailed attachments from school personnel at a credentials address given on the page [U1].
  - UT Austin: "We are unable to accept transcripts via email." [U3]
  - UW: do not mail high school transcripts to the Office of Admissions [U5].
  - Rice does not accept emailed transcripts (reused: [U-reuse-Rice]).
- [推論] Paper means one envelope per college, international postage, and no submission status in the student's dashboard. For a counselor with few applicants the online system is likely less work; this is an inference, not a Common App statement.
- [事實] Partner platforms (Naviance, Scoir, Cialfo, BridgeU, MaiaLearning, Unifrog and others) are optional; a school using none "can still submit online through Common App's native Recommendation system" (reused: [C13]).

### 1.4 Transcript and school profile

- [事實] The School Report "Typically includes school profile and any transcripts." [C2] The counselor section of the paper form asks the counselor to "attach the student's current or most recent official transcript, including courses in progress and a transcript legend. Additionally, counselors have the option to attach a school profile." [C6]
- [事實] Transcripts are required to submit the School Report; "School profile is optional" (reused: [C14]). Common App defines a profile as summary information on "student body, curricular offerings, and grading system" (reused: [C15]).
- [事實] Colleges may separate transcripts from the report (ST, MT, OT, FT document types) [C2].
- [事實] Common App text fields accept only the latin1 character set; other characters become unreadable (reused: [C16]). [推論] Chinese characters typed into fields (school name, counselor name, course titles) may arrive garbled; uploaded PDF transcripts are files, not field text. [假設] PDFs keep their Chinese content; not verified.

### 1.5 School Report questions (2026–27 sample PDF) [C6]

[事實] All items below are on the sample School Report:

| Block | Questions |
|---|---|
| School profile | Approximate date mid-year grades are typically available; graduating class size; percentages of the graduating class attending 2-year / 4-year college, by race/ethnicity, first-generation, U.S. citizens, non-US citizens, free or reduced lunch, financial aid; school setting (rural / suburban / urban); number of AP, IB, Honors, Cambridge courses offered and any per-year cap; block schedule (yes/no); graduation date; "Is your school located outside of the United States? If you answer yes, please complete the international supplement"; required volunteer service |
| Class rank | "How do you report class rank?" Exact / Decile / Quintile / Quartile / None; student's rank; how many share it; weighted / unweighted; start and end dates |
| GPA | "Do you report GPA?" (if no, skip the rest); cumulative GPA; GPA scale; start and end dates; weighted / unweighted; school's passing mark; highest grade/GPA in class |
| Curriculum | "In comparison with other college preparatory students at your school, the student's course selection is:" Less than demanding / Average / Demanding / Very demanding / Most demanding / Prefer not to respond; IB Diploma, AICE Diploma, AP Capstone candidate checkboxes |
| Ratings | Academic achievements, extracurricular accomplishments, personal qualities and character, overall — scale: No basis, Below average, Average, Good (above average), Very good (well above average), Excellent (top 10%), Outstanding (top 5%), One of the top few encountered in my career |
| Additional information | Community disruptions (natural disasters, pandemics, protests, teacher strikes, violence, war, other) and changes to grading scales, graduation requirements, instruction, schedules, testing, academic calendar; max 500 words |
| Summary | Most recent grades on the attached transcript (final junior year; 1st/2nd/3rd quarter senior; 1st trimester; 2nd trimester; "2nd quarter/1st semester senior year grades"); "I recommend this student": No basis / With reservation / Fairly strongly / Strongly / Enthusiastically |

[推論] The rating grid asks for percentile judgments (top 10%, top 5%). A Taiwan school that publishes 校排/班排 percentiles has data to support these; a school that does not may tick "No basis". Neither choice is described by Common App as harmful or neutral.

### 1.6 International Supplement (2026–27 sample PDF) [C7]

[事實] Questions:
- "What is the primary language of instruction in your secondary school?"
- "Is promotion within your educational system based upon standard leaving examinations given at the end of lower and/or senior secondary school by a state or national leaving examinations board?" (yes/no)
- "Has the student already taken leaving exams?" (yes/no). If yes to both, attach an official copy of the results; if results are not yet available, "please indicate predicted results".
- "Please indicate the marking or grading scale used in your school and its approximate equivalence to the A–F scale commonly used in the United States": A (Excellent), B (Very good), C (Average), D (Poor), F (Failing).
- [事實] "Not every college requires the international supplement. Check the requirements grid". [C7]

[假設] Whether Taiwan's 學測 (GSAT) counts as a "leaving examination" that governs promotion is a judgment for the school; this note has no Ministry of Education source on how graduation is determined, so it does not answer the question. [推論] The A–F equivalence line is a key to the school's own scale, not a recalculated transcript; colleges that forbid conversion (below, §2.3) refer to the transcript itself.

### 1.7 Counselor Recommendation, Mid-year, Optional and Final reports

- [事實] Counselor Recommendation (CR): required or optional per college; counselors may submit one even if not required; "The School Report must be submitted before the Counselor Recommendation." [C2]
- [事實] The paper CR asks how long and in what context the counselor has known the student, "the first words that come to your mind", and a short evaluation of "1000 words or less" covering academic, extracurricular and personal characteristics, relevant context (family situation, after-school obligations) and "observed problematic behaviors". A checkbox reads "I will not be sending an evaluation for this student." [C8]
- [事實] Mid-year Report (MR): "should be sent when final grades for first semester or trimester are available"; required vs optional is per college [C2]. The paper MR: "Submit this form as soon as mid-year grades are available", with current transcript and legend [C9].
- [事實] Optional Report (OR): updated grades or corrections at any time, "can only be submitted once an application year", not a substitute for MR/FR [C2].
- [事實] Final Report (FR): "should only be submitted after a student has received all admission decisions", sent to the college of enrolment (or a waitlist college the student would attend). After FR, CR, MR and OR can no longer be submitted. [C2]
- [事實] Fee Waiver form: counselor verifies a student's Common App fee-waiver claim; "the FW is not required to waive the fee". [C2]
- [事實] Early Decision Agreement: the ED form a college receives "includes both the counselor and parent/legal guardian signature." [C2]

## 2. What the 30 schools tell international counselors

Most cells below reuse the international-applicant note (2026-11-07_international-applicant-specifics_v1.md), whose URLs are listed in Sources as "reused". New reads for this note: Stanford forms [U1], Cornell transcript FAQ [U2], UT Austin transcripts [U3], UCSD international [U4], UW how-to-apply [U5].

### 2.1 School profile

- [事實] Requested or expected: Harvard ("a school profile (if available)") [U6]; Columbia ("Included with your secondary school report") [U7]; Johns Hopkins (high school profile with counselor materials) [U8]; Georgetown (required high-school item) [U9]; Olin (required, sent by school) [U10]. Recommended: Harvey Mudd for internationals [U11]; Penn "strongly encourage[s]" profiles [U12]. (All reused.)
- [事實] Stanford: a published profile "on its own does not fulfill the School Report form requirement." [U1]
- [事實] UT Austin asks for a profile only from domestic schools that do not rank; not stated for international schools (reused from the international-applicant note, citing [U3]).
- [事實] Contents of a profile: only Common App's definition was found ("student body, curricular offerings, and grading system") (reused [C15]). No school of the 30 was found listing required profile contents for an international school (checked 2026-11-07, pages in §2).

### 2.2 Grading-scale explanation

- [事實] Common App's International Supplement asks for the local scale and its A–F equivalence [C7]; the School Report asks for GPA scale, passing mark and highest GPA in class [C6].
- [事實] Princeton, Penn and Cornell say not to convert or recalculate grades (reused [U13][U14][U2]). UCSD: "Do NOT convert scores yourself or have them converted professionally." [U4]
- [事實] Cornell: the school official sends the translated transcript in the original grading system [U2] (reused detail); CMU: counselor or school official verifies the translation [U15] (reused).

### 2.3 English translations — certified? by whom?

- [事實] Self-translation forbidden: MIT (teacher or professional may translate), Stanford, Harvard (an English teacher may translate if named), Yale ("certified translation") (reused [U16][U17][U6b][U18]).
- [事實] Original plus translation required: Caltech, Harvard, Columbia, Brown, Georgetown, Rice, Duke, Swarthmore (reused) and UT Austin: "complete and official English translations together with the original language records" [U3].
- [事實] UW: national-compulsory-curriculum schools upload "a scanned, unofficial copy of your transcript for grade levels 9 and higher", "in English or in the native language and accompanied by official English translations" [U5].
- [事實] Princeton is the only school found that lets a School Report or recommendation writer complete forms in another language if "officially translated" before submission, by "an official in your school, or a third party"; paid translation not required (reused [U13]).
- [推論] "Certified" and "official" are not defined by any of these pages as a specific credential (e.g. a court or notary). [推論] A translation issued and stamped by the school itself appears to satisfy the "official" wording at most of these schools, but no page says so explicitly except Princeton (school official) and CMU/Cornell (school official verifies/sends). Confirm with each office before relying on it.

### 2.4 Predicted grades and national exam results

- [事實] Predictions used "if available" or expected: MIT, Caltech, Stanford ("Academic results and predictions are required if available") [U1], Harvard (IB/A-level), Princeton, Penn, Brown, Georgetown, CMU, Georgia Tech, Michigan, Rice, UCLA, UCSD (IB), Amherst (reused).
- [事實] National/leaving exams: Yale says international leaving exams do not satisfy its testing requirement; Harvard allows national leaving exam results or predictions only in exceptional cases; Princeton asks for leaving-exam results "If leaving exams are a part of the graduation requirements" (reused [U18b][U6][U13]). UT Austin asks for "copies of official final exams taken at the end of the program, including external exams" [U3].
- [事實] Taiwan's 學測 (GSAT) or 分科測驗 is not named on any page checked (reused conclusion; no new page contradicted it on 2026-11-07). Purdue is the only school of the 30 with a Taiwan-specific transcript section (junior high year 9 plus senior high years 10–11) (reused [U19]).

### 2.5 Credential evaluation services (WES, ECE, etc.)

- [事實] Cornell: "Your transcripts do not need to be evaluated by a credential evaluation service." Cornell evaluates in-house. [U2]
- [事實] UCSD names approved e-transcript vendors "Parchment and CHESICC" as a delivery route, names no evaluation service, and forbids professional conversion [U4].
- [事實] UT Austin's transcript page and UW's how-to-apply page do not mention any credential evaluation service [U3][U5].
- [事實] No school of the 30 was found requiring WES or ECE for first-year applicants on the pages read for this note and the reused note (checked 2026-11-07). [推論] A Taiwan family should not buy a credential evaluation unless a specific college asks for one.

## 3. Testing and aid logistics from Taiwan

### 3.1 SAT

- [事實] 2026–27 SAT weekend dates, "apply to all students—U.S. and international": Oct 3, Nov 7, Dec 5 2026; Mar 6, May 1, Jun 5 2027. Registration deadlines are about two weeks before (e.g. Dec 5 → Nov 20; late/changes Nov 24). Device-loan requests at least 30 days before test day. Deadlines are 11:59 p.m. ET. [L1]
- [事實] Outside the U.S.: registration $68 "plus a $43 international fee"; a $24 test center fee at select locations for Aug 2026–Jun 2027, naming Hong Kong, Macau, Singapore, South Korea and Taiwan; change $34, cancel $34, late cancel $44, late registration $38 ("Fees Through June 2027"). [L2]
- [推論] The Taiwan-specific test center fee implies SAT weekend centers exist in Taiwan; a total of $135 per sitting ($68 + $43 + $24) before late fees.
- [事實] A list of Taiwan centers is not published as a static page; College Board points to an interactive "Find places that offer the SAT" search and requires Bluebook setup before test day [L3]. Specific Taiwan centers: not captured (checked 2026-11-07, [L3]).
- [事實] Score sending: first 4 score reports free if ordered by 9 days after the test date; additional reports $15 each; rush $31 [L2][L4].
- [事實] SAT fee-waiver eligibility for students outside the U.S.: not stated on the pages read (checked 2026-11-07, [L2][L4]; the eligibility page URL tried returned 404).

### 3.2 ACT (non-U.S.)

- [事實] 2026–27 non-U.S. dates: 5 Sep 2026; 23–24 Oct 2026; 11–12 Dec 2026 (register by 13 Nov; late by 27 Nov; scores from 17 Dec); 12–13 Feb 2027; 2–3 Apr 2027; 11–12 Jun 2027; 9–10 Jul 2027. "Non-US test dates are for all countries outside of the US, US territories, and Puerto Rico." Taiwan is not mentioned. [L5]
- [事實] Non-U.S. fees page: ACT $188.50; with science $198.50; with writing $213.50; with science and writing $223.50; late $42; change $49; additional score reports $20; price includes reports to up to four colleges if codes are given at registration [L6]. The page carries notes dated to fall 2025 and no cycle label. The global.act.org home page lists "The ACT Test: $186.50" [L7].
- [事實] Test format (computer vs paper) and a test-center search are not described on these pages (checked 2026-11-07, [L5][L6]).

### 3.3 AP exams when the school does not offer AP

- [事實] 2027 AP Exams: May 3–7 and May 10–14, 2027 [L9b].
- [事實] Students "can't order AP Exams directly"; they must find a school (AP Course Ledger search by country/city) whose AP coordinator accepts outside students; school orders are due mid-November; a school may add a student later at its discretion [L8][L9].
- [事實] Authorized AP test centers exist only in listed places: mainland China, Hong Kong, South Korea, Singapore, Vietnam, Turkey, United Kingdom, Jordan, India [L8]. **Taiwan is not on the list.** [L8]
- [事實] 2027 fees: $129 per exam outside the U.S. (fees at authorized test centers vary); $40 late-order fee for orders between November 14 and March 12; $40 unused/cancelled-exam fee after the November 13 deadline; schools may charge more [L10].
- [推論] For a Taiwan public-school student, AP testing depends on a Taiwan school (typically an international or American school on the Ledger) agreeing to proctor an outsider, or travel to a listed center. No AP score taken in May 2027 reaches RD readers before decisions; AP is a grade-10/11 decision.

### 3.4 English tests in Taiwan

- [事實] TOEFL iBT registration fee for "Taiwan, China": $272 (ETS country selector) [L11]. Other fees: express registration US$49, rescheduling US$69, additional score reports "US$29 each" [L11].
- [事實] Up to four free score reports chosen before the test, editable until 10 p.m. local test-center time the day before; scores valid 2 years; additional reports "a fee of US$25 per institution/agency" on the send-scores page [L12]. The two ETS pages give different additional-report fees (US$29 vs US$25).
- [事實] IELTS Academic can be taken "at a test centre or remotely online" [L13]. Taiwan centres, fees and results timing: not stated on the page read; the test-centre URL tried returned a not-found page (checked 2026-11-07, [L13]).
- [事實] Duolingo English Test (Traditional Chinese page): online, no appointment, taken at home, about 1 hour, results in 2 days, unlimited score sharing [L14]. Price: not stated on the page read (checked 2026-11-07, [L14]). "全球超 6,500 所院校認可" is the vendor's own claim (secondary).
- [事實] Which of the 30 accept or reject DET, and minimum scores, are in the reused international-applicant note (e.g. DET not accepted by Michigan; DET does not satisfy Georgia Tech's USG rule).

### 3.5 CSS Profile

- [事實] "$25 for the initial application to one institution, and $16 for each additional institution"; credit, debit or gift card [L15].
- [事實] Fee waivers are for domestic undergraduates (family AGI up to $100,000, SAT fee waiver, or orphan/ward). "Are U.S. citizens living abroad eligible for CSS Profile fee waivers? No. A dependent student's custodial parents must live in the U.S. or a U.S. territory" [L16][L17]. (PDF © 2021, linked from the live fee-waiver page.)
- [事實] International applicants can enter figures in home currency; submitting implies consent to cross-border data transfer [L18].
- [推論] A Taiwan-resident family pays $25 + $16 × (n−1) with no waiver route through College Board; some colleges waive their own aid-application costs or use their own forms (e.g. Princeton's own PFAA; Penn's portal for tax documents — reused).

In-person alumni programmes (MIT, Harvard, Princeton, Georgetown, Stanford) depend on volunteers near the applicant, which no page quantifies for Taiwan.

## Non-obvious findings

1. [事實] Common App invitations made before August are discarded at rollover ("any recommender invitations will not carry over in August") [C11]. A counselor briefed in grade 11 will receive a fresh invitation in grade 12.
2. [事實] The Final Report "should only be submitted after a student has received all admission decisions", and once it is sent the Counselor Recommendation, Mid-year and Optional Reports are locked [C2]. A first-time counselor who sends FR early cannot then send the Mid-year Report.
3. [事實] The Optional Report can be used once per application year only [C2].
4. [事實] The counselor co-signs every ED agreement [C2]. [推論] An ED application therefore needs the counselor's action before the ED deadline, not only the student's.
5. [事實] The International Supplement asks the counselor to map the school's scale to A–F [C7], while UCSD says "Do NOT convert scores yourself or have them converted professionally" [U4] and Princeton/Penn/Cornell forbid recalculation (reused). [推論] These fit together only if the A–F line is read as a legend, with the transcript left in its original scale.
6. [事實] Cornell states outright that no credential evaluation service is needed [U2]; no school of the 30 was found requiring WES/ECE. [推論] Paying for one is avoidable cost.
7. [事實] Taiwan is absent from College Board's list of authorized AP test centers, while neighbours (Hong Kong, South Korea, Singapore, Vietnam, mainland China) are listed [L8].
8. [事實] Taiwan is named for an extra $24 SAT test-center fee [L2], making a Taiwan SAT sitting $135 before late fees.
9. [事實] CSS Profile fee waivers require custodial parents living in the U.S. or a U.S. territory [L16]; a Taiwan-resident family pays full fees ($25 + $16 each) [L15].
10. [事實] Official fee figures disagree across owner pages: ACT non-U.S. base $188.50 [L6] vs $186.50 [L7]; TOEFL additional score report US$29 [L11] vs US$25 [L12]. Record both; confirm at checkout.
11. [事實] The School Report's ratings grid offers "Excellent (top 10%)", "Outstanding (top 5%)" and "One of the top few encountered in my career" [C6]; it also asks the date mid-year grades are typically available [C6], which is where a non-U.S. calendar is stated to colleges.
12. [事實] Stanford accepts school-emailed credentials [U1]; UT Austin refuses emailed transcripts [U3]; UW says not to mail transcripts at all [U5]. There is no single offline channel that works for every college.

## 中文摘要（本筆記重點）

- [事實] 國際學生應把「負責成績單的人」設為 counselor；學校沒有輔導老師時，可由校長或其他學校行政人員填寫 [C4][C1]。[推論] 在台灣公立高中，這可以是任何能核發正式成績單、代表學校簽名的人，是哪個處室由學校決定。
- [事實] 帳號在八月 rollover，之前發出的推薦人邀請「不會延續」[C11]。高二就被告知的輔導老師，升高三後會收到一封新的邀請 [C11]。
- [事實] 推薦人可以選擇不走線上系統，改由學生列印紙本、學校逐校直接寄出，表格上寫明不要寄給 Common App [C3][C1][C6]。但各校對寄送與 email 的規定不一：Stanford 收學校人員 email 的附件 [U1]，UT Austin 不收 email 成績單 [U3]，UW 說不要郵寄高中成績單 [U5]。沒有一種離線管道對所有學校都行得通。
- [事實] Common App 文字欄位只接受 latin1 字元集，其他字元會變成無法辨識 [C16]。[推論] 在欄位裡打中文（校名、課名）可能會亂碼；上傳的 PDF 成績單是檔案，不受此限。
- [事實] International Supplement 要求 counselor 把本校分數對應到美國 A–F [C7]；但 UCSD 說「不要自行換算，也不要請專業機構換算」[U4]，Princeton、Penn、Cornell 也禁止重新計算。[推論] 兩者要同時成立，A–F 那一行只能當作圖例，成績單本身維持原本的分數制。
- [事實] 本筆記與沿用筆記查過的 30 校頁面，沒有一校要求 WES、ECE 等學歷認證機構；Cornell 明說不需要 [U2]。[推論] 除非某校明確要求，台灣家庭不必花這筆錢。
