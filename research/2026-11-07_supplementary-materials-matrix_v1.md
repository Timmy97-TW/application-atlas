# B2 — School × material matrix; B4 — biology research route

## Method and reading notes
- [事實] Every school was checked by direct fetch of its official admissions
  pages on 2026-11-07. Web search was not available for most of this run
  (the session's search budget was spent), so pages were reached by known or
  linked URLs only. A cell that says "not stated" means the pages listed
  in its source were read and did not say; it does not mean the school has
  no policy elsewhere.
- [事實] Seven schools' admissions sites refused automated fetch on
  2026-11-07: Princeton (certificate rejected), Columbia (403), Purdue
  (certificate rejected), Michigan (403), Northwestern (certificate
  rejected), Williams (403), Amherst (405). Their cells say
  `not verified (fetch blocked 2026-11-07, URL)`. This is a tool failure, not
  a finding about the school, and needs a manual browser check.
- [事實] Quotes are ≤15 words and were captured through a text-extraction
  fetch; spot-check each against the live page before site use.
- Cell format: `accepted | portal | limits | deadline | reviewer | "weight quote" | [source]`.
- 欄位格式：是否接受 | 管道 | 限制 | 截止 | 審閱者 | 學校原話 | [來源]。

## 中文摘要
- 30 校中，23 校讀到官方頁面；7 校（Princeton、Columbia、Purdue、Michigan、Northwestern、Williams、Amherst）網站在 2026-11-07 拒絕自動擷取，格子留空待人工查。[事實]
- 明文設有「研究」管道的學校：MIT（Research Supplement，SlideRoom）、Caltech（Research Papers and Publications，送件後 portal）、Yale（STEM Research Supplement，Status Portal）、Harvard（Applicant Portal Upload Materials）、Brown（Applicant Portal「Academic Paper/Research」）、JHU 與 Swarthmore（portal 可上傳 research abstract）、Harvey Mudd（只收 abstract，不收全文）。[事實 S1, S5, S15, S14, S22, S42, S58, S40]
- 明文拒收研究摘要或一切補充材料的學校：Duke（不收 resume、research abstract、media）、CMU（非藝術申請者不收任何補充材料）、UC Berkeley、UW、UIUC。[事實 S43, S30, S47, S55, S36]
- 明文設 maker／工程作品集的只有 MIT（Maker Portfolio）與 Caltech（STEM portfolio）；Olin 說「歡迎」藝術或工程作品集但不保證審閱。[事實 S1, S6, S38]
- 讀到的 23 校頁面都沒有提到 Regeneron STS 或 ISEF。[事實，各校來源]

## Non-obvious findings
1. [事實] Research has three different shapes across schools: a structured
   form with mentor letter (MIT, Caltech, Yale), a free upload with
   attribution notes (Harvard, Brown, JHU, Swarthmore), or abstract-only
   (Harvey Mudd). Duke names research abstracts as not accepted. (S1, S5,
   S15, S14, S22, S42, S58, S40, S43)
2. [事實] Caltech's research channel is gated on lab affiliation and paper
   status; independent work is redirected to the STEM portfolio. (S5)
3. [事實] Yale says full research papers are "generally the most helpful",
   while Harvey Mudd asks students not to upload entire papers. Same artefact,
   opposite instruction. (S15, S40)
4. [事實] Links are treated in opposite ways: Olin invites a website link in
   Additional Information; Stanford Art Practice and UW say links will not be
   reviewed / not to send them; Penn accepts a URL but not Google Docs or
   Box. (S38, S9, S55, S20)
5. [事實] CMU's only route for an engineering applicant's project is a
   recorded VIVA interview, not an upload. (S30)
6. [事實] Several channels open only after submission (Caltech, Brown,
   Cornell, Swarthmore, JHU), and Caltech sets supplemental deadlines five
   or six days after its application deadlines (Nov 6 vs Nov 1; Jan 10 vs
   Jan 4). (S7, S22, S28, S58, S42)
7. [事實] Maker/engineering portfolios as a named category exist only at MIT
   and Caltech among pages read; MIT's has the tightest video cap (2 minutes
   total). (S1, S6)
8. [推論] For a biology applicant, the schools with a stated research channel
   and those that refuse all supplements are both in the top tier of this
   list, so the research artefact cannot be planned as a single upload; the
   only channel common to all Common App schools is Activities/Honors/
   Additional Information.
9. [事實] No school page read mentions Regeneron STS or ISEF by name. (all)
