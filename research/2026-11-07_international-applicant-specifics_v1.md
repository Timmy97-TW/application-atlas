## 2. Section (a): What a Taiwan public high-school counselor and an international-school counselor can each submit through Common App

### 2.1 What Common App itself provides `[事實]`

**Who can fill the counselor role.**
- If a school has no counselor, "recommendations may be filled out by your principal or other school official." [S121]
- Any school official, "international or domestic," can be invited by email. Once invited, they are prompted for "transcript information, as well as an international school forms supplement form" [S121].
- International students "should invite the individual responsible for their transcripts as their school counselor." That person is then asked the additional questions of the **International Supplement to the School Report** [S127].

**The forms a college can receive.** [S127]
- The School Report is "Mandatory form for all Counselors/persons responsible for documenting a student's academic progress. Typically includes school profile and any transcripts."
- The Counselor Recommendation must come after the School Report.
- Then the Midyear Report, Optional Report (once per year), Final Report and Teacher Evaluations.
- Each teacher submits one general letter for all colleges.

**Transcript and school profile.** Transcripts are required to submit the School Report. The "School profile is optional." [S125] Common App defines a school profile as summary information on "student body, curricular offerings, and grading system" [S126].

**Offline (paper) route.** If the invited official chooses to submit offline, "you will be required to print these forms and have your official send each set of forms directly to your schools of interest." [S121] Sample offline School Report and Teacher Evaluation forms exist. The official forms are reached through the applicant's "Recommenders and FERPA" section [S124]. `[推論]` A paper route means one mailing per college. The colleges' own pages, not Common App, decide whether mail or email from the school is accepted. For example, Rice does not accept emailed transcripts [S87], and Swarthmore does not accept email uploads for aid documents [S96].

**Integrated platforms.** Common App integrates form and transcript submission with 15 partners, including BridgeU, Cialfo, MaiaLearning, Naviance, Scoir and Unifrog. A school that uses none of them can still submit "online through Common App's native Recommendation system." [S120]

**Character set.** Common App's data "only allows the mysql's latin1 character set." Characters outside it "are translated to an unreadable format when saved to the database." [S122] `[推論]` Chinese characters are outside latin1, so Chinese names, school names or course titles typed into Common App fields may arrive unreadable. Romanized entries are the safer choice. This article does not say whether uploaded PDFs are affected. `[假設]` Uploaded PDFs probably keep their content because they are files, not database text. Not verified.

**Non-English recommendations at the Common App level.** Not stated. Searches of the recommender help center for "translate", "translated recommendation" and "in English" returned no Common App policy article on 2026-11-07 [S120–S127 site]. The only hits were member-college answers and the character-set article. Policy on non-English recommendations is set by each college.

### 2.2 College statements on language of recommendations and documents `[事實]`

- **Princeton** is the only school of the 30 found that addresses non-English recommendation forms directly. A teacher or School Report writer who "is not comfortable writing in English ... may complete the forms in another language." The forms must be "officially translated before they are submitted." Translations can come from "an official in your school, or a third party," and paid translations are not required [S23].
- **Princeton's graded written paper** must be written in English: "should not be translated from another language into English." [S23]
- **Self-translation is not allowed** for transcripts and documents at MIT [S3], Stanford [S11], Harvard [S15] and Yale [S21]. Harvard accepts an English teacher as translator if named [S15]. MIT accepts a teacher or professional [S3].
- **Original plus translation** is explicitly required at Caltech [S7], Harvard [S15], Columbia [S29], Brown [S38], Georgetown [S49], Rice [S87], Duke [S82], Swarthmore [S96] and UT Austin [S113]. The same applies to official transcripts after admission at Michigan [S65] and UIUC [S68].
- **Who sends or verifies the translation.** At Cornell the school official sends the translated transcript [S43]. At CMU the counselor or school official verifies the translation [S51].
- Teacher-recommendation language policy at the other 29 schools: not stated on the pages checked (2026-11-07; see each school's row).

## 3. Section (b): Taiwan-specific points

**Official US admissions text about Taiwan.** `[事實]` Only one page among those checked had a Taiwan-specific section. Purdue's international page tells Taiwan applicants to submit:
- the junior high transcript (year 9) and senior high transcripts (years 10–11);
- if graduated, years 10–12 plus the diploma.

The same page says junior-college diploma holders must apply as transfer students [S60].

**Where Taiwan appears on country lists.** `[事實]`
- Taiwan is not on Michigan's English-test exemption country list [S63].
- Taiwan is not an approved English-official country at UIUC [S68][S69].
- Taiwan is not exempt at UT Austin [S112] or UW [S117].
- Purdue's new Indiana-law review list names "China (including Hong Kong and Macau)" but not Taiwan. The list also includes "other countries identified by the U.S. or the state of Indiana" [S60].

**National exams.** `[事實]` Taiwan's national exams (學測 GSAT, 分科測驗) are not named on any page checked.
- Yale states that international leaving exams do not satisfy its testing requirement [S19].
- Harvard allows "national leaving exam results or predictions" only in exceptional cases [S16].
- Princeton asks for leaving-exam results only "If leaving exams are a part of the graduation requirements" [S23].

`[推論]` In Taiwan, 學測 is an admission exam, not a graduation requirement, so Princeton's wording may not cover it. This needs confirmation from Princeton.

**Academic calendar and transcript format.** No official US admissions page checked discusses the Taiwan academic calendar or transcript format. Not stated (checked 2026-11-07: [S23], [S29], [S60], [S69], [S108]). UCSD names transcript services, including CHESICC, and gives rules for Vietnam and China, but none for Taiwan [S108].

`[假設]` The Taiwan school year starts around August–September, with a first semester ending around January. If so, first-semester grade-12 results may not exist by RD deadlines in early January, and the Midyear Report would follow later. This calendar statement has no source in this note and must be sourced, for example from the Ministry of Education, before use.

**學習歷程檔案 (learning portfolio).** Not mentioned on any official US admissions page checked (2026-11-07). The checked pages are every URL in the Sources table. A dedicated search could not be run because the search budget was exhausted. `[推論]` It is a domestic system. Its content can reach a US college only through Common App's own fields (activities, additional information, uploads where a college allows them) or through the school's documents.

## 4. Section (c): Duolingo English Test (DET) acceptance

**Cross-section of the 30 schools.** `[事實]` For 2026-27, the DET is listed as accepted at 26 schools: MIT, Caltech, Yale, Princeton, Columbia, Penn, Brown, Cornell, Georgetown, CMU, Purdue, UIUC, Olin, Harvey Mudd, JHU, Duke, Northwestern, Rice, Williams, Amherst, Swarthmore, the three UC campuses, UT Austin and UW. See each school's row for the source.

**Exceptions.** `[事實]`
- **Michigan:** "The Duolingo English Test (DET) is not accepted." [S63]
- **Georgia Tech:** DET scores "do not satisfy USG requirement" and are used only in holistic review [S53].
- **Stanford and Harvard** require no English test. Harvard says DET, like TOEFL and IELTS, cannot meet its testing requirement [S11][S15].

**Score levels.** `[事實]` Stated DET levels range from 110 (UW minimum [S117]) and 115 (UC system [S99], UT [S112], Purdue [S58]) up to 135 (Columbia minimum [S27], CMU [S51], UCLA competitive [S107]). Williams lists 135–160 as typical [S89].

**Format details.** `[事實]`
- Harvey Mudd accepts "only the 160-point version" [S75].
- Common App asks applicants to report DET "integrated subscores" and allows highest scores from different dates [S123].
- Duke suggests official DET submission because it is free to send [S82]. Northwestern requires official scores [S84].

**Trend over time.** Not established. No official source checked gives a year-by-year count of US institutions accepting DET (checked 2026-11-07). The vendor's own site claims acceptance by more than 6,500 institutions worldwide ("全球超 6,500 所院校認可") [S128, secondary]. That claim is not independently verified and is not specific to the US.

`[推論]` With 26 of 30 schools accepting DET and one (Michigan) refusing it outright, an applicant aiming at Michigan or Georgia Tech needs TOEFL or IELTS regardless of other plans.

## 5. Section (d): Non-obvious findings

1. `[事實]` **Princeton does not use the CSS Profile or IDOC.** It uses its own PFAA, with an EA deadline of Nov 9. Foreign tax returns go in as the original plus a certified translation [S26]. A family preparing only a CSS Profile would miss Princeton's form.
2. `[事實]` **Some schools require financial documents at application from every international applicant, not only aid applicants.**
   - JHU requires a Certification of Finances; without it the application is incomplete [S80].
   - Rice requires an International Student Financial Statement plus bank statements and does not accept tax documents [S87].

   `[推論]` A Taiwan family that has not asked its bank for an English statement before the ED/EA deadline cannot complete these two applications.
3. `[事實]` **Swarthmore gives admitted internationals seven days** after the decision to submit the CSS Profile, Certificate of Finances and bank statements [S96].
4. `[事實]` **The aid request is one-time.** If aid is not requested at application, later requests are not considered at:
   - Caltech [S7]
   - Stanford [S11]
   - Columbia [S31]
   - Penn [S36]
   - Cornell [S45]
   - Harvey Mudd [S76]
   - Rice [S87]
   - Georgetown, which offers no scholarship in later years if none is given in the first year [S50]
5. `[事實]` **Duke states that aid-seeking international applicants are admitted at "usually less than half" the overall rate** [S82]. This is the only school of the 30 that publishes such a comparison.
6. `[事實]` **Columbia lets SAT Reading and Writing ≥700 or ACT English/Reading ≥29 waive the English test** [S27]. Columbia, Princeton and Harvey Mudd are test-optional in 2026-27 but have announced required testing for 2027-28 [S28][S24][S76].
7. `[事實]` **English test windows are narrow at some schools.**
   - Penn accepts only tests taken on or after 2025-04-01 [S32].
   - CMU accepts scores no more than two years old [S51].
   - UCLA requires all tests by the Nov 30 application deadline, earlier than the UC system's December rule [S107][S99].
   - Berkeley requires proficiency by Jan 31 [S103].
   - UW requires tests by Dec 31 [S117].
8. `[事實]` **Penn does not accept IDOC.** Aid documents go through Penn's own portal [S36]. Princeton also uses its own portal [S26].
9. `[事實]` **UW requires a scanned unofficial transcript with an official English translation at application** for students at national-curriculum schools outside the US [S116]. The three UC campuses and UIUC want self-reported grades and no documents until admission [S100][S106][S69]. `[推論]` For a Taiwan public-school applicant, translation cost and timing differ by school, from "before applying" (UT, UW, most private schools) to "only if admitted" (UC, UIUC).
10. `[事實]` **Common App stores field text in latin1** [S122]. `[推論]` Chinese characters typed into the form may arrive unreadable, so entries should be romanized.
11. `[事實]` **Common App lets any school official act as counselor and supports a paper route** [S121]. The **International Supplement to the School Report** is triggered automatically for international students [S127]. Common App itself treats the school profile as optional [S125], while five schools in this set request it (see 2.3).
12. `[事實]` **Olin and Rice require official SAT/ACT scores from applicants at non-US schools if they submit scores**; US-school applicants may self-report [S72][S87].
13. `[事實]` **Northwestern requires official English test scores** ("may not be self-reported") [S84]†. Most others accept self-reported scores until enrolment, for example Cornell [S42], Georgia Tech [S53], UW [S117] and Amherst [S93]†.
14. `[事實]` **Michigan financial documents can be uploaded only once** through Enrollment Connect [S67].
15. `[事實]` **Georgetown now accepts the Common App** in a three-year pilot starting August 2026 for the fall 2027 class, alongside its own application [S46][S47]. Earlier descriptions saying Georgetown does not use Common App are out of date.

## 中文摘要（本筆記重點）

- [事實] 以國際生身分看，need-blind 的只有六校：MIT、Harvard、Yale、Princeton、Brown、Amherst†；need-aware 的有 11 校；CMU、Olin、UIUC、UCLA、UW 完全不給國際生 need-based aid。
- [事實] 2026–27 仍要求 SAT/ACT 的有 12 校（含 MIT、Caltech、Stanford、Harvard、Yale、Penn、Brown、Cornell 等）；Columbia [S28]、Princeton [S24]、Harvey Mudd [S76] 今年 test-optional，但已宣布 2027–28 改回必繳。
- [推論] 對 2026–27 還在高一、高二的台灣學生來說，輪到自己申請時，test-optional 的學校只會更少。
- [事實] Common App 允許「任何學校職員」擔任 counselor 角色，也可以走紙本路線，由學校逐校寄出 [S121]；國際生會自動觸發 International Supplement to the School Report [S127]。
- [事實] Common App 欄位只收 latin1 字元，範圍外的字存進資料庫會變成無法閱讀 [S122]。[推論] 中文校名、姓名、課名最好一律用羅馬拼音填寫。
- [事實] 30 校中只有 Princeton 明文接受非英文的推薦信表格，但送出前必須由學校職員或第三方正式翻譯，不必找付費翻譯 [S23]；其餘學校在已查頁面上都沒有說明。
- [事實] MIT [S3]、Stanford [S11]、Harvard [S15]、Yale [S21] 不接受申請者自己翻譯成績單；Caltech、Harvard、Columbia、Brown、Georgetown、Rice、Duke、Swarthmore、UT Austin 要求原文加譯文一起交。
- [事實] 已查的美國官方頁面中，只有 Purdue 有台灣專段：要交國三與高一、高二成績單，已畢業者交高一到高三加畢業證書；五專畢業要以轉學生身分申請 [S60]。
- [事實] 學測、分科測驗在所有已查頁面上都沒有被點名。[推論] 學測是升學考試，不是畢業條件，Princeton「leaving exam 屬畢業要求才交」的寫法可能不適用，需向 Princeton 確認。
- [事實] 學習歷程檔案在本次查閱的所有美國官方頁面中都沒有出現；這次也因搜尋額度用完而沒能專門搜尋。
- [假設] 台灣學年約 8–9 月開始、第一學期約 1 月結束，若屬實，RD 截止前可能還沒有高三上成績；這點本筆記沒有來源，使用前要先查證。
- [事實] Duolingo English Test 在 30 校中有 26 校接受；Michigan 明文不收 [S63]，Georgia Tech 說 DET 不符合 USG 要求，只做整體審查參考 [S53]。
- [推論] 想申請 Michigan 或 Georgia Tech 的人，不論其他規劃如何，都得準備 TOEFL 或 IELTS。
- [事實] JHU（Certification of Finances）[S80] 與 Rice（International Student Financial Statement 加銀行證明）[S87] 要所有國際生在申請時就交財力文件。[推論] 若沒在 ED/EA 截止前向銀行申請英文存款證明，這兩校的申請無法完成。
- [事實] Princeton 不用 CSS Profile 或 IDOC，而是自己的 PFAA，EA 截止 11 月 9 日 [S26]；Penn 也不收 IDOC [S36]。
- [事實] 助學金只能在申請時提出、之後補申請不受理的學校包括 Caltech、Stanford、Columbia、Penn、Cornell、Harvey Mudd、Rice；Georgetown 第一年沒給獎學金，之後也不會給 [S50]。
- [事實] Duke 是唯一公開比較數字的學校：申請助學金的國際生錄取率「通常不到」整體的一半 [S82]。
- [事實] Georgetown 從 2026 年 8 月起試辦三年接受 Common App，適用 2027 秋季入學 [S46][S47]。
