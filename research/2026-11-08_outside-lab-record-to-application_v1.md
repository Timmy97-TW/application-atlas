# An outside lab record into the application (2026-11-08)

## Method

- [事實] Question: a student who has trained for three years in a molecular-biology
  lab outside school (an independent training programme, not a school course and
  not a university research post) wants that record to reach a US first-year
  application. Which containers can hold it, under what limits, who can vouch for
  it, and what have admissions offices said about outside research in their own
  voice? The programme is not named anywhere in this note.
- [事實] Official pages first. Common App's public PDFs (handouts and counselor
  decks) were downloaded and read in full; the Salesforce-hosted form PDFs that the
  A1 note read on 2026-11-07 (first-year application, Other evaluation, School
  report) returned only a JavaScript postback page to a plain request today, so
  their facts are carried from the A1 note and cited there (A1-S6, A1-S12, A1-S14,
  A1-S15), not re-read. School pages were fetched directly; Columbia refused
  (HTTP 403) and is marked blocked. Non-university bodies (Society for Science,
  bioRxiv, ORCID, NIH, Scoir) are class `secondary` under rule 1 even where they
  are the issuer's own page.
- [事實] Searches for official admissions statements specifically about outside
  research programmes, pay-to-play research or ghost-written papers were run
  against mitadmissions.org, sites.gatech.edu/admission-blog, admissions.yale.edu
  and apply.jhu.edu. The only direct official statements found are listed in
  §B; where nothing was found the cell says so.
- [事實] Nothing in this note is a statement about how any reader weighs a record.
  Every "fit" or "route" is [推論] from the stated field rules.

## A. Containers a lab record can enter (Common App and the 30 schools)

### A1. Student-filled text containers

| Container | Limit (official) | What Common App says it is for | Fits a lab record? | Src |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Activities and experiences | up to 10 entries; position/leadership 50 chars; organization 100 chars; description 150 chars; hours/week and weeks/year | "highlight the activities and experiences that have been the most meaningful to you throughout high school"; "If you need more space to elaborate … you can use the additional information question in the writing section" | [推論] Yes: the training itself as one entry; hours × weeks carry the time; "Organization" takes a generic programme name | L01 |
| Honors | up to 5; title, when received, level of recognition (title 100 chars per A1) | "academic honors you received during high school" | [推論] Only if an external body awarded something (fair placement, poster prize); hours or techniques are not honors | L02, A1-S6 |
| Additional information | 1–300 words (2025–26 onward; 650 in 2024–25) | Yale, in its own voice: "It's not a space for another essay"; "It doesn't have to be well-written prose" (bullets fine); Common App's deck: a place to list college courses not on the transcript | [推論] The one place a structured record (dates, hours, techniques, project, mentor contact) can sit as text | A1-S6, A1-S12, L12, L04 |
| Courses & Grades | only courses with high-school credit, "exactly as they appear on their high school transcript"; college courses without HS credit "do not need to report" | reporting of the transcript, required by 76 colleges only | [事實] An outside programme's courses have no place here unless the high school put them on its transcript | L04 |
| Education → Colleges & Universities | lists colleges that granted credit | dual enrollment and independent college courses | [推論] Not for a non-degree-granting programme | L04 |

- [事實] The Activities handout's own example cuts a 256-character description to
  141 and removes time spent "because that information is reported elsewhere in
  the section" (L01).
- [事實] Yale's officers describe the Additional Information cut from 650 to 300
  words and say "Take that limit as the freedom to not tell us every little detail
  about each of your activities"; "there is no additional information, leave it
  blank" (L12, episode dated 2025-07-31).

### A2. Recommender containers

| Container | Who fills | Limit / rule | Gate | Src |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Other evaluation (Other Recommender) | "individuals you have worked with outside of school"; types: arts teacher, clergy, coach, college access counselor, employer, family member, peer, other | relationship 20 chars; how long known 20 chars; one uploaded letter, PDF guidance "1000 words or less" (A1) | "Each college can choose which types of other recommenders they accept" and how many letters it requires | L03, A1-S15 |
| Teacher evaluation, "college/university instructor" variant | an instructor who taught the student a course | 16 ratings; letter upload | college sets teacher count | A1-S23 |
| School report + Counselor recommendation | school counselor | transcript upload, school profile, ratings, 500-word disruption box; counselor letter ≤1000 words | Common App's guide: counselors give "Context for and rationale behind specific awards and honors" and view the student "within the context of an entire graduating class" | A1-S14, L05 |
| Whether a counselor may attach an outside programme's record or countersign it | — | not stated (checked 2026-11-08, L03, L05, A1-S14) | — | — |

- [事實] "Research mentor" is not one of the eight Other Recommender types (L03).
  [推論] A lab mentor is invited as "employer" or "other", and whether a given
  college accepts that type is that college's own setting (L03).
- [事實] Common App's writing guide assigns awards-and-honors context to the
  counselor, not to the student (L05). [推論] A counselor who has been told about
  an outside award can give it context; nothing says the counselor verifies it.

- [事實] Caltech's gate excludes a programme that is not "a university or
  research-affiliated organization" and work without a paper in preparation; it
  routes such work to the Portfolio, where "Personal notebooks" and "Research
  posters" are listed item types (L06, L08).
- [事實] MIT's and Yale's research forms both take an abstract or poster and both
  bring the mentor's letter in through the same portal, so the letter and the
  artefact arrive together (L09, L11).
- [事實] Harvard is the only one of the 30 whose page asks, in so many words, for
  the applicant's "particular contribution" and a list of collaborators (L14).

## B. Offices in their own voice on outside research, mentors and trust

- [事實] Caltech: "only 42% of admitted students have participated in research
  prior to Caltech"; supplements "are completely optional"; "submit them only if
  they meaningfully add to your application" (L07).
- [事實] Caltech's research letter rule is the most specific authorship check
  found: the research-site letter "must speak to your specific contributions to
  the submitted research" (L06).
- [事實] MIT on summer programmes: "Most summer programs admit all or most students
  who can pay the (often high) tuition"; "spend your summers in the way that best
  suits your personal needs and goals" (L10). This is the only official sentence
  found among the 30 that names paying for a programme; it is about summer
  programmes in general, not research programmes.
- [事實] Yale: "Most successful Yale applicants submit only the required
  application materials"; "these required elements receive the most weight" (L11).
- [事實] Harvard: "Our standard application materials typically give us ample
  information"; supplements "may be reviewed by faculty" "At the discretion of the
  Admissions Committee" (L14).
- [事實] Harvey Mudd asks for an abstract, not the paper; Brown and Johns Hopkins
  name the abstract as the research artefact they take (L16, L13, L15).
- [事實] No official page or podcast among the 30 was found that uses the words
  "pay-to-play", "ghost-written" or "paid research programme" (searches
  2026-11-08 on mitadmissions.org, sites.gatech.edu/admission-blog,
  admissions.yale.edu, apply.jhu.edu). Statements on distrust of paid research
  exist in news and consultant writing only; they are not cited here.
- [事實] The one competition body that does say it in writing is Regeneron STS:
  "entrants AND mentors must disclose participation in paid programs"; "both
  students and recommenders must disclose any prior familial or personal
  relationship"; "Student researchers will also need to obtain permission from
  their mentors before submitting their research"; "only independent, individual
  research is eligible" (L20).
- [推論] Taken together, the offices that accept research all name the same two
  objects, an artefact (paper, abstract or poster) and a mentor who writes about
  the student's own share. None names a training record, a techniques log or a
  notebook as a thing it receives, except Caltech's portfolio item list.

## C. Proofs an outside programme can produce, who vouches, where each enters

Ranked by who can check it. "Verifier" is the party an office could contact or
whose record exists independently of the applicant.

| Rung | Proof | Who vouches | Independent record exists? | Container(s) | Tag | Src |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Applicant's own text: Activities entry (150 chars), Honors line, Additional Information (300 words) | no one; UC requires entries to be "true and verifiable by an independent party" on request | no | Activities, Honors, Additional Information | [事實] limits; [推論] weight | L01, L02, A1-S6, C1-S41 |
| 2 | Mentor's letter | the mentor; reachable at the email the student enters (MIT, Yale, Caltech forms ask for the recommender's email) | the letter itself | Other Recommender (if the college accepts the type); MIT Research Supplement; Yale STEM form; Caltech research letter; Brown/Harvard extra letters outside Common App | [事實] | L03, L06, L09, L11, L13, L14 |
| 3 | Dated, signed, witnessed lab notebook; hours-and-techniques log kept by the programme | the programme and the witness; NIH's own standard: records "Dated", "Signed or assigned to a particular person", entries "periodically witnessed with a signature and date" by someone "not a co-inventor", "a third party should be able to reconstruct the experiment" | yes, but held by the programme | Caltech Portfolio lists "Personal notebooks" (≤75 pages per document); otherwise summarised in Additional Information or described in the mentor letter | [事實] standard; [推論] that any office reads a notebook | L24, L08 |
| 4 | Competition paperwork: ISEF Research Plan written "BEFORE experimentation", steps "to be taken by you" separated from steps "taken by any supervising adult(s)"; Form 2C completed "by the Supervising Adult in the Setting (NOT the Student(s)) after experimentation"; 250-word abstract that "must describe research conducted by the student(s), not by the supervising adult(s)"; STS: 20-page paper, teacher and mentor recommendations, disclosures of paid programmes and relationships | the fair's SRC/IRB and judges; the supervising adult on Form 2C; STS evaluators ("three Ph.D. level scientists" per the STS site) | yes, held by the competition | Honors (placement), Activities, Additional Information; the forms themselves are a model for a programme's own attestation | [事實] | L17, L18, L19, L20 |
| 5 | Poster or talk at a named meeting; preprint with DOI; ORCID record | the meeting's programme; bioRxiv: "Manuscripts posted on bioRxiv receive DOI's", "screened on submission for plagiarism, non-scientific content", "not certified by scientific peer review", "papers cannot be removed", submitter must "confirm you have approval from all co-authors"; ORCID: "Validated – Added by a trusted ORCID member organization" vs "Self asserted – Added directly by the author" | yes, public and timestamped | MIT (citation if published; poster PDF), Yale (paper/abstract/poster), Caltech (poster in Portfolio; preprint counts as "submitted" only if a journal submission exists), Harvard upload, Additional Information (DOI line) | [事實] | L21, L22, L23, L09, L11, L06, L14 |
| 6 | Peer-reviewed paper with the student as author | the journal, for the manuscript; not for the student's share (ICMJE: editors do not decide authorship) | yes | same as rung 5; Caltech "published" | [事實] | C1-S47, L06 |

- [事實] ISEF limits a project to "no more than 12 months of continuous research"
  and bars research "performed before January 2026" for the 2027 fair; a project
  data book and research paper "are not required, but are strongly recommended for
  judging purposes"; a mentor or institution may be referenced on the board only
  in an acknowledgement and "within official ISEF paperwork, most notably Form
  2C"; published papers "may only be present within a lab notebook" (L17).
- [事實] ISEF Form 2C asks the supervising adult to tick whether the student "Used
  equipment and/or received data", had "Minimal interaction with our group", was
  "Mentored by me or someone else from our group", "Worked as a sub-set of our
  ongoing research" or "Had an independent project from our group" (L18).
  [推論] These five boxes are the clearest published template for what a
  programme's attestation of a three-year record should state, year by year.
- [事實] Scoir's document portal lets a "school official or supporter" upload a
  recommendation or an "Other" document as PDF without a Scoir account; the
  article does not define "supporter" or mention mentors (L25). Whether a
  Coalition-on-Scoir college receives such a document as part of the file: not
  stated (checked 2026-11-08, L25).
- [事實] ORCID iDs are "free of charge to researchers"; a record item shows whether
  it was validated by a member organisation or self-asserted (L22, L23).
  [推論] A student's self-asserted ORCID entry proves nothing an office cannot
  already read on the application; a DOI'd preprint linked to it proves a dated
  public artefact exists, not the student's share.

## D. Honest limits (not stated anywhere read)

- Whether a school counselor will countersign, attach or forward an outside
  programme's record: not stated (checked 2026-11-08, L03, L05, A1-S14).
- Whether any of the 30 accepts a techniques log or hours record as a document:
  not stated; only Caltech lists "Personal notebooks" as a portfolio item (L08).
- Whether a lab mentor qualifies as an Other Recommender at a given college:
  Common App delegates the accepted types to each college (L03); per-college
  settings were not read.
- What MIT asks in the Research Supplement "brief questionnaire": the questions
  are not on the public page (L09).
- Yale's STEM form questions and whether the mentor letter has a length limit:
  not on the public page (L11).
- Any official statement that a paid programme, a ghost-written paper or an
  honorary authorship is discounted: none found on the 30 schools' pages; only
  STS's disclosure rule is in writing (L20).
- Common App Fraud Policy text: page is client-rendered and was not read today;
  the A1 note read it in a browser on 2026-11-07 (A1-S33).
