# Why this note exists

Run 5 closed BACKLOG E8 and E9 and named four things it had left genuinely
empty. Two of them were questions about what an institution publishes, not
about this repo's tables:

- **Purdue's letters-of-recommendation policy.** Absent from the four pages read
  (apply, first-year-criteria, international, and the review-factor list), so
  the cell read "not stated".
- **Northwestern's ISP requirements**, which run 5 said live on the ISP site
  rather than the admissions site.

Both were looked for again this morning. One closes. The other does not, and the
reason it does not is itself worth recording.

# 1. Purdue: the policy exists, on a page for counselors `[事實]`

`V5-U1`, "Need-to-Know Updates for Counselors" on `admissions.purdue.edu` —
Purdue Undergraduate Admissions in its own voice — states, under "Regular
Decision Reminders":

> "Letters of recommendation aren't required, but if a student submits one, we'll
> include it in their review."

So the policy is **optional, and read if sent**. This was verified twice: once by
the research pass and once directly against the live page before being written
here.

Three things to keep straight, because they look like contradictions and are not:

- Purdue's published review-factor list (`V4-T9`) names no letters. That remains
  true. Not being a listed review factor and being read if submitted are
  compatible statements, and the site should say both rather than pick one.
- The statement is on a **counselor-facing seasonal updates page**, not on an
  applicant-facing requirements page. An applicant following the ordinary
  apply → criteria → international path never meets it. That is a finding about
  where the information is, and belongs on the insights page.
- The page carries **no visible publication date**, and its "Jan. 15" and
  "March 31" references do not name a cycle. It may be rewritten seasonally. The
  claim is dated by access date only, which is the weakest kind of dating this
  repo accepts, and the cell says so.

What is still not stated: whether Purdue caps the number of letters, who may
write one, and how one is submitted. `[事實]` only that it is not required and is
included if submitted.

# 2. Northwestern ISP: the structure is published, the materials are not `[事實]`

What Northwestern does state:

- A second application is required. `V5-U2`: "Students who wish to apply to the
  Integrated Science Program (ISP) must also complete an ISP application." And:
  "The application processes to Northwestern and to ISP are separate. Admission
  to Northwestern is not a guarantee of admission to the ISP and the status of
  your ISP application will not affect your Northwestern general acceptance
  decision."
- The order is fixed. `V5-U3`: "students must first be admitted into Northwestern
  before being fully considered for admission into ISP."
- **Either round.** `V5-U4`: "You may apply to both ISP and Northwestern under
  either early or regular decision." ISP does not force ED.
- The ISP deadline falls **after** Northwestern's in both rounds (`V5-U4`), which
  is the reverse of the usual supplement sequencing and matters for planning.
- Late applications are considered: `V5-U4` says applying late "will put you at a
  disadvantage due to the limited number of spaces in ISP."
- Preparation expectations, `V5-U2`: "Your high school preparation should include
  four years of mathematics, including one year of calculus, and four years of
  science, including chemistry and physics." And: "You should complete the study
  of one-variable differential and integral calculus before enrolling in ISP" —
  with, if it will not be finished in high school, "we will ask you to outline
  how you plan to fulfill this requirement."
- `V5-U5`: AP and IB scores must be reported to Northwestern, or ISP "may delay
  making a decision" until they arrive.

## What is not stated, and why that is the honest answer

The materials list does not exist on any current page. The chain dead-ends in a
loop: `V5-U2`, under its own heading "Supplement materials:" for ISP, says only
"For an updated list of required materials, please see the ISP website" — and the
ISP page it links to (`V5-U3`) enumerates nothing. No essay prompts, no word
limits, no form, no portal link, and no statement about letters.

So **the ISP application's contents are not stated**, and neither are its
2026–27 deadlines: the deadline table on `V5-U4` is last cycle's, every date on
it was in the past on the access date, and `V5-U3` independently describes the
deadline as closed.

## A document that exists and is not cited `[事實]`

A fillable ISP application form does sit on the official ISP domain
(`isp.northwestern.edu/documents/isp_ap.pdf`) and is the only document anywhere
with actual prompts. **Nothing in this repo cites it, deliberately**, on three
independent grounds:

1. Its embedded metadata gives a creation date in **2015**.
2. It requires SAT Subject Tests in Math Level II, Physics and Chemistry —
   discontinued in 2021 — and asks for "SAT Reasoning" and "SAT Writing" scores
   and a fax number.
3. It is an orphan: it is not linked from any current ISP or admissions page, and
   its stated "March 1" deadline contradicts the deadline table on `V5-U4`.

Citing it would put an eleven-year-old form's requirements on the site as this
cycle's. It is recorded here as a lead so a later run does not rediscover it and
mistake it for current. This is the same class of error as reading a stale
listing for a changed policy, which the Williams Jan 4 / Jan 5 row already
documents.

# Open question this creates

An applicant who wants ISP cannot find out from Northwestern's published pages
what the ISP application asks. The only route is the email address on `V5-U3`.
That is a real gap in the public record rather than a gap in this repo's
reading, and it is now Q007.

## 中文摘要

- **Purdue 的推薦信政策找到了，而且是官方原文**（V5-U1，Purdue 招生辦自己的頁面）：「Letters of recommendation aren't required, but if a student submits one, we'll include it in their review.」——不要求，但交了就會納入審查。這句話已另外直接對線上頁面核對過一次。
- 三件要分清楚的事：(1) Purdue 公布的審查因素清單裡確實沒有推薦信，這仍然成立——「不是列出的審查因素」和「交了會看」兩句話並不衝突，站上兩句都寫；(2) 這句話出現在**給輔導老師看的季節性更新頁**，不在任何申請者會走到的要求頁上，所以照 apply → criteria → international 路線走的申請者永遠看不到它，這一點本身就是一條洞察；(3) 該頁**沒有標示發布日期**，裡面的 1/15 與 3/31 也沒寫是哪一季，可能會被季節性改寫，所以這條只能用存取日定年份，是本站接受的最弱一種定年，格子裡已註明。
- 仍未載明：Purdue 是否限制推薦信封數、可以由誰寫、怎麼交。
- **Northwestern ISP：結構有公布，要交什麼沒有公布。** 有寫的是：ISP 要另外交一份申請（V5-U2），兩套流程分開、錄取 Northwestern 不等於錄取 ISP、ISP 結果也不影響 Northwestern 的錄取；順序固定，要先被 Northwestern 錄取才會被完整審查（V5-U3）；**ED 或 RD 都可以**，ISP 不強迫走 ED（V5-U4）；ISP 的截止日在兩個輪次都**晚於** Northwestern 的截止日，和一般補充材料的順序相反；遲交會審但吃虧；修課期望是四年數學含一年微積分、四年科學含化學與物理，入學前要修完單變數微積分，沒修完的話「we will ask you to outline how you plan to fulfill this requirement」（V5-U2）；AP／IB 成績要送到，否則 ISP 可能延後決定（V5-U5）。
- **沒有公布的是材料清單**，而且是繞回圈的：V5-U2 在自己「Supplement materials:」標題下只寫「請看 ISP 網站」，而它連到的 ISP 頁（V5-U3）一項都沒列——沒有題目、沒有字數、沒有表單、沒有連結，也沒寫推薦信。2026–27 的 ISP 截止日同樣未載明：V5-U4 的表格是上一季的，表上每個日期在存取日都已過去，V5-U3 自己也說截止日已關閉。
- **有一份表單存在，而本站刻意不引用**：官方 ISP 網域上有一份可填的 ISP 申請表 PDF，是唯一有實際題目的文件，但它的內嵌中繼資料顯示建立於 **2015 年**，要求 2021 年已停辦的 SAT Subject Tests（Math Level II、Physics、Chemistry），還問 SAT Reasoning／Writing 分數與傳真號碼，而且沒有任何現行頁面連到它，它寫的「March 1」也和 V5-U4 的表格矛盾。引用它等於把一份十一年前的表單當成本季要求放上站。這裡記下來，是為了讓之後的run不要重新「發現」它並誤認為現行。
- 這產生一個新的開放問題（Q007）：想申請 ISP 的人，從 Northwestern 公布的頁面上查不到 ISP 申請要問什麼，唯一路徑是 V5-U3 上的 email。這是公開資訊本身的缺口，不是本站讀取的缺口。
